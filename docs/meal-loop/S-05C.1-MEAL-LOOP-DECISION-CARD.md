# S-05C.1 — Meal-loop recovery decision card

**Status.** Decision only. Not implemented. No migration. No new meal-lifecycle code in this unit.

**Date.** 2026-10-17.

## Question

Should a household be able to say it cooked a planned meal without tapping every step of guided cook mode?

**Recommendation.** Yes. The action is explicit, the household asks for it, and it does not invent guided-step completions or a rating. Oversight already leans this way. This card proposes the smallest version. It does not ship it, and it does not ship a discoverability-only relabel of the current path. The current path cannot record “cooked” from a meal that is still `planned` or `selected`.

## What happened

A household cooked Cashew Pesto Pasta. Home still said the meal “is cooking” and offered “Back to the kitchen.” The rating stayed pending because guided cook’s last Finish control was never tapped. Finish is the only write that moves `cooking` to `cooked` (`finishCook` → `mutateDinnerPlan` op `finish_cook`), and the rate screen opens after that write.

## States today

Stored on the plan meal. No extra table.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `planned` | On the plan. Not chosen as tonight’s pick and not cooked. |
| `selected` | Tonight’s pick. Not cooked. |
| `cooking` | `begin_cook` ran. Guided mode is the way back in. |
| `cooked` | `finish_cook` ran from `cooking`. `cooked_recipe_version_id` is set. No rating yet. |
| `partially_rated` | At least one participant rated. Not everyone. |
| `fully_rated` | Every participant rated. Closed. |

Other closes: `exit_cook` moves `cooking` back to `planned` and does not cook. `skip_meal` / `abandon_meal` (`markClosed`) can leave `planned`, `selected`, or `cooking`. `fulfill_meal` rejects recipes. `rate_meal` accepts only `cooked`, `partially_rated`, or `fully_rated`, writes that member’s score, and never fills in anyone else’s.

## Transitions that already exist

- Plan → Shop does not change meal state.
- Home, while any meal is `cooking`: “{title} is cooking”, “Pick up where you left off.”, “Back to the kitchen”, “Shopping list” (`renderHomePlanHero`).
- Tonight, `cooking`: “In the kitchen” and “Back to the kitchen”.
- Tonight, `cooked` or `partially_rated`: “Rate when you’re ready”.
- History shows the plan after the fact. It does not offer a cook or a rate that the meal state rejects.
- A meal left `cooking` is still `cooking` the next day. Nothing expires it.

There is no “mark cooked” from `planned` or `selected`. “Rate when you’re ready” does not appear until `finish_cook` has already succeeded. Sending someone back to the kitchen only helps if they then find Finish. That is the step this household missed. A label change on “Back to the kitchen” would not record the meal.

## Proposed behavior

One explicit control, in the household’s words:

- **We made this** on the home “is cooking” card, on the Tonight cooking card, and on a History row that is still `cooking`, `planned`, or `selected` for a meal the household says it already cooked.
- After it succeeds, the existing rate screen: **How was it?**
- No control on a meal that is already `fully_rated`.

Exact rules:

1. The household taps the control. Nothing marks a meal cooked because a day ended, a list was checked off, or a cook screen was opened.
2. Guided steps that were not tapped stay untapped. The app does not write fake step completions.
3. No score is written. Each diner rates themselves later, or not.
4. `planned`, `cooked`, and `rated` stay distinct. “We made this” lands on `cooked`. Ratings still move `cooked` → `partially_rated` → `fully_rated`.
5. A second tap is idempotent. It does not insert another completion or another rating.
6. Taste evidence stays the rating rows. A cooked meal with no ratings is not evidence of a score.
7. A household that never opened guided mode can still record the meal. That is the case this control exists for.
8. A stale `cooking` session, including one left overnight, stays `cooking` until someone taps “We made this”, “Back to the kitchen” and then Finish, or leaves the meal. It is not auto-closed and not auto-rated.

## Server

`finish_cook` already does the right write, and it refuses anything that is not `cooking`. Reuse it when the meal is already `cooking`.

`planned` and `selected` need a new op, proposed name `mark_cooked`. Same write as `finish_cook`: state `cooked`, `cooked_recipe_version_id` set from the meal’s recipe version, no rating rows. Calling it on a meal that is already `cooked` or rated returns success and does not add a rating or a second completion event. Meals live in the plan JSON, so this does not need a migration.

Do not widen `finish_cook` in silence. A new op keeps the guided Finish path honest and makes the explicit “we made this” path reviewable.

Client copy, not new states: “We made this”, then “How was it?”

## If it is built later

Tests, not part of this unit:

- `planned` and `selected` become `cooked` only through `mark_cooked`.
- `cooking` becomes `cooked` through `finish_cook` or `mark_cooked`, once.
- A second call does not duplicate ratings or completion events.
- `rate_meal` still rejects `planned` and `cooking`.
- No rating row appears until a person submits a score.
- Home and Tonight show the control on `cooking`, and “Rate when you’re ready” after the write.
- A meal left `cooking` overnight is unchanged until an explicit action.

## Risks

- A mis-tap marks a meal cooked. The control should be a deliberate button, not the card itself, and Tonight should keep “Back to the kitchen” beside it.
- Two ops that both reach `cooked` can drift. They should share one helper that sets state and `cooked_recipe_version_id` and nothing else.
- History can list several old `cooking` meals. The control belongs on the meal, not as a global “mark everything cooked.”
- Idempotency has to cover a retry of the same tap after a slow response, or the household will see two completion events.

## Not in this unit

No button, no op, no copy change on the home card, and no discoverability tweak. Item 5 stops at this card.
