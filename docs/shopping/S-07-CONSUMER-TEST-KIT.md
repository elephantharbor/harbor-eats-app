## 2. Scoring (one sheet per path)

**Per line, mark one:** **F** full = right product family and right quantity or package. **P** partial = right name but wrong quantity or package size. **A correct name with the wrong package quantity is P, a partial failure.** **W** wrong product. **U** unmatched or not found.
**Package rule:** a line is F if the cart covers the need with the smallest reasonable package. Examples: ½ cup cilantro → 1 bunch; ⅛ tsp salt → 1 container; ⅔ cup oats → 1 canister. It is P if the line under-buys, defaults to qty 1 where 2+ are needed (beans, limes), turns a fraction into a count ("2/3 cup" → 2 or 3), or buys more than 2× when a smaller package was offered.
**Allergen-sensitive substitution (flag ⚠ on any line):** a substitute that adds milk, egg, tree nut, peanut, soy, wheat or gluten, fish, shellfish or sesame compared with the original. Watch especially: oat milk → dairy, almond or soy milk; Rao's marinara → a cheese or vodka sauce.
