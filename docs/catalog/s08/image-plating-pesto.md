# Image / plating brief — Cashew Pesto Pasta

Regenerate the hero. v3 is a dairy-free cashew pesto with olive oil, so the photograph needs an oil sheen, a greener pesto, and toasted cashews. A pale oil-free photo will not match. This brief is the recipe plus the house style.

House-quality reference for light and finish, not a layout to copy: `factory/tools/image-standards/house-quality-reference-chipotle-lime-black-bean-bowl.png`.

Master 1200×900 WebP and card 640×480 WebP. Export an already-generated source with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py`. That script does not generate pixels.

## Hero subject

A serving of short pasta (penne or fusilli, from 12 oz) coated in cashew-basil pesto, with toasted cashews and halved cherry tomatoes. One bowl is the hero. The recipe serves four.

## Vessel

A wide, shallow bowl. Not a skillet. Not a tall soup bowl.

## Camera angle

Higher, near-overhead, so the pasta shape, the green sauce, the cashew pieces, and the tomatoes all read. Not a side-on stack.

## Required visible components

- Short durum pasta, cooked, coated rather than sitting in a puddle.
- Pesto from 3 cups basil, toasted raw cashews (1/2 cup, with 2 tbsp chopped and reserved), 2 garlic cloves, lemon zest and juice, 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil, and 1/4 cup nutritional yeast. It should look green, glossy from the oil, and slightly coarse, not a white cream sauce and not an oil-free paste.
- Reserved toasted cashews on top, visibly chopped and browned, not pale raw nuts buried in the sauce.
- Cherry tomatoes, from 1 cup, halved, scattered on top. They are not cooked into a tomato sauce.

## Texture cues

Pasta ridges or spirals still visible under the pesto. Oil sheen on the sauce. Cashews look toasted. Basil reads green, not brown and not neon. No cheese strands.

## Sauce and component placement

The pesto coats the noodles. Cashews and tomatoes sit on top. No second sauce. No cream poured at the table. Lemon is in the pesto, not a pile of wedges, unless one thin wedge is clearly the recipe lemon and stays at the rim.

## Legitimate garnish

The reserved toasted cashews are the listed finish. Nutritional yeast is in the sauce and should not look like a mound of grated cheese. No basil sprig that implies an herb the cook did not use; basil is already the sauce.

## Environmental styling

Optional, off the plate, outside the center crop: a few extra toasted cashews or a basil leaf corresponding to the pesto. No cheese wedge. No milk bottle. Those would contradict the dairy-free recipe.

## Color opportunities from real ingredients

Green pesto, gold toasted cashews, red cherry tomatoes, pale pasta. The oil makes the green look wet. Do not add chili flakes, peas, or spinach for color.

## Forbidden elements

Parmesan, pecorino, mozzarella, cream, milk, butter, pine nuts in place of cashews, a raw cashew color on the garnish, chicken, shrimp, a red tomato sauce, and a cheese pull.

## Crop strategy

Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are one 4:3 frame. Fill the frame with pasta. Keep toasted cashews and at least two tomato halves inside the center so the square recommendation-card crop still shows nuts, green sauce, and tomato.
