# Salmon and cashew pesto, before and after

Both revisions are new version files. `v1.json` and `v2.json` were not edited. Neither recipe is kitchen-tested. Evidence on both: structurally validated and culinary-reviewed `s08-2026-10-18`, `kitchen_tested: false`, `household_rated: false`.

Salt weights use UC ANR publication 390575 (November 2023): Diamond Crystal kosher salt 2.8 g per teaspoon, Morton coarse kosher 4.8 g per teaspoon, table salt 6 g per teaspoon.

## Maple Mustard Glazed Salmon

Published version moves from `rv_maple-mustard-glazed-salmon_v2` to `rv_maple-mustard-glazed-salmon_v3`. Yield stays 4 servings. Expected flavor: savory maple-mustard salmon, salt enough that the sweetness is not flat, lemon and dill at the end, asparagus that roasts instead of sitting in glaze.

### Ingredients

| Item | v2 | v3 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salmon fillets | 4 count | 4 count, about 6 oz each | Same dish. The note gives a shopping weight. |
| Asparagus | 1 bunch | 1 lb, about one bunch | A unit the shop can scale. |
| Dijon mustard | 2 tbsp | 3 tbsp | Enough glaze for four fillets plus a reserved spoonful. Maple stays 2 tbsp so the glaze does not turn to candy. |
| Maple syrup | 2 tbsp | 2 tbsp | Unchanged. The mustard now leads. |
| Olive oil | absent | 2 tbsp, split | One spoon on the asparagus, one in the glaze. v2 forbade oil because v1 had not listed it. |
| Garlic | absent | 1 clove, grated | One aromatic in the glaze, not a pile of alliums. |
| Lemon | absent | 1 | Zest in the glaze, 1 tbsp juice in the reserved glaze, the rest as wedges. |
| Fresh dill | absent | 2 tbsp chopped | One herb. Chives are the stated stand-in if dill is out. |
| Salt | absent | 8 g Diamond Crystal | 5 g on the salmon, 2 g on the asparagus, 1 g in the glaze. About 3 tsp Diamond Crystal, 1.75 tsp Morton, or 1.25 tsp table, for the same 8 g. |
| Black pepper | absent | 1/2 tsp | Split between fish and asparagus. |

### Steps

v2 had two steps: mix Dijon and maple, brush both the fish and the asparagus, roast at 400 F for 12 to 15 minutes until opaque. The step said no oil is added.

v3 heats to 425 F, salts the fish before the glaze, seasons the asparagus with oil and salt and does not glaze it (glaze makes the stalks sticky), builds the glaze with mustard, maple, garlic, zest, oil, and a gram of salt, roasts until the thickest part is 145 F, broils 1 to 3 minutes, rests 3 minutes, then finishes with lemon juice in the reserved glaze, dill, and wedges.

### Dietary, safety, shopping

Allergens stay finfish. No new allergen. Dairy-free. `hh001_eligible` stays false on the package. Runtime Household 001 eligibility still allows this finfish meal. That split is documented in the data-integrity note. It was not changed.

Safety: the instruction is 145 F internal, FDA seafood guidance. No 125 F pull. A thin fillet is not pulled early for carryover.

Shopping: oil, garlic, lemon, dill, weighed salt, and pepper are new lines. Quantities are numbers with units. Gram salt scales with servings (`scaleForDiners`). The 8 g line at 2 servings is 4 g.

Uncertainties: broil time depends on the oven. Dill versus chives is one herb, not a stack.

## Cashew Pesto Pasta

Published version moves from `rv_cashew-pesto-pasta_v2` to `rv_cashew-pesto-pasta_v3`. Yield stays 4 servings. Expected flavor: basil-forward pesto that coats pasta, salt in the noodle and the sauce, olive oil carrying the herbs, lemon at the end. Cashews are toasted.

### Ingredients

| Item | v2 | v3 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short pasta | 12 oz | 12 oz | Unchanged. |
| Raw cashews | 1/2 cup, soaked | 1/2 cup, toasted, 2 tbsp reserved | Toasting replaces the soak. The dish still contains cashews and is not nut-safe. |
| Basil | 2 cups | 3 cups packed | The herb should lead. It goes in at the end of blending so it stays green. |
| Garlic | 2 cloves | 2 cloves | Unchanged. |
| Lemon | 2 tbsp juice | 1 lemon, zest plus 3 tbsp juice | Zest in the blend, juice to brighten after tasting the pesto. |
| Olive oil | absent ("No oil and no cheese") | 1/4 cup extra-virgin | 3 tbsp in the pesto, 1 tsp on the tomatoes, the rest on the finished bowl. Oil is what makes pesto a sauce. |
| Nutritional yeast | 3 tbsp | 1/4 cup | The savory stand-in for cheese. Not dairy. No miso, so no new soy allergen. |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp unnamed kosher | 31 g Diamond Crystal | 28 g in 3 qt pasta water (about 1% by weight), 2.5 g in the pesto, 0.5 g on the tomatoes. Same 31 g is about 11 tsp Diamond Crystal, 6.5 tsp Morton, or 5.25 tsp table. Most of the pasta-water salt stays in the pot. |
| Black pepper | absent | 1/4 tsp | In the pesto only. |
| Cherry tomatoes | 1 cup | 1 cup | Salted and oiled, not left raw and flat. |
| Water | 1/4 cup in the blender | 3 qt for the pot | The thin blend water is gone. A spoon or two of pasta water is the emulsion, and only if the blender needs it. |

### Steps

v2 soaked the cashews, blended them with basil, garlic, lemon juice, nutritional yeast, the half teaspoon of salt, and water, and said no oil and no cheese. Pasta was boiled with no salt in the water. Tomatoes were tossed on at the end.

v3 toasts the cashews, blends a thick pesto, tastes that pesto (it is meant to be eaten without further cooking), boils the pasta in salted water, seasons the tomatoes, then tosses off the heat with reserved pasta water until the sauce looks glossy.

### Dietary, safety, shopping

Still plant-based and dairy-free. Allergens remain cashew, nuts, and wheat. The ingredient note says the dish is not nut-safe. No cheese, no milk, no miso.

No raw animal food. The taste step is the pesto and, later, a cooked noodle.

Shopping: olive oil, a whole lemon, more basil, and a weighed salt line replace the oil-free blend and the unsalted boil. The 3 qt water is the 4-serving pot. Salt scales by servings. The pot volume is the amount for that yield, not something to multiply blindly for a one-person portion without a smaller pot.

Uncertainties: toast color and how hard the basil is bruised. The 1% figure is a ratio for discarded water.

The v2 time note said the cashews soak 20 minutes and prep is 25. v3 replaces that note: toast is 4 to 5 minutes, prep 15, cook 15.
