# Image / plating brief — Moroccan Chickpea Skillet

Regenerate the hero only if the current photograph is a bare skillet. v3 finishes with 2 tbsp chopped parsley on the chickpeas, and that parsley is the visible change. If the current hero already shows chopped parsley on the skillet and couscous, do not regenerate.

House-quality reference for light and finish, not a layout to copy: `factory/tools/image-standards/house-quality-reference-chipotle-lime-black-bean-bowl.png`.

Master 1200×900 WebP and card 640×480 WebP. Export an already-generated source with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py`. That script does not generate pixels.

## Hero subject

Chickpeas in a skillet sauce with spinach, garlic, ras el hanout, and preserved lemon, served over couscous, with chopped parsley on top. One plated serving is the hero. The recipe serves four.

## Vessel

A shallow bowl or a low plate that can hold couscous with the skillet spooned over it. Not a soup bowl. The skillet itself may appear only as the source, not as an empty pan.

## Camera angle

Higher, near-overhead, so the chickpeas, the green spinach, the parsley, and the couscous at the edge all read.

## Required visible components

- Chickpeas, from two drained cans, in a light tomato-less skillet sauce. Ras el hanout and garlic are in the sauce, not a dry spice pile.
- Preserved lemon, chopped rind, visible as small yellow pieces, not fresh lemon wheels.
- Spinach wilted into the chickpeas, still green.
- Couscous, from 1 1/2 cups dry, as the base, grains separate enough to read.
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley scattered on top. This is the reason to regenerate a bare-skillet hero.

## Texture cues

Whole chickpeas, not a puree. Wilted spinach, not raw salad leaves. Couscous fluffy, not a porridge. Parsley cut, bright, and on the surface.

## Sauce and component placement

The skillet mixture sits on the couscous, not beside an empty mound with the sauce missing. Parsley is on the chickpeas. No yogurt sauce. No second stew.

## Legitimate garnish

Chopped parsley only. Preserved lemon is an ingredient in the skillet, not a fresh lemon wedge garnish.

## Environmental styling

Optional, off the plate, outside the center crop: a small parsley sprig corresponding to the finish. No fresh lemon half. No yogurt bowl. No bread unless it is clearly out of the crop and not a plated side the recipe does not serve.

## Color opportunities from real ingredients

Tan chickpeas, green spinach, yellow preserved lemon, green parsley, pale couscous, warm spice color in the oil. Do not add pomegranate, olives, or feta for color.

## Forbidden elements

Feta, yogurt, fresh lemon wedges as the garnish, chicken, lamb, a bare skillet with no parsley, rice in place of couscous, and a smooth hummus-style puree.

## Crop strategy

Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are one 4:3 frame. Keep parsley and chickpeas in the center. Couscous may show at the edge but must still be recognizable after the center square crop of the recommendation card.
