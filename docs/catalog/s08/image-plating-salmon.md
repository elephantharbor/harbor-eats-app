# Image / plating brief — Maple Mustard Glazed Salmon

Regenerate the hero. The current photograph was audited with neither dill nor lemon wedges. v3 plates both. Doneness is 145 F. This brief is the recipe plus the house style. Do not generate from the title alone.

House-quality reference for light and finish, not a layout to copy: `factory/tools/image-standards/house-quality-reference-chipotle-lime-black-bean-bowl.png`.

Master 1200×900 WebP and card 640×480 WebP. Export an already-generated source with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py`. That script does not generate pixels.

## Hero subject

One salmon fillet, about 6 oz, roasted with a maple-Dijon glaze, asparagus beside it, finished with fresh dill and a lemon wedge. The recipe serves four fillets and 1 lb asparagus. Show one plated serving, not the whole sheet pan.

## Vessel

Dinner plate. Not a bowl. Not the sheet pan as the vessel.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter view, chosen so the fillet surface, the glaze, the asparagus, the dill, and the lemon wedge all read. Do not use a straight overhead bowl shot.

## Required visible components

- Salmon fillet, patted dry, roasted at 425 F until the thickest part is opaque and 145 F, then a short broil so the glaze spots amber. Rest is off-camera. The surface is glazed, not breaded, not fried.
- Asparagus, about a quarter of 1 lb, tossed with olive oil, salt, and pepper, roasted beside the fish. The glaze is not on the asparagus.
- Maple-Dijon glaze on the salmon: Dijon, maple syrup, a little olive oil, grated garlic, lemon zest. Glossy, amber in spots, not a pool of syrup.
- Fresh dill, from 2 tbsp for the recipe, scattered on the salmon after roasting.
- Lemon wedge from the one lemon. The juice also goes into the reserved glaze; the wedge is on the plate.

## Texture cues

Moist roasted salmon, glaze that looks brushed on and spotted from the broiler, asparagus with a little browning and still green. Dill leaves should look fresh, not cooked into the glaze. No grill marks. No crispy skin story the recipe does not claim.

## Sauce and component placement

The glaze coats the fillet. A small spoon of the reserved glaze, loosened with lemon juice, can sit on the fish with the dill. It is not a separate bowl of sauce. Asparagus sits beside the fillet, unglazed. The lemon wedge is at the edge of the fillet, not a pile of lemons.

## Legitimate garnish

Dill and the lemon wedge are in the recipe. No other herb. No extra citrus.

## Environmental styling

Optional and off the plate, outside the center crop: a dill sprig or the remaining lemon half, corresponding to the finish. They must not look like a second side.

## Color opportunities from real ingredients

Orange-pink salmon, amber glaze, green asparagus, green dill, yellow lemon. Do not add tomato, chili, or herbs that are not in the recipe.

## Forbidden elements

Rice, potatoes, a salad, cucumber, parsley, chives, garlic cloves arranged whole, a raw salmon center, grill marks, breading, a cheese crust, butter as a visible sauce, and glaze painted onto the asparagus.

## Crop strategy

Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are one 4:3 frame. Keep the fillet and the dill near center. Asparagus and the lemon wedge stay inside the central 75% of the width. The recommendation card square-crops the 4:3 frame from the center. Dill on the salmon and at least a few asparagus tips must survive that crop.
