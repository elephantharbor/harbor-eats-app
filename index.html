Passes household locks ~40 min Serves 2 Moderate

Ingredients

  • Chipotle powder, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic
  • Cornstarch + oil (for crisp)
  • Green cabbage, carrot, cilantro, lime

Time

Step 1

Press & season tofu
