## Steps

### 1. Start the rice and prep

Rinse the rice, put it in a medium saucepan with 2 cups water, and bring to a boil over high heat. Cover, reduce to low, and cook 15 minutes; turn off the heat and let it rest, covered, 10 minutes. It needs no attention after the first few minutes. Meanwhile, cut the fish into 1-inch chunks and pat dry; keep it cold. Slice the green beans into thin rounds. Zest the lime, then cut it into wedges. Remove the ribs from the lime leaves (if using) and slice into thin threads. Quarter and thinly slice the cucumber; slice the shallot and chile. Read the curry paste and fish sauce labels: no shrimp, shrimp paste, or other shellfish. HARD CONTRACT: no peanuts or other nuts on the relish; no shellfish anywhere.

### 2. Make the cucumber relish

In a bowl, whisk the rice vinegar, 3 tbsp sugar, the salt, and 2 tbsp warm water until the sugar and salt dissolve. Add the cucumber, shallot, and chile, and toss. Set aside; it improves as it sits. Stir in a few cilantro leaves right before serving.

### 3. Make the fish paste

In a food processor, combine the cold fish, curry paste, fish sauce, egg, and 1 tsp sugar. Pulse to break up, then process until it forms a thick, sticky, springy paste, about 1 minute, scraping down once. Scrape into a bowl and fold in the green beans, lime zest, and lime-leaf threads. With a wet hand, scoop and slap the paste against the side of the bowl for 1 minute; this gives the cakes their bouncy texture.

### 4. Shape the cakes

Lightly oil your hands. Shape the paste into 16 flat patties about 2 1/2 inches wide and 1/2 inch thick, setting them on an oiled plate or parchment.

### 5. Pan-fry

Heat 1/4 cup oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Fry 8 cakes until puffed and deep golden-brown with crisp, lacy edges, 2 to 3 minutes per side; they are done when they are opaque and springy in the center (145 F). Drain on paper towels. Repeat with the remaining oil and cakes.

### 6. Serve

Fluff the rice. Plate the fish cakes with rice, spoon the relish and some of its brine alongside, scatter with cilantro, and add lime wedges. No peanuts; no sweet chili sauce needed. kitchen_tested is false.
