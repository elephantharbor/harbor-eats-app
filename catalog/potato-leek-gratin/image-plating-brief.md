# Image / Plating Brief — Potato and Leek Gratin

Source (read-only): certified package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`. No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

Thinly sliced Yukon Gold potatoes and leeks baked in a heavy-cream and vegetarian Gruyère custard, in a 2-quart baking dish, until the center is tender and the cheese on top is browned in patches. The uncut gratin in its dish is what the recipe serves after a 5-minute rest. It is not a soup and not a breadcrumb-topped casserole.

## Vessel

2-quart baking dish, greased with butter, which is the vessel the recipe bakes and rests. Not a skillet, not individual ramekins (not in the recipe), not a sheet pan.

## Camera angle

Slightly elevated 3/4 so the browned cheese surface and the dish edges read, and so any visible side layer of potato and leek can show. A straight overhead of only the cheese crust hides the potatoes and leeks that define the dish. See ambiguity about lifting a portion.

## Required visible components

- Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, sliced about 1/8 inch, layered. Yellow flesh if a cut or edge is visible.
- Leeks: white and pale green only, halved, rinsed, sliced 1/4 inch, cooked soft in butter, not dark brown. No dark green leek tops.
- Cream-cheese custard: heavy cream whisked with half the grated vegetarian Gruyère (microbial rennet), minced garlic, kosher salt, black pepper, ground nutmeg, and fresh thyme leaves. No egg and no flour. At the end of baking the cream is bubbling at the edges and thickened, not watery. Do not show added milk, stock, or water.
- Remaining 2 oz Gruyère scattered on top, browned in patches. No breadcrumb crust.

## Texture cues

Tender potato layers, creamy set custard, patched browning on the cheese, soft pale leeks between slices. Surface looks baked, not deep-fried and not au-gratin-with-crumbs. Nutmeg and thyme are in the custard; thyme is leaves, not sprigs stuck on top.

## Sauce/component placement

Custard is poured over the layers as they are built and pressed so the cream comes nearly to the top. It should be visible at the edges as bubbling, thickened cream, not as a broth. Cheese is the top layer only. Do not add a parsley finish, a breadcrumb lid, or a cream drizzle after baking.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

None beyond the cheese and thyme leaves already in the dish. Thyme leaves go into the custard, not on as a sprig garnish. The recipe lists no finishing herb, salt flake, or breadcrumb.

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional and small, outside the dish and outside the center crop: a thyme sprig (the herb in the custard) or a pale leek slice. Do not set out a wedge of cheese or a cream pitcher in a way that looks like extra servings. No potato skins; the potatoes are peeled.

## Natural color opportunities

Gold browned Gruyère, yellow Yukon potato, pale green and white leek, cream at the edge. The meal is naturally quiet in color. Do not add chives, paprika, tomato, or greens that are not the pale part of the leek and the thyme already in the custard.

## Forbidden elements (ingredients/sides/techniques NOT in the recipe)

Breadcrumbs, panko, flour, egg, milk as a stand-in that thins the custard, stock, water added to the dish, dark green leek tops, cheddar or a pre-shredded blend in place of Gruyère if it changes the look to orange cheese, ham, bacon, nutmeg dusted on the finished top as a visible brown cloud, a side salad, or a breadcrumb crust. The recipe says there is no breadcrumb crust and no egg or flour in the custard.

## Crop strategy (1200x900 master, 640x480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Master 1200×900 and 640×480 card are one 4:3 frame. Fill the frame with the baking dish. Keep browned cheese and a visible potato-leek edge inside the central 75% of the width. The mobile recommendation card is a square center cover-crop (about 12.5% off each side); tablet cards stay 4:3. At card scale the image must still read as a browned potato gratin, not as a plain cheese pull. Do not leave the only layered edge on the outer rim where the square crop will cut it off.

## Ambiguity (not resolved; recipe not edited)

The recipe does not say to cut or scoop a serving. The finished dish is the whole gratin after a 5-minute rest. A lifted portion would show the 1/8-inch potato and leek layers, which are the dish, but lifting is not instructed and would change the image. Leaving the surface intact hides those layers under the cheese. This brief does not add a scoop the recipe does not describe. Peel is explicit (potatoes are peeled); that part is not ambiguous.
