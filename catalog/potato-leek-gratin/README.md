## Steps

### 1. Slice and grate

Peel and slice the potatoes 1/8 inch thick and hold them in cold water. Wash and slice the leeks. Grate the cheese. Mince the garlic. Strip the thyme. This prep finishes before the oven is turned on.

### 2. Heat the oven and soften the leeks

Heat the oven to 400 F, about 10 minutes, inside the cook time. During those same 10 minutes, melt 1 tbsp butter and cook the leeks with 1/4 tsp salt until soft, about 8 minutes. The sauté is not added on top of the preheat.

### 3. Mix the cream-cheese custard

Whisk the heavy cream with 2 oz of the vegetarian Gruyère, the garlic, 1 tsp kosher salt, the black pepper, the nutmeg, and the thyme. No egg. No flour. The other 2 oz of cheese waits for the top.

### 4. Layer the dish

Butter a 2-quart baking dish with the remaining 1 tbsp butter. Drain and dry the potatoes. Layer potatoes and leeks, pouring the custard over them. Press the slices down. Scatter the remaining 2 oz cheese. Do not add breadcrumbs, milk, or stock.

### 5. Bake and rest

Bake at 400 F for 40 minutes, then rest 5 minutes. Both are inside the cook time, after the 10-minute preheat. Tent with foil only if the top colors early, and stay inside the 40 minutes when you do. If the center is still firm at 40 minutes, keep baking and check every 5 minutes; that extra is not in the 70-minute total because this recipe is not kitchen-tested.
