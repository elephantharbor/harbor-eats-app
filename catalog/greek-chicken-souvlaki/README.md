## Steps

### 1. Marinate the chicken

In a bowl, whisk 3 tbsp olive oil, 3 tbsp of the lemon juice, the minced garlic, dried oregano, 1.25 tsp kosher salt, and black pepper. Add the chicken pieces and toss to coat. Marinate 20 minutes at room temperature (or up to 2 hours refrigerated). Meanwhile, soak 8 bamboo skewers in water if using wood.

### 2. Make the cucumber salad

In a serving bowl, combine the sliced cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, sliced red onion, chopped dill, the remaining 1 tbsp lemon juice, the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, and the remaining 1/4 tsp kosher salt. Toss. No yogurt, no feta, no olives required. Hold while the chicken cooks.

### 3. Thread the skewers

Thread the marinated chicken onto 8 skewers, packing pieces snugly but not crushed. Discard leftover marinade that touched raw chicken; do not brush it on after cooking.

### 4. Grill or broil the souvlaki

Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high, or set an oven broiler to high with a rack 6 inches from the element (broiler path: preheat 5 minutes overlaps skewer threading). Cook the skewers, turning every 2 to 3 minutes, until chicken is browned in spots and the thickest piece reads 165 F, about 10 to 12 minutes total. Do not serve pink chicken.

### 5. Plate

Rest skewers 3 minutes. Serve 2 skewers per person with cucumber salad and lemon wedges. No pita, no tzatziki, no fries. kitchen_tested is false.
