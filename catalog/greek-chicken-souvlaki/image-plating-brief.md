# Image / Plating Brief — Greek Chicken Souvlaki with Cucumber Salad

## Hero subject
Two chicken souvlaki skewers with lemon-oregano browning, plated with cucumber-tomato-dill salad and a lemon wedge.

## Vessel
Modern dinner plate, off-white. Skewers laid across; salad mound beside. Not wrapped in pita.

## Camera angle
Slightly elevated 3/4 to show skewer browning and salad colors.

## Required visible components
- Boneless chicken thigh chunks on skewers, browned spots, cooked through look
- Cucumber slices, halved cherry tomatoes, red onion, dill
- Lemon wedge
- No yogurt sauce, no feta, no pita, no fries

## Texture cues
Chicken moist with grill/broil browning. Salad crisp and juicy.

## Forbidden elements
Tzatziki, feta, olives (not listed), pita, french fries, gyro stack, hands/text/logos.

## Crop strategy
4:3; skewers + salad in center for card crop.
