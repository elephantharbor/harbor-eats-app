## Steps

### 1. Mix the meatballs

In a large bowl, combine the ground lamb, beaten egg, panko, grated half onion, 3 cloves minced garlic, half the parsley (1/4 cup), all the mint, cumin, coriander, paprika, red pepper flakes, 1.25 tsp kosher salt, and black pepper. Mix with a fork or clean hands just until combined. Do not overwork. Shape into 16 meatballs, about 1.5 inches each.

### 2. Brown the meatballs

Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Add the meatballs in a single layer, working in two batches if needed. Brown on two sides until a crust forms, about 2 to 3 minutes per side. They will not be cooked through. Transfer to a plate.

### 3. Build the tomato-herb sauce

Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and the diced onion half. Cook until soft, about 4 minutes. Add the remaining 2 cloves minced garlic and the tomato paste. Stir 1 minute. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and water. Add the remaining 0.5 tsp kosher salt. Simmer 3 minutes, scraping browned bits.

### 4. Simmer meatballs in sauce

Nestle the browned meatballs into the sauce. Simmer uncovered over medium-low, turning once, until the centers read 160 F and the sauce has thickened slightly, about 12 to 14 minutes.

### 5. Finish with herbs

Take the skillet off the heat. Scatter the remaining 1/4 cup parsley over the meatballs and sauce. Serve from the skillet: 4 meatballs with sauce per person. No yogurt, no cheese, no pasta required. kitchen_tested is false.
