# Image / Plating Brief — Spiced Lamb Meatballs with Tomato-Herb Sauce

## Hero subject
Lamb meatballs nestled in red tomato-herb sauce in a skillet or shallow bowl, parsley finish.

## Vessel
Prefer the 12-inch skillet (recipe serves from skillet) or a shallow warm-neutral bowl with meatballs and sauce spooned in. Not spaghetti tangle unless pasta is added (it is not).

## Camera angle
Higher 3/4 so meatball roundness and sauce gloss read.

## Required visible components
- Browned lamb meatballs (about golf-ball size), not pink-raw centers visible as undercooked mush
- Tomato sauce with onion softness, not watery
- Chopped parsley on top; mint is mixed in, not required as visible leaf pile

## Texture cues
Meatballs tender with browned crust. Sauce spoonable. Herbs fresh.

## Forbidden elements
Yogurt drizzle, feta, spaghetti, pita, pine nuts (nuts banned for fidelity unless listed — not listed), hands/text/logos.

## Crop strategy
4:3; several meatballs + sauce in center for card crop.
