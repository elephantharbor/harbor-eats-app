## Steps

### 1. Roast the squash

Heat the oven to 425 F. Seed the butternut squash and cut it into 1-inch wedges. Toss with 1 tbsp of the olive oil, 1 tsp of the kosher salt, and 1/4 tsp of the black pepper. Spread in one layer on a sheet pan. Roast 35 minutes, flipping at 20 minutes, until the edges are browned and a knife meets no resistance. There is no flour and no breading.

### 2. Simmer the farro

When the pan goes into the oven, rinse the farro and simmer it in the water with the bay leaf and 3/4 tsp of the kosher salt for 30 minutes, until the grains are tender and some have split. Drain. Discard the bay leaf. This 30 minutes sits inside the 35-minute roast and is not added on top of it. Use pearled farro. Whole farro will not finish in this water or this time. If the pot goes dry before 25 minutes, the pot was too wide; this recipe does not add a rescue splash.

### 3. Mix the dressing

Whisk the remaining 4 tbsp olive oil with the zest and all of the juice of the lemon, the garlic, the dijon mustard, the fresh parsley, the remaining 1/4 tsp kosher salt, and the remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper until it looks lightly thickened. If it separates, whisk again. Do not add water, honey, or cheese.

### 4. Plate

Use a dinner plate, not a bowl. Put the farro on one side and the butternut squash wedges on the other. Do not toss them together. Spoon the dressing across both. No cheese, no nuts, no seeds, and no bread.

## Scaling for 1, 2, and 4

Ratios from a base of 4: 1 serving x0.25, 2 servings x0.5, 4 servings x1. Floors below override the ratio.

**1 serving.** Shop one small butternut, about 1.5 lb, not a precut quarter that you roast for the full 35 minutes without looking. Check a smaller piece at 25 minutes. Farro does not like a tiny wide pot: use 1/2 cup dry pearled farro and 1 3/4 cups water, which is above a strict quarter of 4.5 cups, so it does not boil dry. Bay leaf stays 1. Dressing oil floor is 1 tbsp, not 1 tsp, or the mustard will not emulsify it. Use 1/2 lemon, not a quarter. Garlic stays 1 small clove. Squash salt about 1/4 tsp; farro salt a pinch; dressing salt a small pinch off the tip of a 1/4 tsp spoon. Oven stays 425 F.

**2 servings.** Half of a 2.5 lb squash, or one squash at about 1.5 to 2 lb with leftovers that are not a second recipe. Farro: 3/4 cup dry and 2 1/2 cups water in a small saucepan (a strict half of 4.5 cups is 2 1/4 cups; the extra 1/4 cup is the floor for a wide pot). One bay leaf. Halve the dressing except do not go under 1 small lemon. Same oven. Roast time stays near 35 minutes if the wedges are still 1 inch.

**4 servings.** As written. One squash about 2.5 lb, 1.5 cups dry pearled farro, 4.5 cups water, one bay leaf, one lemon, 5 tbsp olive oil.

Do not scale: oven temperature; wedge thickness of about 1 inch; pearled-not-whole farro; bay leaf below 1; the rule not to add a rescue splash of water.
