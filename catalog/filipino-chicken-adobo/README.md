# Filipino Chicken Adobo

Bone-in chicken thighs seared until golden, braised in soy sauce, vinegar, smashed garlic, bay leaves, and cracked black pepper, then reduced until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the chicken. Served over jasmine rice with crisp garlic chips and scallions.

## Steps

### 1. Prep

Trim excess skin from the thighs and pat them very dry. Smash 8 garlic cloves; thinly slice the other 4. Lightly crack the peppercorns. Slice the scallions on the bias. Rinse the rice. In a measuring cup stir together the soy sauce, vinegar, 1/2 cup water, and brown sugar.

### 2. Make garlic chips

Put the oil and sliced garlic in a 12-inch deep skillet or 5-quart braiser with a lid and set over medium-low. Cook, stirring constantly, until the garlic is pale gold, 3 to 4 minutes. Lift out onto a paper towel (it darkens a shade as it cools). Keep the garlic oil in the pan.

### 3. Sear the chicken

Raise the heat to medium-high. Add the thighs skin-side down in a snug single layer and cook without moving until the skin is deep golden and much of the fat has rendered, 7 to 8 minutes. Flip and cook 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate. Pour off all but about 1 tbsp fat.

### 4. Start the rice

As the braise goes on, combine the rice, 2 1/4 cups water, and 1/2 tsp salt in a 2-quart saucepan; bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and cook 15 minutes, then leave covered off the heat until serving. Set-and-forget; runs inside steps 5 to 7 (overlap stated).

### 5. Build the braise

Over medium heat, add the smashed garlic and stir 30 seconds. Pour in the soy-vinegar mixture, add the peppercorns and bay leaves, and let it boil 1 minute without stirring to soften the vinegar's sharpness. Return the chicken skin-side up with any juices; the liquid should come about halfway up the thighs, leaving the skin above it.

### 6. Braise

Cover, reduce the heat to low, and simmer gently 25 minutes, until the thighs are tender and register at least 175°F near the bone.

### 7. Reduce to a glaze

Uncover, raise the heat to medium, and simmer, spooning the sauce over the chicken every minute or two, until the sauce has reduced by about half and turns glossy and lightly syrupy, 8 to 10 minutes. Remove the bay leaves before serving (one may be left on the platter as a visual cue; do not eat it).

### 8. Serve

Fluff the rice. Serve 2 thighs per person over or beside the rice, spoon the glaze over, and scatter the garlic chips and scallions on top. kitchen_tested is false.
