# Image / Plating Brief — Crispy Skillet Chicken Sandwiches

Source (read-only): certified package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`. No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

One assembled sandwich: a soft split roll, toasted on the cut side, holding a pound-thin chicken cutlet (about 1/4 inch), romaine, sliced tomato, and lemon-dijon mayonnaise. The sandwich is the meal. The recipe makes four; the frame should read as this sandwich, not a basket of sides.

## Vessel

Plain plate under the sandwich. Not a fry basket, not a board crowded with pickles, not the skillet as the served dish (the skillet is only the cooking pan).

## Camera angle

Low 3/4, chosen so the stack is legible: roll, sauce, romaine, tomato, thin cutlet. An overhead shot that hides the layers fails the recipe. Do not shoot a tall pub-style stack that the 1/4-inch cutlet cannot support.

## Required visible components

- Boneless skinless chicken breast, butterflied and pounded to about 1/4 inch, seared in canola oil in a skillet. Crust is all-purpose flour then beaten egg only: thin, deep golden, not a thick breadcrumb shell.
- Interior cooked through (165°F). No raw pink chicken. Color of the crust is not the doneness test, but the cut edge should not look rare.
- Soft sandwich roll, split, cut side toasted.
- Lemon-dijon mayonnaise spread on the rolls: store-bought mayonnaise mixed with Dijon, lemon zest and juice, minced garlic, chopped parsley, and salt. Parsley should read as flecks in the sauce, not a pesto layer.
- One large romaine leaf and slices of tomato.

## Texture cues

Crisp, thin flour-and-egg crust with real browning from the skillet, not a deep-fry shell and not a pale naked cutlet. Soft roll with a toasted cut face. Crisp romaine rib. Juicy tomato. Creamy mayonnaise, not a glossy glaze.

## Sauce/component placement

Lemon-dijon mayonnaise is the only sauce, spread on the rolls. Do not add a second drizzle, hot sauce, or gravy. Do not show a lemon wedge on the sandwich: the recipe uses the zest and juice of the whole lemon in the mayonnaise.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped parsley is an ingredient of the mayonnaise, not a separate sprinkled herb crown. No other garnish is listed.

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional, off the plate and out of the center crop: a small parsley sprig (the herb in the sauce). A whole lemon beside the plate is ambiguous — the recipe consumes zest and juice of the entire lemon and never serves a wedge — so do not plate a lemon, and do not rely on a lemon prop to imply a garnish. No tomato vine styled as a side salad.

## Natural color opportunities

Deep golden crust, red tomato, green romaine, pale roll, off-white herb mayonnaise. That is the contrast. Do not add pickle green, cheese orange, or hot-sauce red.

## Forbidden elements (ingredients/sides/techniques NOT in the recipe)

Fries, chips, pickles, cheese, bacon, avocado, onion, coleslaw, lettuce varieties other than romaine, iceberg shreds, a second sauce, honey, buffalo sauce, breadcrumbs, panko, a thick unpounded breast, deep-fried chicken, a grilled (bar-marked) breast in place of the skillet crust, butter on the roll, or a lemon wedge served on the plate.

## Crop strategy (1200x900 master, 640x480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are the same 4:3 frame. Put the sandwich slightly left or right of dead center only if the cut face stays inside the middle 75% of the width. The mobile recommendation card is a square center cover-crop (about 12.5% lost on each side); tablet recommendation cards stay 4:3. The cutlet edge, tomato, romaine, and sauce must remain identifiable after that square crop. Do not let the roll crown fill the frame so the filling is cropped away.
