# Smoky Pumpkin and Pinto Bean Chili

A thick, brick-red pinto bean chili built on smoked paprika, chili powder, and fire-roasted tomatoes, with canned pumpkin melted in for body and a gentle sweetness. Finished with lime and topped with diced avocado, radish, cilantro, and crushed corn tortilla chips. Plant-based; no cheese, no sour cream.

## Steps

### 1. Prep the vegetables, spices, and toppings

Dice the onion and the red bell pepper (1/2-inch). Mince the garlic. In a small bowl combine the 2 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp ground chipotle, and 1 tsp oregano. Measure the 2 tbsp tomato paste. Open the pumpkin and tomatoes; drain and rinse the pinto beans. For the toppings: thinly slice the radishes, chop the cilantro, juice 1 lime (about 2 tbsp) and cut the other into wedges, and lightly crush the tortilla chips in their bag. Leave the avocados whole for now. Confirm the contracts: vegetable broth, 100% pure pumpkin, plain corn chips. About 12 minutes.

_Clock: prep 12 min_

### 2. Soften the onion and pepper

Heat the 2 tbsp olive oil in a 5- to 6-quart Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium heat. Add the onion, bell pepper, and 1/2 tsp of the kosher salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and the onion edges turn golden, 6 to 7 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant. About 8 minutes.

_Clock: cook 8 min_

### 3. Bloom the spices and tomato paste

Add the spice mix and the tomato paste. Stir constantly for 1 to 2 minutes, until the paste darkens to brick red and the spices smell toasty. Lower the heat if anything starts to scorch. About 2 minutes.

_Clock: cook 2 min_

### 4. Simmer the chili

Add the pumpkin puree, the fire-roasted tomatoes with their juices, the pinto beans, the 2 cups vegetable broth, and the remaining 1 tsp kosher salt. Stir well, scraping up anything stuck to the bottom. Raise the heat to medium-high and bring to a simmer, about 5 minutes. Reduce to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes, until thick enough to mound on a spoon. In the last 5 minutes, press about 1 cup of the beans against the side of the pot with the spoon and stir them back in to thicken. While it simmers, halve, pit, and dice the avocados (this overlaps the simmer and adds no time). About 20 minutes.

_Clock: cook 20 min (avocado dicing overlaps the 15-minute simmer)_

### 5. Finish with lime and top

Take the pot off the heat and stir in the lime juice. Taste and add salt if needed. Ladle into wide bowls. Top each with diced avocado, radish coins, cilantro, and a handful of crushed tortilla chips; serve lime wedges alongside. No cheese, no sour cream. About 3 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 3 min_

## Scaling notes

- **1 serving(s):** Quarter everything; use a 2-quart saucepan. Use about 1/4 of the pumpkin can (about 1/2 cup) and refrigerate the rest. The smaller volume thickens faster: simmer about 10 minutes.
- **2 serving(s):** Halve everything in a 3-quart saucepan; 1 can of beans; about half the pumpkin can. Simmer about 12 minutes.
- **4 serving(s):** As written.
- Do not scale: vegetable broth contract; 100% pure pumpkin contract; plain corn chips (non-peanut oil) contract; no cheese or sour cream; dice avocado at serving
