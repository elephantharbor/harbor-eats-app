# Image / Plating Brief — Air-Fryer Honey-Mustard Pork Tenderloin

Source (read-only): wave-10 package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`.

## Hero subject

Sliced pork tenderloin medallions with sticky honey-mustard glaze, browned in patches, juices look cooked (145 F rest). Optional parsley flecks. Air-fryer method — looks roasted/glazed, not deep-fried breaded.

## Vessel

Warm off-white dinner plate. Slices fanned or slightly overlapped; glaze visible on surfaces.

## Camera angle

Slightly elevated 3/4 — shows glaze sheen and slice interior doneness. Not a raw-center hero.

## Required visible components

- Pork tenderloin slices (~1/2 inch), cooked through, faint blush OK if believable for 145 F after rest — not raw
- Honey-mustard glaze sticky and patch-browned
- No flour crust, no breadcrumbs, no egg wash coating
- Optional chopped parsley if used

## Texture cues

Glazed exterior, tender interior, sticky mustard-honey surface — not BBQ lacquer fantasy.

## Sauce/component placement

Glaze on the meat and a little on the board/plate from resting — not a separate gravy lake of unrelated sauce.

## Legitimate garnish

Optional parsley only. No fruit salsa invented.

## Environmental styling

Optional small mustard jar spoon or parsley sprig outside crop — do not show the air fryer appliance as the hero prop if it crowds the food. Food first.

## Natural color opportunities

Golden-brown glaze, cooked pork, parsley green. Do not invent charred broccoli or mac-and-cheese sides.

## Forbidden elements

Breading, fried cutlet look, bacon wrap (not in recipe), cream sauce, soy-teriyaki dark lacquer that implies soy sauce (not in glaze), nuts, text/logo/watermark, hands/faces.

## Crop strategy

Several medallions with visible glaze survive square center crop. Interior doneness readable at card size.

## Ambiguity

Air fryer is the method, not a required visible appliance in frame.
