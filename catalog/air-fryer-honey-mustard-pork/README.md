## Steps

### 1. Preheat the air fryer and make the glaze

Set the air fryer to 400 F and let it preheat about 3 minutes. Those 3 minutes are inside the cook time. Meanwhile, whisk the Dijon mustard, honey, apple cider vinegar, minced garlic, most of the olive oil (reserve 1 tsp), 1/4 tsp of the kosher salt, 1/4 tsp of the black pepper, and the smoked paprika into a smooth honey-mustard glaze. This glaze is made here. It is not a bottled honey-mustard salad dressing and not a soy-based teriyaki. Trim silver skin from the pork tenderloin and pat it dry. Rub with the remaining 1 tsp olive oil, then season with the remaining 3/4 tsp kosher salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper.

### 2. Air-fry the first stretch

Place the seasoned pork tenderloin in the air-fryer basket. Air-fry at 400 F for 10 minutes. The air fryer is the primary equipment; do not move this cook to a sheet pan unless you are abandoning this package method.

### 3. Glaze and finish

Brush about two-thirds of the honey-mustard glaze over the pork. Air-fry 6 to 8 minutes more at 400 F, until an instant-read thermometer in the thickest center reads 145 F and the glaze looks sticky and browned in patches. If the tenderloin is thick and still under 145 F at 8 minutes, continue in 2-minute checks; extra time beyond the written window is not inside the 35-minute total. Brush with the remaining glaze in the last 2 minutes if it looks dry. kitchen_tested is false.

### 4. Rest and slice

Transfer the pork to a cutting board. Rest 5 minutes (inside cook time). Slice into 1/2-inch medallions. Spoon any glaze from the board over the slices. Scatter optional parsley if using. No flour dredge. No egg wash. No breadcrumbs.
