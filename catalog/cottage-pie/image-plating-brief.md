# Image / Plating Brief — Cottage Pie with Thyme Gravy

Source (read-only): this package's recipe only — `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version` (written at package_revision `freeze-r2`, before generation; current package_revision `freeze-r3`, recipe and image unchanged). ## Hero subject

The cottage pie in its skillet: a ridged, golden-brown crisp mash top, with one portion scooped out to reveal glossy brown thyme gravy, ground beef, orange carrots, and green peas.

## Vessel

Cream-enameled 12-inch cast-iron skillet (light, oven-safe), plus a portion on an off-white plate in the foreground.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 40 degrees) so the ridged top and the cut cross-section both show.

## Required visible components

- Mash top raked into fork ridges with golden-brown crisp peaks
- One scooped portion missing from the skillet, exposing the filling
- Glossy brown gravy with ground beef
- Diced orange carrots and green peas visible
- Fresh thyme leaves scattered on top
- A served portion on a plate showing mash over filling

## Texture cues

Crisp, browned ridges over fluffy mash; glossy, bubbling gravy at the skillet edge; tender beef crumbles.

## Sauce / component placement

Gravy visible at the cut and bubbling slightly at the skillet edge; not flooding the plate.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Fresh thyme leaves and a small sprig (recipe-listed).

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bundle of fresh thyme, a serving spoon, a folded neutral linen. No bread or salad.

## Natural color opportunities

Golden-brown ridges, creamy mash, deep brown gravy, orange carrots, green peas, green thyme against a cream skillet.

## Forbidden elements

- Melted cheese on top
- Pastry crust
- Lamb chops or lamb
- Corn kernels
- Mushrooms
- Bread or side salad
- Black, burnt peaks
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Skillet and plated portion within the center 75% of the width; the scooped cross-section in the middle third so it reads at 640x480 and in the card crop. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

British cottage pie (beef, mash top), not shepherd's pie with lamb and not a pot pie.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
