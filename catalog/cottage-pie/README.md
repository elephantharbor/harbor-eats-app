## Steps

### 1. Prep and start the potatoes

Peel the potatoes, cut into 1 1/2-inch chunks, and put them in a large pot with cold water to cover by 1 inch and 1 tbsp salt. Dice the onion, carrots, and celery; mince the garlic; strip the thyme leaves (keep a pinch for the top). Set the potato pot over high heat at the end of prep.

### 2. Cook the potatoes

Bring the potatoes to a boil, about 10 minutes, then simmer until a knife slides in with no resistance, 12 to 15 minutes. Drain well and return to the hot pot for 1 minute to steam dry.

### 3. Brown the beef

Meanwhile, heat the oil in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Add the beef and 1 tsp salt, break it up, and cook until well browned with crusty bits, about 8 minutes. Spoon off all but about 1 tbsp of fat.

### 4. Soften the vegetables

Reduce the heat to medium. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook, stirring, until softened, about 6 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes, until the paste darkens.

### 5. Make the thyme gravy

Sprinkle the flour over the beef and stir 1 minute. Stir in the broth, Worcestershire, thyme leaves, bay leaf, and pepper, scraping up the browned bits. Simmer until the gravy is glossy and coats a spoon, about 10 minutes. At the start of this simmer, heat the oven to 400 F with a rack in the upper-middle position. Off the heat, remove the bay leaf and stir in the frozen peas.

### 6. Mash

Warm the milk (about 45 seconds in the microwave). Mash the potatoes with 4 tbsp butter, the warm milk, and 1/2 tsp salt until smooth and fluffy.

### 7. Top

Spoon the mash over the filling, starting at the edges to seal it in, then spread to cover. Drag a fork across the top to make deep ridges. Dot with the remaining 1 tbsp butter.

### 8. Bake, broil, and rest

Set the skillet on a rimmed sheet pan and bake until the gravy bubbles at the edges, about 20 minutes. Switch to broil and broil until the ridges are golden-brown and crisp, 2 to 3 minutes, watching closely. Rest 8 minutes so the gravy sets, scatter with the reserved thyme leaves, and serve in scoops. kitchen_tested is false.
