## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven, rest the dough, make the topping and salad base

Put a large rimmed sheet pan upside down on the lowest oven rack and heat the oven to 475 F for at least 20 minutes (it heats through this prep). Divide the dough into 6 balls, cover with a towel, and let it relax on the counter. Cut six 10-by-8-inch pieces of parchment (two fit side by side on the inverted pan with no overhang). For the topping, pulse the quartered onion, the bell pepper, 1 seeded tomato, the garlic, and 1/2 cup parsley in a food processor until finely minced. Add the lamb, tomato paste, 2 tsp Aleppo pepper, cumin, 1 tsp kosher salt, black pepper, and 1 tbsp olive oil; pulse to a smooth, spreadable paste. For the salad, toss the sliced red onion with the sumac and 1/4 tsp salt; dice the remaining 2 tomatoes; pick 1 1/2 cups parsley leaves; juice 1 lemon and cut the other into wedges. Do not dress the salad yet. About 20 minutes.

_Clock: prep 20 min (oven and inverted pan preheat at 475 F for the full 20 minutes)_

### 2. Roll and top the first two

Lightly flour two parchment pieces. Roll two dough balls directly on them into very thin ovals about 9 by 7 inches (about 1/8 inch). If the dough springs back, let it rest 1 minute and roll again. Spread one-sixth of the lamb paste over each in a thin, even layer right to within 1/4 inch of the edge, pressing it into the dough. About 6 minutes.

_Clock: cook 6 min_

### 3. Bake in three batches

Slide the two lahmacun, still on their parchment, onto the hot inverted pan. Bake 3 minutes, then use tongs to slide the parchment out from under each lahmacun and discard it; bake directly on the pan 4 to 5 minutes more (7 to 8 minutes total), until the rims and underside are browned and crisp and the topping is sizzling and cooked through with no pink (160 F). While each batch bakes, roll and top the next two on floured parchment. Repeat for three batches total, stacking finished lahmacun loosely on a board. About 24 minutes.

_Clock: cook 24 min_

### 4. Dress the salad and serve

Toss the sumac onions, diced tomatoes, and parsley leaves with the lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, and the last 1/2 tsp Aleppo pepper. Pile salad down the middle of each lahmacun, add a lemon wedge, and roll or fold to eat. No cheese, no yogurt. About 2 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 2 min_
