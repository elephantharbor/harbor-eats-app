# Image / Plating Brief — Saag Paneer

Source (read-only): wave-10 package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`.

## Hero subject

Skillet or shallow bowl of spinach saag with golden-browned paneer cubes visible in the green sauce, cream-enriched but still clearly spinach-forward, cilantro finish.

## Vessel

Prefer serving in a shallow bowl or showing a portion from the skillet on a warm off-white plate/bowl. Paneer cubes must be visible, not fully buried.

## Camera angle

Higher 3/4 — green saag body with distinct paneer cubes. Not a puree-only overhead with no paneer.

## Required visible components

- Coarse spinach saag (green, cooked, spoonable — not raw baby spinach leaves floating)
- Paneer cubes browned/golden on several faces
- Tomato integrated into saag (not a separate red pool)
- Cilantro flecks
- Cream richness visible as silkiness, not a white cream lake on top

## Texture cues

Soft browned paneer vs. thick spinach sauce; creamy finish without looking like pureed pea soup only.

## Sauce/component placement

Paneer nestled in saag. No chicken pieces. No lamb. No chickpeas.

## Legitimate garnish

Chopped cilantro; lemon is a finish — optional small lemon wedge outside crop only.

## Environmental styling

Optional cilantro sprig or cumin seeds environmental — do not add naan as required plated side if it implies a side not in the package (naan is outside this recipe). Keep props minimal.

## Natural color opportunities

Deep spinach green, golden paneer, cilantro bright green. Do not invent pomegranate seeds or cashews for color (nuts banned for HH001 awareness and not in recipe).

## Forbidden elements

Tofu labeled as paneer; chicken; chickpeas; heavy cream pour as a separate topping swirl that ignores the stirred finish; nuts; text/logo/watermark; hands/faces.

## Crop strategy

At least two paneer cubes and green saag remain after square center crop. Must read as saag paneer, not generic spinach soup.

## Ambiguity

Fresh spinach is the written path; image should look like cooked saag, not a raw salad with cheese cubes.
