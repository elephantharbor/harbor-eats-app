## Steps

### 1. Make the special sauce and toppings

Finely chop 2 tbsp of the pickles. Stir together the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, chopped pickles, 1 tsp pickle brine, garlic powder, and a pinch of pepper. Shred the lettuce, slice the onion paper-thin, and set out the remaining pickle chips.

### 2. Portion the beef and butter the buns

Divide the chuck into 8 loosely formed 3-oz balls without packing or kneading; keep them on a plate in the refrigerator until the pan is hot. Spread the softened butter on the cut sides of the buns. Cut 8 small squares of parchment for pressing.

### 3. Heat the skillet

Heat a 12-inch cast-iron skillet (or griddle) over medium-high until very hot, about 4 minutes; a drop of water should skitter.

### 4. Toast the buns

Toast the buttered cut sides in two batches until golden, about 1 minute per batch. Transfer to plates and spread the special sauce on both halves. Wipe the skillet with a paper towel.

### 5. Smash and sear in batches

Add a thin film of oil. Place 3 beef balls well apart in the skillet. Within the first 30 seconds, press each one hard with a stiff metal spatula (parchment square between) to about 1/4 inch thick with ragged edges. Season the tops with salt and pepper. Cook undisturbed until the edges are lacy and deep brown and only a little pink shows on top, about 2 minutes. Scrape under the crust with the spatula edge and flip. Lay a slice of cheese on each and cook 45 to 60 seconds. Stack each cheesy patty onto another to make doubles as they come off. Scrape out excess fat between batches and repeat in batches of 3, 3, and 2 (about 4 minutes per batch).

### 6. Assemble

Build each burger: sauced bottom bun, double patty with cheese, pickle chips, onion, shredded lettuce, sauced top bun. Serve immediately. kitchen_tested is false.
