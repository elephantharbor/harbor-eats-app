## Steps

### 1. Shred the cabbage and make the sauce

Core the cabbage and shred it very finely (about 1/8 inch) to get 6 packed cups. Thinly slice the scallions and set aside the greens of 2 for garnish. Stem the shiitake and thinly slice the caps. For the okonomi-style sauce, whisk 1/4 cup ketchup, 2 tbsp Worcestershire, 2 tsp of the soy sauce, and 2 tsp sugar until the sugar dissolves. Spoon the Japanese mayonnaise into a small zip-top bag (snip a tiny corner later) or a squeeze bottle. Contract check: no pork belly, no shrimp, no squid, no tenkasu, no cheese, no dashi powder. About 12 minutes.

_Clock: prep 12 min_

### 2. Mix the batter

In a large bowl whisk the 1 cup flour, 1/2 tsp baking powder, and 1/2 tsp kosher salt. Add the 2/3 cup cold water, the remaining 1 tsp soy sauce, and the 4 eggs and whisk until smooth. Fold in the cabbage and the sliced scallions (not the reserved greens) until every shred is coated. The mixture should be mostly cabbage, just bound by batter. About 3 minutes.

_Clock: prep 3 min_

### 3. Heat the skillet

Set a 12-inch nonstick skillet (or an electric griddle at 375 F) over medium heat with 1 1/2 tbsp of the neutral oil until shimmering. About 2 minutes.

_Clock: cook 2 min_

### 4. Cook the first side of batch one

Scoop a quarter of the batter into each side of the pan to form two rounds about 5 1/2 inches wide and 3/4 inch thick; tidy the edges with the spatula. Lay a quarter of the shiitake slices over the top of each, pressing them in lightly. Cover and cook until the underside is deep golden and the edges look set, 4 to 5 minutes. About 5 minutes.

_Clock: cook 5 min_

### 5. Flip and finish batch one

Using two wide spatulas, flip each pancake so the mushrooms are on the bottom. Press gently, cover, and cook 4 to 5 minutes, until the mushrooms are browned and the center is set: a thin knife in the middle comes out with no wet batter (the egg batter should reach 160 F). Flip once more so the first golden side faces up and move to a plate or rack, loosely tented. About 5 minutes.

_Clock: cook 5 min_

### 6. Cook batch two

Add the remaining 1 1/2 tbsp oil and repeat the two-sided cook with the rest of the batter and shiitake: 4 to 5 minutes covered, flip, 4 to 5 minutes covered, flip back. About 11 minutes.

_Clock: cook 11 min_

### 7. Sauce, mayo, and bonito

With the golden side up, spoon or brush the okonomi sauce over each pancake right to the edges. Snip a small corner of the mayo bag and pipe thin parallel lines across, then drag a chopstick through at right angles for a lattice. Scatter the bonito flakes (they ripple in the heat) and the reserved scallion greens; add aonori and pickled red ginger if using. Cut into wedges and serve hot. About 3 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 3 min_
