# Image / Plating Brief — Korean Soft Tofu Stew

Source (read-only): package recipe `v2.json` `recipe_version` (freeze-r6; same ingredients as freeze-r4 v1). No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

A bowl of Korean soft tofu stew (sundubu-style, seafood-free / meat-free): large spoonable chunks of soft tofu in a red spicy kimchi-gochugaru broth with zucchini coins and chopped plant kimchi, finished with sliced scallions and a sheen of toasted sesame oil. No egg on top.

## Vessel

Deep ceramic bowl (warm off-white / light stoneware). Stew fills the bowl. Not a flat plate. Not a skillet-as-serve unless composition still reads clearly as a bowl stew at card crop.

## Camera angle

Higher 3/4 / near-overhead — enough to see tofu chunks, red broth, zucchini, kimchi, and scallions as separate components. Not a side pour that hides the tofu.

## Required visible components

- Soft tofu in large white/ivory spoonable chunks, intact (not scrambled)
- Red spicy broth (gochugaru / gochujang color), glossy, not watery clear
- Chopped plant kimchi pieces
- Zucchini slices
- Sliced scallions scattered on top
- Subtle sesame-oil sheen — no separate oil puddle that looks like a second sauce

## Texture cues

Silky soft tofu vs. spicy broth; zucchini tender-crisp; kimchi distinct fermented cabbage pieces; scallions fresh.

## Sauce/component placement

Broth surrounds and partially covers tofu. Scallions on top. No egg yolk, no raw egg white, no sheet of egg. No seafood shells, shrimp, clams, or pork belly.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Sliced scallions; sesame oil as finish sheen only. Do not sprinkle sesame seeds unless added to the recipe (they are not listed).

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional and small, outside center crop: a scallion stalk or a small spoon of gochugaru. Do not add eggshells, mushrooms, rice bowl as a required side, meat, or seafood props.

## Natural color opportunities

Red chili broth, white tofu, green scallions, green zucchini, kimchi orange-red. Do not invent cilantro, lime, or cheese for color.

## Forbidden elements

Egg garnish or cracked egg; mushrooms of any kind; shrimp, mussels, clams, fish cakes, pork, beef; dairy swirl; rice mound required in-frame as part of the dish; jarred stew concentrate look; sesame-seed blizzard if seeds are not in the recipe; chopsticks branding; text/logo/watermark; hands/faces.

## Crop strategy (1200×900 master, 640×480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Fill the frame with the bowl. Keep several tofu chunks and red broth in the center square cover-crop (~12.5% off each side of 4:3). After crop it must still read as soft-tofu stew, not plain red soup.

## Ambiguity (not resolved; recipe not edited)

Rice on the side is outside the package; do not require a rice bowl in the hero. Soft tofu may look silken; keep chunks large and spoonable as written.
