# Image / Plating Brief — Vietnamese Chicken Pho

Source: wave-10 package recipe only. No recipe text changed for this brief.

## Hero subject
A deep bowl of Vietnamese chicken pho: flat rice noodles, shredded chicken, steaming clear amber broth, raw onion slivers, scallions, cilantro, Thai basil, lime wedge at the rim.

## Vessel
Deep ceramic soup bowl, off-white / warm-neutral. Not a flat plate. Not a skillet.

## Camera angle
Higher 3/4 / near-overhead so noodles, chicken, herbs, and broth surface read clearly. Not a side-only steam shot that hides the noodles.

## Required visible components
- Flat rice noodles submerged / partly visible under broth
- Shredded chicken strips (no skin, no bones)
- Clear chicken-spice broth (amber, not creamy, not coconut)
- Thin raw yellow onion slices
- Scallions, cilantro, Thai basil leaves
- Lime wedge (recipe-listed)
- Optional: jalapeño slices if shown as table garnish, not a second salad

## Texture cues
Broth looks hot and fluid. Noodles soft but distinct strands. Chicken moist shredded, not cubed grilled. Herbs fresh, not fried.

## Sauce/component placement
Broth fills the bowl; herbs floated on top; lime on the rim or saucer. No chili crisp oil slick unless recipe-listed (it is not). No hoisin swirl required.

## Legitimate garnish
Scallions, cilantro, Thai basil, lime, optional jalapeño — all recipe-listed.

## Environmental styling
Optional small: lime half, Thai basil sprig outside the bowl, outside center crop. Do not add bean sprouts if treated as required (recipe says not required) — omit sprouts to avoid fidelity fight. No chopsticks branding.

## Natural color opportunities
Amber broth, green herbs, white onion, pale noodles, lime. Do not invent red chili oil for color.

## Forbidden elements
Beef brisket/rare steak slices, meatballs, coconut milk, cream, egg noodles, wheat ramen, fried shallot piles not in recipe, Sriracha bottle logos, chopsticks with brand marks, hands/faces/text/watermarks, dark restaurant gloom.

## Crop strategy
4:3 master 1200×900 and 640×480 card. Bowl fills frame. Keep herbs + chicken + noodle strands in center for square card crop.
