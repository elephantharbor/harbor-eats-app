## Steps

### 1. Char onion and ginger

Heat a dry 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Place the halved unpeeled yellow onion cut-side down and the halved ginger cut-side down. Char until blackened in spots and fragrant, about 5 minutes per side for the onion and 4 minutes for the ginger, turning once. Transfer to a plate. Do not add oil.

### 2. Toast spices and start the stock

In a large stockpot over medium heat, toast the star anise, cinnamon stick, whole cloves, and coriander seeds until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add the 12 cups water, the bone-in chicken thighs, the charred onion, and the charred ginger. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Skim foam. Simmer uncovered until the chicken is cooked through and the broth tastes of chicken and spice, about 35 minutes. The thickest thigh should read 175 F near the bone.

### 3. Pull chicken and season broth

Lift the chicken to a board. Strain the broth into a clean pot; discard the solids. Return the clear broth to a simmer. Stir in the fish sauce, kosher salt, and sugar. Taste; the broth should be savory and lightly sweet, not salty-flat. Keep at a bare simmer. When the chicken is cool enough to handle, discard skin and bones. Shred the meat into bite-size strips.

### 4. Soak and soften the rice noodles

Place the dried flat rice noodles in a large bowl. Cover with very hot tap water and soak until pliable but not mushy, about 8 to 12 minutes depending on brand. Drain. Do not boil them in the pho broth.

### 5. Assemble bowls

Divide the drained noodles among 4 deep bowls. Top with shredded chicken and the thinly sliced raw yellow onion. Ladle about 2 cups of very hot broth over each bowl so the noodles finish softening and the chicken warms through, about 1 minute in the bowl. The broth must be steaming hot when served.

### 6. Finish with herbs and lime

Scatter the sliced scallions, chopped cilantro, and Thai basil leaves over each bowl. Serve with lime wedges and optional jalapeño slices on the side. Each serving is noodles, chicken, hot broth, and herbs. No bean sprouts are required. kitchen_tested is false.
