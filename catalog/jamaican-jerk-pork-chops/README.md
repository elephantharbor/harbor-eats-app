## Steps

### 1. Make the jerk paste

In a blender or mini food processor, combine the scallions, thyme leaves, garlic, ginger, Scotch bonnet, ground allspice, cinnamon, nutmeg, brown sugar, tamari, 2 tbsp lime juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp kosher salt, and black pepper. Blend to a thick, spoonable paste, about 30 to 60 seconds, scraping once. Wear gloves when handling the Scotch bonnet.

### 2. Coat the chops

Pat the pork chops dry. Rub all of the jerk paste over both sides of the 4 chops. Rest at room temperature 15 minutes while you make the slaw. Do not rinse the paste off.

### 3. Make the pineapple slaw

In a large bowl, toss the shredded green cabbage, diced pineapple, julienned red bell pepper, chopped cilantro, juice of 1 lime (about 2 tbsp), and the remaining 1/4 tsp kosher salt. Taste; the slaw should be bright and crunchy, not watery. Hold at room temperature while the chops cook.

### 4. Sear the jerk chops

Heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Add the chops in a single layer. Cook until the first side is deep brown with spice crust, about 5 to 6 minutes. Flip. Cook the second side until an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part away from bone reads 145 F, about 5 to 7 minutes more. If the paste darkens too fast, lower to medium-low. Do not char to bitter black.

### 5. Rest and plate

Transfer chops to a plate. Rest 5 minutes. Plate one chop per person with a mound of pineapple slaw beside it. Spoon any skillet juices over the pork. No second sauce. kitchen_tested is false.
