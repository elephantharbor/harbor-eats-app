# Image / Plating Brief — Jamaican Jerk Pork Chops with Pineapple Slaw

## Hero subject
One bone-in jerk pork chop with visible spice crust, plated with a generous mound of pineapple-cabbage slaw.

## Vessel
Modern dinner plate, off-white / warm-neutral. Not a bowl-only presentation. Not a cutting board heap.

## Camera angle
Slightly elevated 3/4 for chop thickness, crust, and bone. Slaw must remain readable.

## Required visible components
- Bone-in pork loin chop, seared brown jerk crust (allspice-thyme-chile paste), not breaded, not BBQ sauce-glazed
- Pineapple-cabbage slaw: green cabbage shreds, pineapple dice, red bell pepper julienne, cilantro
- Optional skillet juices on the chop only

## Texture cues
Chop juicy at 145 F rest, crust browned. Slaw crunchy and bright — not wilted coleslaw mayo style (no mayo in recipe).

## Sauce/component placement
Chop dominant; slaw beside, not buried under. No bottled jerk sauce puddle. No ranch.

## Legitimate garnish
Cilantro already in slaw; optional thyme sprig only if treated as environmental from recipe thyme — keep minimal. Lime wedge OK (lime is in recipe).

## Environmental styling
Optional lime half or scallion outside plate, outside center crop. Do not add rice-and-peas as an unlisted side.

## Forbidden elements
Mayo coleslaw, rice and peas, plantains as required side, bottled bright-red jerk sauce look, deep-fry breading, cheese, yogurt, hands/text/logos, fake gloss.

## Crop strategy
4:3; keep chop + slaw in center for square card crop.
