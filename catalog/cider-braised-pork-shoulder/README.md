## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven and season

Heat the oven to 325 F. Pat the boneless pork shoulder dry. Season it on all sides with 2 tsp of the kosher salt and the black pepper. If the piece flaps into two thin tails, tie it with cotton kitchen twine. If it is already one compact piece, do not tie it. The twine is equipment, not an ingredient.

### 2. Sear

Set a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add 1 tbsp of the olive oil. Sear the boneless pork shoulder on all sides until deeply browned, about 8 minutes. Move the pork to a plate. Do not wipe out the fond.

### 3. Build the braise

Add the yellow onion, the apple wedges, the garlic, and the fresh thyme to the pot. Stir 4 minutes, until the onion softens. Add the apple cider, 1/2 cup of the water, and 2 tbsp of the dijon mustard. Return the pork. The liquid should come about halfway up the meat. If the pot is so wide that the liquid sits below one-third of the height, move to a smaller pot. Do not pour in a second carton of cider.

### 4. Braise

Cover and put the pot in the 325 F oven. Start checking at 2 hours 30 minutes. It is done at 2 hours 30 minutes to 2 hours 45 minutes, when the center is 195-203 F and a fork slides in with little resistance. The published cook time uses the long end, 2 hours 45 minutes. A shoulder that is tender at 2 hours 30 minutes finishes 15 minutes sooner. 145 F is not the target for this cut.

### 5. Rest and reduce

Move the boneless pork shoulder to a board and rest it 15 minutes. Discard the fresh thyme sprigs. While it rests, boil the pot liquid over medium-high until it coats a spoon, about 8 to 10 minutes. You want about 1 cup. Mash the soft apple into the sauce. Do not add flour. If a thick cap of fat is on the surface, skim it. This reduction shares the rest window with the cabbage and needs a second burner.

### 6. Cook the cabbage

During that same rest, heat a 12-inch skillet over medium. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Lay in the 8 green cabbage wedges, cut side down, for 3 minutes, until the cut face browns. Turn them. Add the remaining 1/4 cup water, the apple cider vinegar, the remaining 1 tbsp dijon mustard, and the remaining 1/2 tsp kosher salt. Cover 4 minutes, until the core is tender to a knife. Uncover if the skillet is watery. One burner instead of two adds about 10 minutes after the sauce; the published total is the two-burner clock.

### 7. Slice and serve

Remove any twine. If the boneless pork shoulder holds, cut 1/2-inch slices against the grain. If it collapses, pull large chunks. Do not puree it and do not shred it into a fine pile. Spoon the cider sauce and the soft apple over the pork. Sprinkle the fresh parsley. Serve the green cabbage wedges beside the meat. No bread and no potatoes.

## Scaling for 1, 2, and 4

Ratios from a base of 4: 1 serving x0.25, 2 servings x0.5, 4 servings x1. Floors below override the ratio.

**1 serving.** Buy about 1 lb boneless pork shoulder in one chunk, not thin cutlets. Braising liquid has a floor: 1 cup apple cider and 1/4 cup water in the pot, even though a strict quarter of the written liquid is less. Below that the pot scorches. Cabbage water: 1 tbsp. Use 1 small apple, not a quarter of an apple. Two thyme sprigs, not a fraction of a sprig. Two cabbage wedges from a small head; the rest of the head is not part of this scaled recipe. Dijon: 2 tsp in the pot and 1 tsp on the cabbage. Oven stays 325 F. Target stays 195-203 F. Time does not quarter: plan about 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours in the oven for a 1 lb piece of similar thickness.

**2 servings.** 1.5 lb in one piece. Liquid floor: 1 1/4 cups apple cider and 1/3 cup water in the pot, not a strict half, if the Dutch oven is wide. Cabbage water: 2 tbsp. One apple. Two thyme sprigs. Four cabbage wedges. Halve the salt, pepper, oil, and dijon (1 tbsp in the pot, 1 1/2 tsp on the cabbage). Oven 325 F and 195-203 F do not change. Oven time is about 2 hours 15 minutes, not half of 2 hours 45 minutes.

**4 servings.** As written. 3 lb is about 12 oz raw per person before the braise shrinks it. Liquid is 2 cups cider plus 1/2 cup water in the pot and 1/4 cup water in the skillet.

Do not scale: oven temperature; 195-203 F doneness; the rule that the liquid must reach about halfway up the meat; rest time.
