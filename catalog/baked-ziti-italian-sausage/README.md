## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven and prep

Set a rack in the upper-middle position and heat the oven to 400°F; it preheats during prep and the sauce (about 37 minutes before the bake; overlap stated). Bring 4 quarts water with 1 tbsp salt to a boil in a large covered pot. Dice the onion, mince the garlic, shred the mozzarella, and grate the Parmesan. Stir the ricotta with 1/4 tsp salt, the pepper, and 2 tbsp torn basil.

### 2. Brown the sausage

Heat the olive oil in a 12-inch deep skillet or 5-quart Dutch oven over medium-high. Add the sausage and break it into bite-size pieces; cook until well browned and no longer pink, 6 to 7 minutes.

### 3. Add the aromatics

Add the onion and cook until softened, about 3 minutes. Add the garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes and stir 30 seconds.

### 4. Simmer the sauce

Add the crushed tomatoes and 1/2 tsp salt. Simmer gently, stirring now and then, until slightly thickened, about 12 minutes. Taste for salt.

### 5. Boil the pasta

While the sauce simmers, add the ziti to the boiling water and cook 2 minutes less than the package's al dente time (about 8 minutes); it finishes in the oven. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water and drain. Runs inside step 4 (overlap stated).

### 6. Combine and layer

Off heat, stir the ziti into the sauce with 1 cup mozzarella and 1/4 cup Parmesan, adding a splash of pasta water if it looks tight. Spread half in a 9x13-inch (3-quart) baking dish, dollop the ricotta mixture over in spoonfuls, and cover with the rest of the pasta. Scatter the remaining 1 cup mozzarella and 1/4 cup Parmesan over the top.

### 7. Bake

Bake uncovered on the upper-middle rack until bubbling at the edges and the cheese is melted with golden-brown spots, about 20 minutes.

### 8. Rest and serve

Rest 5 minutes so it holds together, then scatter the remaining 2 tbsp torn basil. kitchen_tested is false.
