# Chicken Enchiladas Verdes

Finished with lime crema, diced onion, radishes, and cilantro.

## Steps

### 1. Heat the broiler and load the pan

Set an oven rack about 6 inches below the broiler and heat the broiler to high; it heats during this prep (overlap stated). Line a rimmed sheet pan with foil. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp oil, the cumin, 3/4 tsp salt, and the pepper. Arrange the chicken, tomatillos, the two onion wedges, jalapeño halves (cut side down), and unpeeled garlic on the pan in a single layer.

### 2. Prep the garnishes

Finely dice a quarter of the onion. Thinly slice the radishes. Pick 1/4 cup cilantro leaves; roughly chop the rest with tender stems for the salsa. Shred the cheese if using a block. Juice the lime.

### 3. Broil

Broil 6 minutes, until the tomatillos blister and the chicken browns in spots. Flip the chicken and turn the tomatillos; broil 5 to 6 minutes more, until the tomatillos are soft, slumped, and charred in patches and the chicken reaches 175°F. Move the chicken to a board. Switch the oven to bake at 425°F (it is already hot).

### 4. Blend the salsa verde

Squeeze the garlic from its skins. Blend the tomatillos with their juices, broiled onion, jalapeños, garlic, 3/4 cup chopped cilantro, 1 tbsp lime juice, 3/4 tsp salt, and 1/4 cup water until smooth but still slightly textured. Taste. You should have about 3 1/2 cups.

### 5. Make the filling

Shred the chicken with two forks and toss with 3/4 cup salsa verde and 1 cup Monterey Jack.

### 6. Warm the tortillas

Brush the tortillas lightly on both sides with 1 tbsp oil, stack, wrap in a damp paper towel, and microwave about 60 seconds until pliable (or warm them one at a time in a dry skillet). Warm tortillas roll without cracking.

### 7. Fill and roll

Spread 1/2 cup salsa in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Fill each tortilla with about 1/3 cup filling, roll snugly, and lay seam-side down in two rows. Pour the remaining salsa (about 2 1/4 cups) evenly over, covering the tortilla ends so they don't dry out. Scatter the remaining 1 cup cheese over the top.

### 8. Bake

Bake at 425°F until the salsa bubbles at the edges and the cheese is melted with golden spots, about 15 minutes.

### 9. Finish

Stir the sour cream with the remaining 1 tbsp lime juice and 1 tbsp water into a drizzle-able lime crema. Rest the enchiladas 3 minutes, then drizzle with crema and scatter the diced onion, radishes, and cilantro leaves. Serve 3 per person. kitchen_tested is false.
