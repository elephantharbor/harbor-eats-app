## Steps

### 1. Start the water and prep

Fill a large pot with 4 quarts water, add the 2 tbsp kosher salt, cover, and set over high heat. It takes about 12 minutes to boil, which overlaps this prep and the anchovy step. Thinly slice the garlic. Drain and finely chop the anchovies. Roughly chop the olives. Rinse and drain the capers. Crush the tomatoes by hand in a bowl, keeping every bit of juice. Chop the parsley. HARD CONTRACT: egg-free dried spaghetti; no Parmesan, pecorino, butter, or cream anywhere in this dish.

### 2. Melt the anchovies with garlic and chile

In a 12-inch skillet over medium heat, warm 3 tbsp olive oil. Add the anchovies, garlic, and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring and pressing the anchovies with a wooden spoon, until they dissolve into the oil and the garlic is pale gold at the edges, about 3 minutes. Do not let the garlic go dark brown. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1 minute, until it darkens slightly.

### 3. Simmer the sauce

Add the crushed tomatoes and their juice, the olives, capers, and oregano. Bring to a brisk simmer over medium-high, then adjust the heat to keep it bubbling steadily. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until the sauce is thick and glossy and the oil begins to pool at the edges, about 10 minutes.

### 4. Cook the spaghetti

As soon as the sauce reaches its simmer (the water is at a rolling boil by now), add the spaghetti to the pot. Stir during the first minute so it does not stick. Cook until 1 minute shy of package al dente, about 8 to 9 minutes. Scoop out 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.

### 5. Toss and finish

Add the drained spaghetti and 1/2 cup reserved pasta water to the sauce. Toss with tongs over medium-high heat until the sauce clings to every strand and looks glossy, 1 to 2 minutes; add more pasta water a splash at a time if it tightens. Off heat, toss in most of the parsley, the black pepper, and the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Taste and add a pinch of salt only if needed. Serve in shallow bowls topped with the reserved parsley. No cheese, no butter.
