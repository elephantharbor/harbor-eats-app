# Image / Plating Brief — Grilled Swordfish with Olive-Caper Relish

Source (read-only): certified package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`. No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

A thick grilled swordfish steak (about 6 oz, about 1 inch thick) with olive-caper relish spooned on it, and grilled fennel wedges beside it. One plated steak is the hero. The recipe serves each steak this way. Do not turn it into a rice plate.

## Vessel

Dinner plate. Not a bowl. Not a bed of grains.

## Camera angle

Slightly elevated 3/4 so the steak’s thickness, grill marks, fennel wedges, and the relish on top are all readable. The recipe does not slice the fish, so do not rely on a cut face.

## Required visible components

- Swordfish steak, patted dry, salted and peppered, grilled over direct high heat (about 450–500°F): about 4 minutes on the first side without moving, then 3–4 minutes. Grill marks. Pull target in the recipe is 130–135°F in the center, then a 3-minute rest: edge opaque, center just losing translucence, not chalky. Because the steak is served whole, the interior may not be visible. Do not invent a sliced presentation to prove doneness.
- Fennel bulb wedges (1/2 inch), grilled about 3 minutes per side, cut faces browned, still with slight resistance. Two bulbs in the recipe; show wedges for the plated steak, not a raw shaved fennel salad.
- Olive-caper relish spooned on the fish, uncooked: chopped pitted green olives, rinsed capers, minced oil-packed anchovy, minced garlic, lemon zest and all of the lemon juice, chopped parsley, olive oil, a little kosher salt, red pepper flakes. It should look like a chopped relish, not a smooth puree and not a cooked sauce.

## Texture cues

Firm steak with distinct grill marks, moist not chalky, no breading. Fennel wedges with browned cut faces. Relish is chunky: visible olive pieces, capers, and parsley. Anchovy is minced into the relish, not laid on as whole fillets.

## Sauce/component placement

The relish is the only sauce. Spoon it on the fish at serving. It is not a marinade and it is not rubbed on before the grill. No second sauce. No oil slick beyond the olive oil bound into the relish. Fennel sits beside the steak, not under a pool of relish.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley, lemon zest, and red pepper flakes are in the relish, not separate garnishes. The recipe does not list an extra herb or citrus finish. No lemon wedge: the serve step says no extra lemon wedge, and all zest and juice go into the relish.

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional, off the plate, outside the center crop: a few green olives, capers, or a parsley sprig corresponding to the relish. Do not add a lemon half; that would read as the wedge the recipe forbids. Do not add fennel fronds; the bulb is trimmed into wedges and fronds are not an ingredient.

## Natural color opportunities

Pale grilled fish with dark grill marks, green olive relish, caper green, parsley green, browned fennel. Keep it believable. Do not add tomato, citrus wedges, or chili threads for color.

## Forbidden elements (ingredients/sides/techniques NOT in the recipe)

Rice, couscous, potatoes, a lemon wedge, a second sauce, tomato, caperberries in place of capers if they change the look to large berries, whole anchovy fillets arranged on top, cheese, nuts, herbs other than parsley, blackened Cajun crust, breading, frying, or a relish cooked onto the fish as a glaze.

## Crop strategy (1200x900 master, 640x480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Master 1200×900 and 640×480 card are one 4:3 frame. Place the steak and the relish near center, fennel immediately beside it and still inside the central 75% of the width. Mobile recommendation cards square-crop the 4:3 frame from the center (`object-fit: cover`, about 12.5% off each side). Tablet cards stay 4:3. Relish and at least one browned fennel face must survive that square crop.

## Ambiguity (not resolved; recipe not edited)

Doneness is defined by an internal temperature and a center that is just losing translucence, but the serve step does not cut the steak. Showing a cut face would change the image and is not instructed. Do not slice the fish to illustrate 130–135°F.
