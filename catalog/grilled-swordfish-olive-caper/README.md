## Steps

### 1. Mix the relish

Chop the pitted green olives. Rinse and drain the capers. Mince the anchovy fillets and the garlic. Zest the lemon and squeeze in all of the juice. Chop the fresh parsley. Stir them with 3 tbsp of the olive oil, 1/4 tsp of the kosher salt, and the red pepper flakes. This olive-caper relish is the only sauce. It is not cooked, and it is not a marinade rubbed on the fish before the grill.

### 2. Heat the grill

Heat a gas or charcoal grill for direct high heat, about 450-500 F, for about 10 minutes. Brush the grates with 1 tbsp of the olive oil. This clock does not overlap the preheat with the relish.

### 3. Season

Pat the swordfish steak dry. Season both sides with 3/4 tsp of the kosher salt and the black pepper. Toss the fennel bulb wedges with 1 tbsp of the olive oil and the remaining 1/4 tsp kosher salt.

### 4. Grill the fennel

Lay the fennel bulb wedges on the oiled grates. Grill about 3 minutes per side, until the cut faces are browned and a knife meets slight resistance. Move them to a plate.

### 5. Grill the swordfish

Lay the swordfish steak on the grates. Cook 4 minutes on the first side without moving it, then flip. Cook 3 to 4 minutes on the second side. Pull it at 130-135 F in the center. Do not press the fish. If a steak is thicker than 1 1/4 inches, add about 1 minute per side and trust the thermometer. For 145 F, add about 1 minute per side and expect a drier steak.

### 6. Rest and plate

Rest the swordfish steak 3 minutes. Serve each steak with fennel bulb wedges and spoon the relish on the fish. No rice, no extra lemon wedge, and no second sauce.

## Scaling for 1, 2, and 4

Ratios from a base of 4: 1 serving x0.25, 2 servings x0.5, 4 servings x1. Floors below override the ratio.

**1 serving.** One 6 oz swordfish steak, about 1 inch thick, not a thin tail scrap. Half a fennel bulb. Anchovy does not scale to a quarter fillet: use 1 anchovy fillet. Lemon does not scale to a quarter lemon: use 1/2 lemon, zest and juice, so the relish stays wet. Oil: about 1 tsp on the grates, 1 tsp on the fennel, 2 tsp in the relish. Olives: 2 tbsp chopped. Capers: 1 1/2 tsp rinsed. Garlic: 1 small clove, not a quarter clove. Salt: a scant 1/4 tsp on the fish, a pinch on the fennel, a pinch in the relish. Grill temperature stays 450-500 F. Same thickness uses the same minutes; use the thermometer.

**2 servings.** Two steaks and 1 fennel bulb. Two anchovy fillets. Use 1 small lemon, not half a lemon. Halve the olives, capers, oil, and salt. Keep 1 garlic clove. Same grill temperature and the same 130-135 F target.

**4 servings.** As written. 1.5 lb is four steaks, about 6 oz raw each.

Do not scale: grill temperature; internal temperature target; rest time; steak thickness of about 1 inch.
