## Steps

### 1. Start the water and prep

Fill a 5- to 6-quart pot with 3 quarts water, add 1 tbsp of the kosher salt, cover, and bring to a boil over high heat (about 10 minutes; this overlaps the prep). Halve the potatoes, trim the green beans, halve the cherry tomatoes, separate, wash, and dry the lettuce leaves, mince the shallot, grate the garlic, chop the parsley, and drain the capers. Set out a large bowl of ice water. Pat the tuna dry and leave it on a plate while you work. About 12 minutes.

_Clock: prep 12 min (pot comes to a boil during this prep block)_

### 2. Shake the Dijon-shallot vinaigrette

In a jar, combine the minced shallot, 3 tbsp red wine vinegar, and 1/2 tsp kosher salt; let it sit while you measure the rest so the shallot mellows. Add the 1 tbsp Dijon, the grated garlic, 1/4 tsp cracked pepper, and the 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil. Close and shake hard until creamy and emulsified. The dressing is oil, vinegar, and mustard only: no butter, cream, yogurt, or cheese. About 3 minutes.

_Clock: prep 3 min_

### 3. Boil the potatoes and jammy eggs in one pot

Add the potatoes to the boiling water and keep it at a steady boil. After 6 minutes, gently lower the cold eggs into the same pot with a slotted spoon. Boil 7 more minutes, then move the eggs to the ice water. Test a potato: a knife should slide in with no resistance. Lift the potatoes out with the slotted spoon into a wide bowl and toss them while hot with 2 tbsp of the vinaigrette so they absorb it. Keep the water boiling. About 13 minutes.

_Clock: cook 13 min_

### 4. Blanch the green beans; heat the skillet

Drop the green beans into the same boiling water and cook 2 1/2 to 3 minutes, until bright green and crisp-tender. Move them to the ice water with the eggs for 1 minute, then drain and pat dry. At the same time, set a 12-inch cast-iron or heavy stainless skillet over high heat with the 1 tbsp neutral oil so it is shimmering hot by the time the beans are out. About 3 minutes.

_Clock: cook 3 min_

### 5. Sear the tuna

Season the tuna on all sides with 3/4 tsp kosher salt and 1/2 tsp cracked pepper, pressing the pepper in. Lay the steaks in the shimmering skillet and do not move them: 1 1/2 to 2 minutes per side for a rare, deep-red center with a thin cooked band (about 115 F inside), or 3 minutes per side to cook through (145 F). Briefly sear the edges with tongs. Move to a cutting board. About 4 minutes.

_Clock: cook 4 min_

### 6. Rest the tuna; peel the eggs

Let the tuna rest 3 to 4 minutes. Meanwhile peel the eggs under a trickle of water and halve them lengthwise; the yolks should be jammy and just set at the edges. About 4 minutes.

_Clock: cook 4 min_

### 7. Slice and compose

Slice the tuna across the grain into 1/2-inch slices. Line a large platter or 4 shallow plates with lettuce leaves. Arrange the dressed potatoes, green beans, cherry tomatoes, egg halves, and olives in separate groups, and fan the tuna slices in the center. Scatter the capers and parsley; drape the anchovy fillets over the eggs if using. Spoon the remaining vinaigrette over everything and finish with the last 1/4 tsp cracked pepper. Serve right away while the potatoes are warm. About 5 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 5 min_
