## Steps

### 1. Mix the blackening rub and make the remoulade

Stir together the sweet paprika, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, dried thyme, dried oregano, 1 tsp of the kosher salt, and 1/2 tsp of the black pepper. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg mayonnaise with the Dijon mustard, lemon juice, 1 tbsp of the neutral oil, dill pickle relish, chopped capers, parsley, chives, garlic paste, hot sauce, the remaining 1/4 tsp kosher salt, and the remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper. This herb remoulade is made here. It is not a bottled remoulade. Confirm the mayonnaise has no milk, buttermilk, or cream. Chill the remoulade while the fish cooks. Pat the catfish fillets dry.

### 2. Coat the catfish

Press both sides of each catfish fillet into the blackening rub so the spices adhere in an even coat. Shake off only the loosest excess. Do not flour the fish. Do not egg-wash it. Do not bread it.

### 3. Blacken in a hot skillet

Heat a 12-inch cast-iron or heavy stainless skillet over medium-high until it is very hot, about 3 minutes. Add 2 tbsp of the neutral oil and swirl. Lay in as many fillets as fit without crowding (often 2). Cook undisturbed until the spice crust is darkened and fragrant and the edges look opaque, about 3 to 4 minutes. Flip once. Cook the second side about 3 to 4 minutes more, until the thickest part flakes and is opaque (about 145 F if checked). Move cooked fillets to a warm plate. Add a thin film of the remaining oil if the pan is dry and cook the remaining fillets the same way. The written clock uses one 3-minute preheat plus two batches of about 7 minutes each (14) overlapping the plate-hold; cook_minutes below uses 17 for preheat + batch work. Do not deep-fry. Do not bake. kitchen_tested is false.

### 4. Plate with remoulade

Serve each blackened catfish fillet with a spoonful of the dairy-free herb remoulade on the side or a stripe on the plate. No butter basting. No buttermilk soak. No nuts. No breaded coating.
