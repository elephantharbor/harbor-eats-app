# Image / Plating Brief — Cajun Blackened Catfish with Herb Remoulade

Source (read-only): wave-10 package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`.

## Hero subject

Blackened catfish fillet with a darkened Cajun spice crust, plated with a visible spoonful or stripe of pale herb remoulade (parsley/chive flecks). Dairy-free remoulade — no buttermilk look, no cream swirl.

## Vessel

Clean modern dinner plate (warm off-white / light stoneware). Fillet as the hero; remoulade as accompanying sauce, not drowning the fish.

## Camera angle

Slightly elevated 3/4 — shows crust texture and fillet thickness. Not a pure overhead that flattens the crust; not a macro of remoulade alone.

## Required visible components

- Catfish fillet with blackened (darkened, fragrant) spice crust — paprika/cayenne color, not pale raw spice and not incinerated ash
- Opaque cooked fish edges / flake suggestion without looking dry
- Herb remoulade: mayo-based, pale, with visible parsley and chives, pickle/caper texture optional but believable
- No flour breading, no cornmeal crust, no taco shell, no slaw pile unless later added to recipe (not listed)

## Texture cues

Charred-spice crust vs. moist fish interior suggestion; remoulade creamy and spoonable (egg mayo), not broken oil.

## Sauce/component placement

Remoulade beside or lightly striped — amount plausible for a spoonful per serving. Do not bury the fillet under sauce.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley/chives are inside the remoulade. Do not add lemon wedges unless lemon is shown only as environmental juice fruit matching the remoulade lemon juice — optional small lemon half outside center crop is acceptable; do not invent a side salad.

## Environmental styling

Optional: small lemon half or parsley sprig outside center crop. No shrimp, grits bowl, cornbread, or butter pats.

## Natural color opportunities

Dark spice crust, pale remoulade with green herbs, warm plate. Do not invent tomato salsa or avocado for color.

## Forbidden elements

Buttermilk drizzle, cheese, butter basting pools, breading/batter, taco shells, cabbage slaw (not in this recipe), shrimp, grits, nuts, text/logo/watermark, hands/faces.

## Crop strategy

Fillet and remoulade both survive the square center cover-crop. Crust texture must remain readable at 640×480.

## Ambiguity

Recipe allows two skillet batches; image shows plated finished fillets (one or two as a serving), not the empty cast-iron alone.
