# Image / Plating Brief — Thai Red Curry with Kabocha and Tofu

## Hero subject
Bowl of Thai red coconut curry with kabocha chunks and golden tofu cubes over jasmine rice, Thai basil and lime.

## Vessel
Shallow bowl, off-white / warm-neutral. Curry and rice together as served.

## Camera angle
Higher 3/4 / near-overhead for bowls/curries — show kabocha, tofu, red coconut sauce, basil.

## Required visible components
- Orange kabocha chunks in red coconut curry
- Golden-brown tofu cubes (extra-firm, not silken collapse)
- Red bell pepper strips
- Thai basil leaves
- Jasmine rice (visible under or beside curry)
- Lime wedge
- No shrimp, no fish, no chicken, no egg, no peanut garnish

## Texture cues
Sauce creamy-coconut (not broken oily). Kabocha tender. Tofu browned edges. Basil fresh.

## Forbidden elements
Fish sauce bottle, shrimp, chicken, egg, peanuts, dairy cream swirl, chili crisp not listed, hands/text/logos.

## Crop strategy
4:3; bowl fills frame; kabocha+tofu in center for card crop. Plant-forward equal hero treatment.
