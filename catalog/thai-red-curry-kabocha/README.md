## Steps

### 1. Cook the rice

Rinse the jasmine rice. Cook with water per package directions (typically 1.5 cups rice to about 2.25 cups water), covered, until tender, about 15 minutes, then rest 5 minutes off heat. Start this before or while the curry so rice is ready at plating.

### 2. Press and brown the tofu

Press the tofu 10 minutes under a plate. Cut into 3/4-inch cubes. Pat dry and season with 1/4 tsp of the kosher salt. Heat 1 tbsp coconut oil in a wide pot or deep skillet over medium-high. Brown the tofu on two sides until golden, about 6 minutes total. Transfer to a plate.

### 3. Bloom the vegetarian curry paste

Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining 1 tbsp coconut oil. Add the vegetarian/vegan Thai red curry paste. Cook, stirring, until fragrant and the oil takes on red color, about 1 to 2 minutes. Do not use a paste that lists shrimp paste or fish sauce.

### 4. Simmer kabocha in coconut curry

Stir in the coconut milk, water, brown sugar, and remaining 1/2 tsp kosher salt. Add the kabocha chunks. Bring to a simmer. Cover and cook until kabocha is tender when pierced, about 12 to 15 minutes. No fish sauce at any point.

### 5. Add pepper, tofu, spinach

Stir in the red bell pepper and the browned tofu. Simmer uncovered 3 to 4 minutes until the pepper softens slightly but stays bright. Fold in the baby spinach until wilted, about 1 minute. Stir in the lime juice. Taste for salt — adjust with salt only, never fish sauce.

### 6. Finish and serve

Take off the heat. Fold in most of the Thai basil leaves. Serve the curry over jasmine rice. Garnish with remaining basil and lime wedges. Each serving is curry with tofu and kabocha over rice. kitchen_tested is false.
