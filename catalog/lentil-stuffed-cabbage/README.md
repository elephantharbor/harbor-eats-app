## Steps

### 1. Blanch the cabbage

Bring a large pot of tap water to a boil with 1 tsp of the kosher salt. This blanch pot is not the measured water ingredient; that measured water is only for the lentils and the rice. The teaspoon of kosher salt is drained away and is not eaten. Do not drop it below 1 tsp when scaling. Core the green cabbage, lower the head in, and boil about 8 minutes, until 8 large outer leaves peel off without tearing. Rinse the leaves in cold water, pat them dry, and shave the thick part of each rib flat. Chop the leftover inner head. You need 8 leaves, not 12.

### 2. Simmer the lentils

Rinse the brown lentils. Simmer them in 4 cups of the water for 18 minutes, until tender with a bite in the center. If they are mush now, the rolls split later. Drain, and reserve 1/2 cup of that cooking water for the sauce. Discard the rest of the lentil water.

### 3. Parcook the rice

In a separate pot, simmer the long-grain white rice in the remaining 2 cups of water for 8 minutes. It should be half-cooked, with a white core. Drain and discard that water. The rice finishes in the oven.

### 4. Mix the filling

Heat a skillet over medium. Add 1 tbsp of the olive oil and 1 diced yellow onion. Cook 5 minutes. Add 2 cloves of the garlic, the sweet paprika, 1.5 tsp of the kosher salt, and the black pepper. Stir 1 minute. Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the brown lentils, the long-grain white rice, and 2 tbsp of the fresh parsley. The filling should be moist, not soupy. If it is wet enough to pour, the rolls will burst.

### 5. Make the sauce

In a saucepan over medium, heat the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil. Add the second diced yellow onion and cook 6 minutes. Add the remaining 2 cloves of garlic, the crushed tomatoes, the reserved 1/2 cup lentil water, the red wine vinegar, the brown sugar, the remaining 1 tsp kosher salt, and the chopped inner green cabbage. Simmer 10 minutes. The sauce should pour. If it is a paste, do not add new water; you have lost the reserved lentil water and this batch is off the recipe.

### 6. Roll

Lay one green cabbage leaf flat. Spoon about 1/2 cup of filling on the rib end. Fold the sides in and roll it closed. Repeat for all 8 leaves. Pack them seam side down in a baking dish. Pour the sauce over them, including the crushed tomatoes. Cover.

### 7. Bake

Start the oven at 375 F while rolling. When at temperature, bake 40 minutes covered, then 10 minutes uncovered, until a green cabbage leaf edge shows browned spots and the center of a roll is hot and steaming. The published clock is sequential: it does not subtract an overlap if you own two burners. No sour cream, no cheese, and no bread.

### 8. Serve

Serve two rolls per person with sauce. Sprinkle the remaining 1 tbsp fresh parsley. Do not add a dairy finish.

## Scaling for 1, 2, and 4

Ratios from a base of 4: 1 serving x0.25, 2 servings x0.5, 4 servings x1. Floors below override the ratio.

**1 serving.** Two large leaves, not a fraction of a leaf. Buy a small head; the unused leaves are not a second recipe. Filling: 1/4 cup dry brown lentils and 2 tbsp dry long-grain white rice. Sauce floor: 3/4 cup crushed tomatoes, not a strict quarter of the 28 oz can, or the dish bakes dry. Use 1 small yellow onion, half diced in the filling and half in the sauce. One garlic clove in the filling and one in the sauce. Blanch salt stays 1 tsp even for two leaves. Oven stays 375 F. Bake time stays about 40 minutes covered and 10 uncovered. Two rolls do not finish in 15 minutes.

**2 servings.** Four leaves. Half the lentils (1/2 cup) and rice (1/4 cup). Use half the can of crushed tomatoes, 14 oz, in a smaller dish, not a quarter can. One yellow onion split between filling and sauce. Halve the oil, paprika, pepper, parsley, vinegar, and sugar. Food salt halves; blanch salt stays 1 tsp. Same oven and the same lentil texture rule.

**4 servings.** As written. Eight leaves, two rolls per person, one 28 oz can, 1 cup dry lentils, 1/2 cup dry rice.

Do not scale: oven temperature; blanch-water salt below 1 tsp; the rule to stop the lentils while they still have a bite; covered-then-uncovered bake order.
