# Image / Plating Brief — Lentil-Stuffed Cabbage with Tomato Sauce

Source (read-only): certified package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`. No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

Cabbage leaves rolled around a brown-lentil and parcooked long-grain white rice filling, baked in a crushed-tomato sauce, finished with parsley. A serving is two rolls with sauce. The rolls are the hero, not a bowl of sauce.

## Vessel

The rolls are packed seam side down in a baking dish and baked, then served as two rolls per person with sauce. The recipe does not say to unmold them onto a special plate. Show the rolls in sauce. A baking dish is faithful to the bake step; a plate with exactly two rolls is faithful to the serve step. See ambiguity.

## Camera angle

Slightly elevated 3/4, the default for a baked dish, close enough to see leaf edges and sauce. Not a overhead shot so tight that the rolls look like a red casserole with no leaf structure.

## Required visible components

- Green cabbage rolls: 8 large outer leaves in the recipe, seam side down, rib shaved flat. After baking, a leaf edge shows browned spots. A serving is two rolls.
- Tomato sauce poured over the rolls: canned crushed tomatoes, diced yellow onion, minced garlic, chopped inner cabbage, reserved lentil cooking water, red wine vinegar, brown sugar, salt. Sauce should look pourable, with visible cabbage and onion, not a thick paste and not a cheese-topped pasta sauce.
- Filling is brown lentils (dry, simmered until tender with a bite), half-cooked long-grain white rice finished in the oven, onion, garlic, sweet paprika, salt, pepper, and parsley. The rolls are served closed. The filling is not required to be exposed. See ambiguity.
- Remaining chopped parsley (1 tbsp) sprinkled on at the end.

## Texture cues

Soft cabbage leaves with a few browned edges, not raw crunch and not burnt black. Sauce looks simmered and spoonable. If any filling is visible, lentils should look intact (not red lentils, not mush) and rice should look like separate white grains, not a paste.

## Sauce/component placement

Sauce covers and surrounds the rolls, including the crushed tomatoes. Parsley is sprinkled on top after baking. Do not add a cream drizzle, cheese layer, or breadcrumb crust. Paprika is in the filling, not a dusted rim.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped fresh parsley: 2 tbsp is mixed into the filling, 1 tbsp is sprinkled on top at the end. The visible garnish is that final tablespoon. Nothing else.

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional and small, outside the center crop: a parsley sprig. Do not surround the dish with raw lentils, a rice bowl, or a sour-cream crock. Those would read as extra servings.

## Natural color opportunities

Red tomato sauce, green cabbage, browned leaf edges, green parsley. Sweet paprika may warm the filling if it is exposed, but do not paint the top with paprika. Do not add dill, sour cream, or lemon.

## Forbidden elements (ingredients/sides/techniques NOT in the recipe)

Sour cream, yogurt, cheese, bread, meat, bacon, rice pilaf on the side, red lentils, canned lentils, a jarred pasta sauce with herbs that are not parsley, breadcrumbs, a dairy finish, lemon, dill, or caraway. The recipe states no sour cream, no cheese, and no bread, and says not to add a dairy finish.

## Crop strategy (1200x900 master, 640x480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Master 1200×900 and 640×480 card are one 4:3 frame. Keep at least two rolls and sauce in the center so the mobile square recommendation-card crop (1:1 `object-fit: cover`, about 12.5% off each side of the 4:3 frame; tablet cards stay 4:3) still shows cabbage leaf, red sauce, and parsley. Do not leave the rolls at the frame edge with only sauce in the middle.

## Ambiguity (not resolved; recipe not edited)

Two image-changing gaps are in the recipe and are left open. (1) Bake vessel versus plated serving: the rolls are packed in a baking dish, and the serve step is two rolls per person with sauce. Showing the full dish of eight versus a plate of two changes the image. (2) The lentil-rice filling is inside closed rolls. The serve step does not cut a roll open. Cutting one to prove the filling would change the image and is not instructed; leaving them closed hides the ingredient that defines the dish.
