# Image / Plating Brief — Lemon Pea Risotto

Source (read-only): wave-10 package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`.

## Hero subject

Creamy lemon pea risotto on a plate or shallow bowl: Arborio rice with bright green peas, lemon brightness, Parmesan and butter richness, parsley flecks. Spreads slowly — not a stiff rice cake, not soup.

## Vessel

Shallow bowl or wide-rimmed plate, warm off-white stoneware. Risotto occupies most of the useful frame.

## Camera angle

Higher 3/4 / near-overhead — shows pea scatter and creamy rice texture. Slight angle for depth.

## Required visible components

- Creamy Arborio risotto body (starch sheen from rice + butter + Parmesan — not a cream puddle on top)
- Bright green peas distributed through, not a frozen block
- Fine Parmesan presence (grated, melted in — not giant cold shards only)
- Lemon zest flecks or lemon brightness without a huge lemon wedge dominating
- Chopped parsley

## Texture cues

Creamy/starchy risotto, tender peas, soft rice grains still distinct (al dente suggestion).

## Sauce/component placement

No separate gravy boat. No cashew cream drizzle look. No grilled chicken on top.

## Legitimate garnish

Parsley; lemon zest as in recipe. Optional environmental lemon half outside crop.

## Environmental styling

Optional lemon half or parsley sprig outside center crop. No bread basket required. No wine bottle label branding.

## Natural color opportunities

Green peas, pale creamy rice, parsley green, lemon yellow accents. Do not invent roasted red pepper or shrimp for color.

## Forbidden elements

Cashew cream, chicken, shrimp, mushrooms-as-primary (not in recipe), stiff molded rice tower, excessive cheese crust, text/logo/watermark, hands/faces.

## Crop strategy

Peas and creamy rice must remain obvious after square center crop. Avoid empty plate rim dominating.

## Ambiguity

Wine is cooked into the rice — do not show a large wine pour as a plated component.
