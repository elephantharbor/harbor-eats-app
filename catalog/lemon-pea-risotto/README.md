## Steps

### 1. Heat the stock and prep

Bring the vegetable stock to a bare simmer in a saucepan and keep it hot over low heat for the whole cook. Finely dice the shallot. Mince the garlic. Finely grate the vegetarian Parmesan. Zest and juice the lemon. Chop the parsley. Measure the Arborio rice, frozen peas, wine, butter, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Do not rinse the rice.

### 2. Soft the shallot and toast the rice

In a wide heavy pot or deep skillet, melt 1 tbsp of the unsalted butter with the olive oil over medium heat. Add the shallot and 1/4 tsp of the kosher salt. Cook until soft, about 3 minutes, without browning. Add the garlic and stir 30 seconds. Add the Arborio rice and stir until the edges look translucent, about 2 minutes. This toast is part of the risotto method, not a pilaf rinse.

### 3. Deglaze with wine

Pour in the dry white wine. Stir until it is mostly absorbed, about 2 minutes. Do not add cream, milk, or cashew cream at any point; dairy finish is butter and Parmesan only.

### 4. Ladle in hot stock

Add one ladle of hot vegetable stock (about 3/4 cup). Stir often until the liquid is mostly absorbed. Continue adding stock one ladle at a time, stirring often, for about 18 to 20 minutes total ladle time. The rice should be tender with a slight bite (al dente) and the pot should look creamy from the rice starch, not soupy and not dry/stuck. You may not need every last drop of the 5 cups; stop when the texture is right. kitchen_tested is false: 18 to 20 minutes is the expected agitation window.

### 5. Finish with peas, lemon, butter, and Parmesan

Stir in the frozen peas and cook 2 minutes until hot and bright green. Take the pot off the heat. Stir in the remaining 2 tbsp unsalted butter, the grated vegetarian Parmesan, the lemon zest, the lemon juice, the black pepper, and enough of the remaining kosher salt to taste. Fold in the parsley. The risotto should slowly spread on a plate, not stand in a stiff mound and not pour like soup. Serve immediately. No grilled chicken on top. No cream splash beyond the butter and cheese already listed.
