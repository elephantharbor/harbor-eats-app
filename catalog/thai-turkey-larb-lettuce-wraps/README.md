## Steps

### 1. Prep the herbs, aromatics, and wraps

Thinly slice the shallots and scallions. Pick and tear the mint; chop the cilantro. Separate, wash, and spin dry the lettuce leaves. Slice the cucumber. Juice 2 limes to get 1/4 cup and cut the third into wedges. About 10 minutes.

_Clock: prep 10 min_

### 2. Toast and grind the rice

Put the 2 tbsp raw jasmine rice in a dry 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Toast, shaking and stirring constantly, until the grains are deep golden and smell like popcorn, about 5 minutes. Tip into a mortar or spice grinder and grind to coarse sand (about 1 minute). This is the toasted rice powder. About 6 minutes.

_Clock: cook 6 min_

### 3. Cook the turkey

Return the same skillet to medium-high heat with the 1 tbsp oil. Add the turkey and the 3 tbsp water and break it into small crumbles with a spatula. Cook, stirring and breaking it up, until no pink remains, the liquid has evaporated, and a few bits are lightly browned, 6 to 8 minutes (165 F). About 8 minutes.

_Clock: cook 8 min_

### 4. Dress the larb

Take the skillet off the heat and let it cool for 1 minute. Stir in the 3 tbsp fish sauce, the 1/4 cup lime juice, the 1 tsp sugar, and the chile flakes. Fold in the shallots, scallions, two-thirds of the toasted rice powder, and most of the mint and cilantro. Taste: it should be sharp, salty, and spicy; add a squeeze more lime or a dash of fish sauce if needed. About 3 minutes.

_Clock: cook 3 min_

### 5. Serve as wraps

Pile the larb into a serving bowl or onto a platter. Scatter the remaining herbs and toasted rice powder on top. Arrange the lettuce cups, cucumber slices, and lime wedges alongside. To eat, spoon larb into a lettuce leaf, add cucumber, squeeze lime, and fold. About 2 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 2 min_
