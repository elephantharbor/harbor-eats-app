# Image / Plating Brief — Skillet Shakshuka

Source (read-only): certified package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`. No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

Eggs set in a 12-inch skillet of tomato and red pepper sauce. The recipe says to serve from the skillet. The skillet is the meal: sauce, peppers, and eggs, finished with parsley. Written egg target is soft glossy yolks and fully set whites.

## Vessel

The 12-inch skillet with the sauce and eggs still in it. Not a plated stack of eggs, not a bowl of sauce, not a baking dish. No bread on the side.

## Camera angle

Higher 3/4, enough overhead to count eggs in wells and see red pepper strips, with enough angle to show sauce thickness and yolk dome. Not a side shot that hides the eggs. Not a single-egg macro that drops the pepper sauce.

## Required visible components

- Tomato-pepper sauce in the skillet: olive oil, softened diced yellow onion, sliced red bell pepper (stemmed and seeded; the recipe says not to char them), minced garlic, sweet paprika, ground cumin, ground coriander, one 28 oz can of crushed tomatoes, 1/2 cup water, sugar, salt, and black pepper. Sauce thickened so a spoon leaves a well. It is made in this skillet, not a jar of shakshuka sauce.
- Eight large eggs, one per well, two per serving. Whites fully set, no liquid white at the edge. Yolks soft and glossy. Eggs are not flipped.
- Chopped fresh parsley (1/4 cup packed) scattered over the sauce and eggs after the skillet comes off the heat.

## Texture cues

Thick, spoonable tomato sauce, not watery and not baked-on dry. Pepper strips slumped and soft, still identifiable, not blackened. Egg whites set and opaque. Yolks rounded, soft, and glossy, not chalky and not broken into a scramble. Parsley fresh, not fried.

## Sauce/component placement

Eggs sit in wells in the sauce, not on top of a dry pepper hash. Parsley is scattered over sauce and eggs. No oil drizzle beyond the olive oil already cooked into the sauce. No cheese, yogurt, butter, cream, or second sauce. Spices are stirred into the sauce; do not show them as separate piles.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped fresh parsley scattered at the end. Paprika, cumin, and coriander are ground spices in the sauce, not a garnish sprinkle. No other finish is listed.

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional and small, outside the skillet rim and outside the center crop: a parsley sprig or a piece of red bell pepper. Do not add eggshells as clutter, a bread board, or a spice-jar lineup that competes with the skillet.

## Natural color opportunities

Red tomato sauce, red pepper strips, orange-red paprika in the sauce, green parsley, bright yolks against set whites. Do not add feta, olives, cilantro, or chili crisp for contrast.

## Forbidden elements (ingredients/sides/techniques NOT in the recipe)

Cheese, feta, yogurt, labneh, butter, cream, bread, pita, a second sauce, cilantro, olives, harissa paste, a jarred sauce look with unnamed toppings, charred pepper strips, flipped or scrambled eggs, firm chalky yolks as the target, spinach, sausage, or an oven-baked cheese crust. The recipe says not to add cheese, yogurt, butter, cream, or bread, and says there is no second sauce.

## Crop strategy (1200x900 master, 640x480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Master 1200×900 and 640×480 card are the same 4:3 frame. Fill the frame with the skillet. Keep several eggs and pepper strips in the center. The mobile recommendation card is a square center cover-crop (about 12.5% off each side of the 4:3 frame); tablet cards stay 4:3. After that crop the image must still read as eggs in red pepper-tomato sauce with parsley, not as a plain tomato soup. Do not place the only parsley at the outer rim.

## Ambiguity (not resolved; recipe not edited)

The written target is soft yolks. The same step allows about 2 extra covered minutes for firm yolks and says that is not the written target. An image of firm yolks would depict a branch the recipe itself sets aside. Also, the skillet holds 8 eggs while a serving is 2 eggs with sauce. The serve line says to serve from the skillet, so the full skillet is the instructed presentation; cropping the photo down to two eggs is not required and would change how much of the dish is visible. This brief keeps the full skillet and the soft-yolk target.
