## Steps

### 1. Prep

Peel the parsnips and cut into 1-inch pieces. Pull the small side muscle off each scallop, then lay the scallops on a paper-towel-lined plate, pat the tops very dry, and refrigerate uncovered. Pick and dry the sage leaves. Halve the lemon.

### 2. Simmer the parsnips

Put the parsnips, milk, water, and 1 tsp salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a bare simmer over medium, about 5 minutes (watch that the milk does not boil over), then simmer gently until very tender, about 15 minutes.

### 3. Fry the sage in brown butter

While the parsnips simmer, melt 4 tbsp butter in a small skillet over medium. Once it foams, add the sage leaves in a single layer and fry until crisp and the butter turns nutty brown, 2 to 3 minutes. Lift the leaves onto a paper towel and sprinkle lightly with salt; pour the brown butter into a small bowl. Takes 4 to 5 minutes, inside the parsnip simmer.

### 4. Blend the purée

With a slotted spoon, move the parsnips to a blender. Add 2 tbsp butter and about 1/2 cup of the cooking liquid and blend until completely silky, 2 to 3 minutes, adding more liquid a splash at a time until it is soft and spoonable. Taste for salt. Return to the saucepan, cover, and keep warm on the lowest heat.

### 5. Sear the scallops

Heat a 12-inch heavy skillet (cast iron or stainless) over high until very hot, about 2 minutes. Season the scallops with 1/2 tsp salt and the pepper. Add 1 tbsp oil and half the scallops, flat side down, spaced apart. Sear without moving until a deep golden-brown crust forms, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 minute more, until just opaque at the edges and still translucent in the center (120 to 125 F). For fully cooked scallops (recommended for young children, pregnant, older, or immunocompromised diners), cook 1 to 2 minutes longer on the second side until opaque throughout (145 F). Move to a plate and repeat with the remaining oil and scallops.

### 6. Dress the salad and plate

Toss the arugula with the olive oil, a squeeze from half the lemon, and a pinch of salt. Swoosh a generous spoonful of purée on each plate, set 4 scallops crust side up on it, spoon over the sage brown butter, and top with the crispy sage. Add a small mound of arugula and a lemon wedge cut from the remaining half. Serve right away. kitchen_tested is false.
