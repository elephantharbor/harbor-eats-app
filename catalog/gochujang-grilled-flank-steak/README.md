## Steps

### 1. Mix the marinade

In a bowl, stir the gochujang, soy sauce, 1 tbsp of the rice vinegar, 3 tsp of the toasted sesame oil, brown sugar, garlic, fresh ginger, and black pepper into a thick paste. Mince 2 of the scallions and stir them in. This marinade cooks on the meat. Do not serve any of it raw.

### 2. Marinate the steak

Pat the flank steak dry and coat both sides with all of the marinade. Leave it at room temperature for 15 minutes. Do not add kosher salt to the steak; the soy sauce is already salty.

### 3. Make the salad

While the steak marinates, thinly slice the cucumber and slice the remaining 2 scallions. Toss them with the remaining 1 tbsp rice vinegar, the remaining 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, the kosher salt, and the sesame seeds.

### 4. Heat the grill

After the 15 minutes, heat a gas or charcoal grill for direct high heat, about 450–500°F. Give it about 10 minutes. When the grates are hot, brush them with the canola oil. This timed method does not overlap preheat with the marinade.

### 5. Grill

Lay the flank steak on the oiled grates. Close the lid on a gas grill, or grill over direct coals with the vents open. Cook 4 to 5 minutes per side. It is done at 130–135°F in the thickest center for medium-rare. Do not press the steak. If the piece is thicker than 1 inch, add about 1 minute per side and trust the thermometer.

### 6. Rest and slice

Move the flank steak to a board and rest it for 5 minutes. Slice it thin, against the grain: cut across the long muscle lines, not parallel to them. Discard any marinade left in the bowl.

### 7. Plate

Serve the slices with the cucumber and scallions beside them. This plate has no rice and no extra sauce.

## Scaling for 1, 2, and 4

Ratios from a base of 4: 1 serving ×0.25, 2 servings ×0.5, 4 servings ×1.

**1 serving.** Buy about 6 oz flank steak, not a tiny scrap that will fall through the grates. Round the marinade down but keep enough to coat: about 2 tsp gochujang, 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce, 3/4 tsp rice vinegar in the marinade and 3/4 tsp in the salad, 3/4 tsp toasted sesame oil in the marinade and 1/4 tsp in the salad, 3/4 tsp brown sugar, 1 garlic clove, 1 tsp grated fresh ginger. Use 1/4 of the cucumber and 1 scallion. Grill temperature stays 450–500°F. A smaller piece of the same thickness may finish about 1 minute sooner per side; use the thermometer.

**2 servings.** Halve every quantity. Two garlic cloves is the practical round of 1.5. Grill temperature and target doneness do not change. A half-width steak still needs the 5-minute rest.

**4 servings.** As written. 1.5 lb is about 6 oz raw per person.

Do not scale: grill temperature; internal temperature; rest time.
