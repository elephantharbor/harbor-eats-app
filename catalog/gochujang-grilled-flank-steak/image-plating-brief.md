# Image / Plating Brief — Gochujang Grilled Flank Steak

Source (read-only): certified package recipe only. `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version`. No recipe text was changed for this brief.

## Hero subject

Thin slices of grilled flank steak (1.5 lb, about 3/4 inch thick before slicing), cut against the grain, medium-rare in the center (130–135°F after a 5-minute rest). The steak is the dominant subject. The cucumber-scallion salad sits beside it, not under it and not as a bowl base.

## Vessel

Dinner plate. The recipe says to serve the slices with the salad beside them, and it says this plate has no rice. Not a rice bowl, not a grill platter of an unsliced steak, not the mixing bowl the marinade was made in.

## Camera angle

Slightly elevated 3/4. Close enough that the cut face of several slices and the salad are both readable. Do not use a top-down documentation shot that hides the pink interior, and do not shoot only the uncut crust.

## Required visible components

- Flank steak sliced thin across the long muscle lines, not left whole and not cut with the grain into long strings.
- Medium-rare interior: pink center, not well-done gray and not raw.
- Surface from the gochujang marinade that cooked on the meat: dark red-brown chili paste, with grill marks from direct high heat (about 450–500°F, 4–5 minutes per side).
- Scallion-cucumber salad beside the meat: thinly sliced cucumber, sliced scallions (the two not minced into the marinade), rice vinegar and toasted sesame oil sheen, and sesame seeds.
- Sesame seeds are a salad garnish, not a heavy crust on the steak.

## Texture cues

Seared, glazed flank with distinct grill marks; moist medium-rare slices, not dry shoe leather. Marinade reads as a thick cooked paste, not a watery jus. Cucumber looks crisp and fresh next to the meat. Salad looks lightly dressed, not pickled in a pool.

## Sauce/component placement

The marinade is the only coating, and it cooks on the meat. Do not serve any of it raw, and do not show leftover marinade from the bowl. The recipe says to discard marinade left in the bowl and that this plate has no extra sauce. No drizzle added after slicing. The salad is tossed and placed beside the slices, not poured over them.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Sesame seeds (1 tsp), tossed with the salad. Scallions appear twice in the recipe: two minced into the marinade (not a visible raw garnish) and two sliced in the salad. No other finish is listed.

## Environmental styling (recipe-corresponding props only; optional)

Optional and small, outside the plate, not a second serving: a few sesame seeds, a scallion, or a short piece of cucumber. Do not place a gochujang tub, soy bottle, or ginger knob so that it reads as a condiment course. Props must not enter the center crop.

## Natural color opportunities

Dark red-brown gochujang crust, pink meat, pale green cucumber, bright green scallion, off-white sesame. Those are the only color breaks. Do not add lime, kimchi, perilla, or lettuce to buy color.

## Forbidden elements (ingredients/sides/techniques NOT in the recipe)

Rice, noodles, lettuce, kimchi, perilla or shiso, cilantro, lime, raw marinade as a dipping sauce, a second sauce, butter, cheese, sesame leaves, grilled scallions as a separate side, a whole unsliced flank as the only subject, kosher salt on the steak (salt is for the salad only; soy sauce seasons the meat), charcoal-black burnt crust, or well-done gray meat.

## Crop strategy (1200x900 master, 640x480 card, FlavorWeave recommendation-card crop)

Master is 1200×900 WebP (4:3). The 640×480 card is the same 4:3 frame, scaled, not a different composition. Keep steak slices and the salad inside the central 75% of the width. The FlavorWeave recommendation card (mobile `.option-card__media`) is a 1:1 `object-fit: cover` center crop of that 4:3 frame, which cuts about 12.5% off each side; tablet cards stay 4:3. Do not park the salad on the far left or right edge. The pink interior and cucumber must survive the square center crop.
