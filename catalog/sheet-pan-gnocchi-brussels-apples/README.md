## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven and prep

Set racks in the upper-middle and lower-middle positions and heat the oven to 450°F. The oven preheats during this prep and the pan loading in step 2 (about 15 minutes total; preheat overlap stated, so it is not added to cook time). Trim and halve the Brussels sprouts. Core the apples and cut into 3/4-inch wedges. Cut the red onion into 1-inch wedges through the root. Strip the thyme leaves. In a small bowl whisk the maple syrup, cider vinegar, Dijon, 1 tsp thyme, and 1/4 tsp salt; this is the glaze. Open the gnocchi and break apart any pieces stuck together. HARD CONTRACT: gnocchi label shows no milk, whey, cheese, butter, or egg; no Parmesan or butter anywhere in this recipe.

### 2. Load two sheet pans

Pan 1: toss the sprouts and onion with 2 tbsp olive oil, 3/4 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper; turn the sprouts cut-side down. Pan 2: toss the uncooked gnocchi and apple wedges with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp pepper, and 2 tsp thyme; spread in a single layer with a little space between pieces so they crisp instead of steam.

### 3. Roast

Roast the gnocchi pan on the upper rack and the sprout pan on the lower rack for 15 minutes.

### 4. Flip, swap, and finish roasting

With a thin metal spatula, flip the gnocchi and apples; toss the sprouts and onion. Swap rack positions. Roast 10 minutes more, until the gnocchi are puffed, golden, and crisp at the edges, the apples are browned at the edges but still hold their shape, and the sprouts are deeply browned on the cut sides and tender when pierced.

### 5. Glaze

Drizzle the glaze over both pans, about half on each, and toss. Return to the oven for 3 minutes, until the glaze bubbles and turns sticky.

### 6. Combine and serve

Slide the sprouts and onion onto the gnocchi pan (or a warm platter) and toss gently. Taste; add a pinch of salt if needed. Serve right away while the gnocchi are crisp. No Parmesan, no butter, no nuts. kitchen_tested is false.
