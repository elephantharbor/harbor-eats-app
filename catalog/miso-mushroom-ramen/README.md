## Steps

### 1. Soak the dried shiitake and prep

Rinse the dried shiitake and drop them into 8 cups of hot tap water in a large bowl or pitcher to soak while you prep (they soften and start the broth). Fill a separate large pot with water for the noodles, cover, and set it over high heat. Remove the fresh shiitake stems and slice the caps 1/4 inch thick. Halve and rinse the bok choy. Thaw the corn in a sieve under warm water. Grate the garlic and ginger. Slice the scallions, keeping whites and greens separate. Cut the nori into 8 rectangles. In a small bowl stir the miso, tahini, and 2 tbsp soy sauce into a smooth paste (the tare). HARD CONTRACT: plain miso (no bonito/dashi added), egg-free noodles, no butter, no egg topping, no pork or chicken broth.

### 2. Sear the mushrooms

Heat the neutral oil in a 5- to 6-quart pot over medium-high. Add the sliced fresh shiitake in an even layer with a pinch of salt and cook undisturbed 3 minutes, then stir occasionally until browned with crisp edges, 3 to 4 minutes more. Add 1 tbsp soy sauce and toss 30 seconds until absorbed and glossy. Transfer to a plate; this is the topping.

### 3. Build the broth

Lower the heat to medium. Add 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, the garlic, ginger, and scallion whites; stir 1 minute until fragrant without browning. Pour in the soaked dried shiitake and their soaking water, leaving any grit in the bottom of the bowl. Bring to a simmer over high, about 8 minutes, then reduce to a gentle simmer, partly covered, for 8 minutes. Lift out the rehydrated shiitake, slice thinly, and return them to the pot.

### 4. Cook the bok choy, corn, and noodles

Add the bok choy and corn to the simmering broth and cook until the bok choy stems are crisp-tender, 2 to 3 minutes; lift the bok choy out with tongs. At the same time, cook the noodles in the boiling noodle pot according to the package (fresh about 2 to 3 minutes, dried about 4 to 5) until just tender; drain well and shake dry. Do not rinse.

### 5. Season the broth

Turn the broth to low; it should not boil from here on. Ladle about 1 cup hot broth into the miso-tahini tare, whisk smooth, and stir it back into the pot. Taste; add a pinch of salt only if needed.

### 6. Assemble the bowls

Divide the noodles among 4 deep bowls and ladle about 1 1/2 cups broth with corn and sliced shiitake over each. Arrange the seared shiitake and two bok choy halves on top, tuck 2 nori rectangles at the rim, and finish with scallion greens, sesame seeds, and a few drops of the remaining 1 tsp toasted sesame oil. Serve immediately. No egg, no butter, no chashu. kitchen_tested is false.
