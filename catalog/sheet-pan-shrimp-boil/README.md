# Sheet-Pan Shrimp Boil with Corn and Andouille

All the flavor of a Lowcountry boil without the boil: Old Bay–seasoned baby potatoes, red onion, corn rounds, and smoky andouille roast until browned, then garlic-Old Bay butter shrimp go on for the last few minutes. Finished with lemon and parsley, straight from the pans.

## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven and prep

Set racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven and heat it to 425 F; it preheats while you prep (about 15 minutes). Halve the potatoes and cut the onion into wedges. Husk the corn and cut each ear into 3 rounds with a heavy knife (rock the blade through the cob). Slice the andouille. Pat the shrimp very dry. Mince the garlic, chop the parsley, halve one lemon, and cut the other into wedges. About 15 minutes.

_Clock: prep 15 min (oven preheat to 425 F runs inside this block)_

### 2. Roast the potatoes and onion

On two rimmed half-sheet pans, toss the potatoes and onion with 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp Old Bay, and the 1/2 tsp kosher salt. Spread out, potatoes cut side down. Roast 15 minutes. About 15 minutes.

_Clock: cook 15 min_

### 3. Add the corn and andouille

Toss the corn rounds with the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil and 1 tsp Old Bay. Add the corn and andouille to the pans, nestling them among the potatoes, then switch the pans between racks. Roast 10 minutes, until the potatoes are nearly tender and browned underneath and the sausage is sizzling. During the last 2 minutes, put the butter and garlic in a large microwave-safe bowl and microwave 45 to 60 seconds until melted and sizzling; stir in 1 tbsp Old Bay and add the shrimp, tossing to coat (overlaps the roast; adds no time). About 10 minutes.

_Clock: cook 10 min (shrimp-butter toss overlaps the last 2 minutes of this roast)_

### 4. Roast the shrimp

Spoon the buttered shrimp onto the pans in a single layer among the vegetables and drizzle any garlic butter left in the bowl over the corn. Roast 6 to 7 minutes, until the shrimp are pink, opaque, and curled into loose C shapes (145 F) and the potatoes are fork-tender. About 7 minutes.

_Clock: cook 7 min_

### 5. Finish with lemon and parsley

Squeeze the halved lemon over both pans, scatter the parsley, and tuck in the lemon wedges. Serve straight from the pans or pile everything onto a platter. About 3 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 3 min_
