## Steps

### 1. Soak the rice and steep the saffron

Rinse the basmati in several changes of cool water until nearly clear, then cover with fresh water and soak 20 minutes (the soak runs through all of prep). Warm 3 tbsp water until hot but not boiling, crumble in the saffron, and set aside to steep.

### 2. Prep

Halve and very thinly slice the onions. Cut the cauliflower into 1-inch florets and dice the carrots. Grate the garlic and ginger; slit the serrano. Pick the mint. Juice one lemon (3 tbsp) and cut the other into wedges. Fill a large pot with 3 quarts water and 2 tbsp salt and cover it. HARD CONTRACT: oil not ghee; no yogurt, cream, butter, or paneer; cashews are the only nut.

### 3. Toast the cashews

Heat the 1/2 cup oil in a heavy 5- to 6-quart Dutch oven over medium. Add the cashews and stir constantly until light golden, 2 to 3 minutes; lift out with a slotted spoon onto paper towels. Set the covered pot of salted water over high heat now so it boils by step 6.

### 4. Fry the onions

Add the onions and a pinch of salt to the hot oil. Cook over medium-high, stirring often at first and constantly once they color, until deep golden brown and crisp at the edges, 10 to 12 minutes. Lift out with the slotted spoon and spread on paper towels to crisp. Carefully pour off all but 3 tbsp oil into a heatproof cup; keep 1 tbsp of the poured-off oil for the rice.

### 5. Build the vegetable base

Over medium heat, add the garlic, ginger, and serrano to the 3 tbsp oil and stir 45 seconds. Add 2 tsp garam masala and the turmeric and stir 15 seconds. Add the cauliflower, carrots, 1 1/4 tsp salt, 2 tbsp lemon juice, and 1/2 cup water; stir, cover, and cook 6 minutes, until the cauliflower is just tender at the edges but still firm (it finishes during the steam). Stir in the peas, half the fried onions, and 1/3 cup mint. Spread level and turn off the heat.

### 6. Parboil the rice

While the vegetables cook in step 5, drain the soaked rice, add it to the boiling salted water, and boil uncovered 5 to 6 minutes, until the grains are long and bend but still have a firm white core (about 70% cooked). Drain well. This runs inside step 5's 8 minutes (overlap stated).

### 7. Layer

Spoon the rice evenly over the vegetables without stirring. Drizzle the saffron water over in streaks, then the reserved 1 tbsp oil and the remaining 1 tbsp lemon juice. Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 tsp garam masala and scatter a few mint leaves and a small handful of the fried onions. Cover the pot tightly with foil, crimp the edges, and set the lid on top.

### 8. Steam (dum) and rest

Cook over medium heat 2 minutes, until you hear steam, then over the lowest heat 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest, still covered, 5 minutes. Do not open the pot during this time.

### 9. Finish and serve

Uncover: the top should show saffron-orange and white grains. Gently fold from the edges with a large spoon, or scoop deep so each portion gets vegetables and rice. Top with the remaining fried onions, the toasted cashews, and the remaining mint; serve with lemon wedges. No raita, no ghee. kitchen_tested is false.
