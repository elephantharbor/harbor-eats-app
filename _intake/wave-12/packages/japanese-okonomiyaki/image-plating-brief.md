# Image / Plating Brief — Okonomiyaki (Japanese Cabbage Pancakes)

Author: Juniper (Harbor Eats - Catalog Curator), 2026-10-08 (America/Chicago). Written before generation. Source: this package's `v1.json` `recipe_version` (exact recipe), plus the FlavorWeave house style (`image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`; house-quality reference = Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for quality/light/appeal, not composition).

## Hero subject

One thick, round, golden-crisp cabbage okonomiyaki, glazed with glossy dark-brown okonomi sauce to the edges, striped with a fine white mayo lattice, topped with a tumble of pale tan bonito flakes and bright scallion greens; one wedge cut and slightly pulled away to reveal the shredded cabbage interior.

## Vessel

Flat matte off-white or light-grey ceramic plate, slightly larger than the pancake. A second dressed pancake may sit soft-focus behind on another plate (4 servings, 4 pancakes).

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 35–45 degrees) to show the 3/4-inch thickness, the crisp side edge, the cut interior, and the volume of the bonito flakes.

## Required visible components

Round pancake about 3/4 inch thick with deep-golden crisp edges; dark glossy brown sauce covering the top; thin white crosshatched mayonnaise lines; curled bonito flakes; sliced scallion greens; at the cut wedge: pale-green finely shredded cabbage bound in set egg batter, with browned shiitake slices visible on the underside.

## Texture cues

Crisp, browned edges; tender, set (not wet) interior; glossy sauce; feathery bonito flakes.

## Sauce/component placement

Sauce spread evenly to the edges (not drizzled); mayo in neat thin lattice lines; no puddles on the plate.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Bonito flakes, scallion greens. Optional aonori and pickled red ginger are recipe-listed optionals but keep them OFF the hero so it shows the default build.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a pair of plain wooden chopsticks, a few scallions or half a cabbage wedge at the edge, soft-focus second plate. No bowls of other dishes.

## Color opportunities

Golden-brown crust, mahogany sauce, white mayo lines, tan-pink bonito, green scallion, pale-green cabbage at the cut.

## Forbidden elements

Shrimp, squid, pork belly or bacon strips, any meat, noodles (no yakisoba/modanyaki layer), cheese, a fried egg on top, sesame seeds, nori strips, heavy green aonori coating, red pickled ginger on the hero, tempura bits, sushi or rice, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Crop strategy

Hero pancake centered and fully inside the middle ~70% of the width; generator output may be 16:9 and is center-cropped to 4:3. The cut wedge and bonito must read at 640×480 and in the card crop.

## Ambiguity

Shiitake is pressed into one face and ends up on the underside; it is visible at the cut edge, not on top.

## Certification targets

K fidelity (every Required visible component, nothing Forbidden), L1 technical/style (4:3 WebP 1200×900 + 640×480, crop-safe, no artifacts), L2 hero appeal (bright natural light, believable vivid color, visible texture, abundant intentional plating, strong at card scale). Accurate-but-dull fails; beautiful-but-wrong fails.
