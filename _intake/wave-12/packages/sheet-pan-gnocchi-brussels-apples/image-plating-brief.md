## Required visible components (fidelity)

- Gnocchi pillows with visible ridges and golden-brown crisp faces (not pale or boiled-looking)

- Apple wedges with red skin and browned edges, still holding shape
- Red onion wedges as soft purple petals
- Fresh thyme leaves scattered
- Light sticky glaze sheen, no pooled sauce

## Texture cues

Crisp shell on the gnocchi, charred lacy sprout leaves, tender glossy apples.

Thin sticky glaze clinging to surfaces only; no cream sauce, no pooled liquid, no drizzle lines.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Fresh thyme leaves and cracked black pepper only.

Optional and small: a couple of thyme sprigs, one whole apple or apple half, a few raw Brussels sprouts at the frame edge; neutral linen. Must not dominate or imply a side dish.
