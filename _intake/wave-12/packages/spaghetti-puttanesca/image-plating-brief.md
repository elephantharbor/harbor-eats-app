# Image / Plating Brief — Spaghetti Puttanesca

Source (read-only): this package's recipe only — `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version` (package_revision `freeze-r2`). Written before generation. Standard: `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md` — truthful enough to trust, attractive enough to choose.

## Hero subject

A generous twirled nest of spaghetti coated in chunky, brick-red puttanesca sauce, with visible olive pieces, capers, and thin golden garlic slices.

## Vessel

Wide, shallow off-white or warm-neutral stoneware pasta bowl.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 60 degrees). Pasta reads best from above, and this angle still shows the height of the twirl and the sauce clinging to the strands.

## Required visible components

- Spaghetti (long strands, twirled), fully coated, not sitting on top of sauce
- Chunky tomato sauce, brick red, glossy
- Purple-black Kalamata olive pieces
- Small green capers
- Thin pale-gold garlic slices
- Chopped flat-leaf parsley
- A few red chile flakes

## Texture cues

Glossy sauce clinging to al dente strands; rough chunks of hand-crushed tomato; wrinkled olive pieces; a thin sheen of olive oil at the edge of the sauce.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce tossed through the pasta, with a little extra chunky sauce and olives on top of the twirl. Anchovies are melted in: no whole fillets on top.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped flat-leaf parsley, a few red pepper flakes, and a thin drizzle of olive oil (all recipe-listed).

## Environmental styling

Optional, secondary: a small bunch of flat-leaf parsley and a tiny ramekin of capers, partly out of frame; a fork beside the bowl. Nothing that reads as an extra side.

## Natural color opportunities

Brick-red sauce, purple-black olives, green capers and parsley, gold garlic, red chile flecks against an off-white bowl.

## Forbidden elements

- Grated, shaved, or powdered cheese of any kind
- Butter
- Basil leaves (not in recipe)
- Whole anchovy fillets draped on top
- Shrimp or other shellfish
- Meat or meatballs
- Bread, garlic bread, or a side salad
- Lemon wedges
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered with generous side margins so a 4:3 center-crop from a wider generation keeps the full bowl. The twirl and olives sit in the middle third so they stay legible at 640x480 and in the card crop. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

Finished, tossed pasta in a bowl; not a pan shot and not sauce poured over plain pasta.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
