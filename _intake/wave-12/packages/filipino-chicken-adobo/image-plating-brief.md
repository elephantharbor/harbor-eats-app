## Hero subject

Two glossy, lacquered mahogany chicken thighs with golden seared skin, glistening with reduced soy-vinegar glaze, resting against a fluffy mound of white jasmine rice with some glaze running into it, topped with golden garlic chips and bright green bias-cut scallions.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white stoneware bowl or rimmed plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 40 degrees) to show the glaze sheen on the skin and the rice height.

## Required visible components (fidelity)

- Two bone-in skin-on thighs with deep golden-mahogany glazed skin
- Glossy dark sauce spooned over and pooling slightly
- Fluffy white jasmine rice
- Golden garlic chips
- Green bias-cut scallions
- Optionally one bay leaf on the plate

## Texture cues

Rendered, glazed skin (not crispy-fried or battered), tender meat, separate rice grains, crisp garlic chips.

## Sauce / component placement

Reduced glossy glaze, a modest pool, plausible for 1/2 cup reduced by half; not a soup.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Garlic chips and scallions; one bay leaf optional.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a whole garlic head, a few scattered whole black peppercorns, two scallions at the frame edge.

## Natural color opportunities

Mahogany chicken, white rice, golden garlic chips, bright green scallions.

## Forbidden elements

- hard-boiled eggs
- potatoes
- coconut milk or creamy sauce
- pork belly
- breaded or deep-fried chicken look
- sesame seeds
- cilantro
- red chiles
- lime
- cucumber or tomato salad
- text, logos, watermarks, packaging, hands, faces
- excessive steam

Thighs at the center-right and rice at center-left, together filling about 75% of the frame so the 4:3 crop and 640x480 card show glaze, rice, and green scallions clearly.

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography; soft directional natural-window light; vibrant but believable color; light neutral tabletop and off-white/warm-neutral ceramics; food fills most of the frame. ## Ambiguity

Braised and glazed adobo, not fried chicken and not teriyaki with sesame.
