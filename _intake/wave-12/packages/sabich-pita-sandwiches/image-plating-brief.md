# Image / Plating Brief — Sabich Pita with Crispy Eggplant and Tahini

Source (read-only): this package's recipe only — `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version` (package_revision `freeze-r1`). Written before generation. Standard: `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md` — truthful enough to trust, attractive enough to choose.

## Hero subject

Two generously stuffed pocket pitas standing upright so the layered cross-section shows: golden fried eggplant, sliced egg with yellow yolk, red-and-green chopped salad, pickles, and an ivory tahini drizzle.

## Vessel

Light off-white ceramic plate or shallow oval platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, slightly lower than for a bowl (about 30-35 degrees), so the vertical layers inside the pockets are visible.

## Required visible components

- Pocket pita (soft, lightly toasted)
- Golden-brown pan-fried eggplant rounds with crisp edges and creamy interiors
- Hard-boiled egg slices with firm yellow yolks
- Finely diced tomato-cucumber salad
- Dill pickle slices
- Chopped parsley
- Ivory tahini drizzle running over the filling

## Texture cues

Crisp, browned eggplant edges against creamy flesh; smooth set egg; juicy diced salad; glossy pourable tahini.

## Sauce / component placement

Tahini drizzled over the top of the filling, running slightly down the pita; a small bowl of extra tahini beside the plate. Hummus is inside and does not need to show.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped parsley and the tahini drizzle (recipe-listed). Amba is optional and is not shown so the image matches the base recipe.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of tahini, a halved lemon, a few Persian cucumbers or a tomato in soft focus. Must not read as an extra side.

## Natural color opportunities

Golden eggplant, yellow yolks, red tomato, green cucumber and parsley, ivory tahini, warm pita.

## Forbidden elements

- Feta, labneh, yogurt, or any cheese
- Falafel balls
- Meat, shawarma, or chicken
- Fish or shellfish
- Lettuce (not in recipe)
- French fries
- Yellow-orange amba drizzle (optional; omitted from hero)
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Both pitas within the center 70% of the width so a 4:3 center-crop keeps them whole. The cross-section must stay legible at 640x480; avoid a wide tabletop. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

Sabich: eggplant and egg in pita. Not a falafel pita and not a shawarma wrap.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
