## Hero subject

A loose fan of puffed, golden-brown pan-fried Thai fish cakes with smooth blistered surfaces and crisp, lacy edges, one cut open to show a pale tan, lightly curry-tinted (warm beige-orange, from the red curry paste) fully cooked interior, with a bowl of glistening cucumber relish and a neat mound of jasmine rice.

## Vessel

Off-white ceramic dinner plate; relish in a small pale ceramic bowl on or beside the plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 40-45 degrees) to show the domed, blistered surface and crisp edges of the cakes and the translucent relish.

## Required visible components

- 5 to 6 round, flattened pan-fried fish cakes, about 2 1/2 inches wide and 1/2 inch thick
- Smooth, puffed, golden-brown blistered pan-fried surface with crisp lacy edges and no breadcrumbs, panko, batter, or crumb coating
- Thin green-bean rings (1/8-inch slices, each a small pale-green ring showing tiny seeds) flecked through the cakes, plus fine lime-leaf threads
- One cake cut open: opaque, matte, pale tan, lightly curry-tinted (warm beige-orange, from the red curry paste) fully cooked interior with only tiny flecks of curry paste and green-bean rings; no pink, no salmon color
- Cucumber relish: thin cucumber quarter-moons, shallot slices, red chile rings in clear brine
- Fluffy white jasmine rice
- Cilantro leaves and a lime wedge

## Texture cues

Smooth, blistered, golden-brown pan-fried surface with crisp, lacy edges (bare fish paste, no crumb coating); dense, bouncy, fully cooked opaque interior (not flaky fillet, not breaded, not moist or translucent); crisp, glossy cucumber.

## Sauce / component placement

Relish in its own small bowl with a little clear brine; no separate dipping sauce.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro leaves, lime wedge (recipe-listed).

## Environmental styling

Optional: a halved lime, a few makrut lime leaves, a red chile. Subtle; must not read as an extra dish.

## Natural color opportunities

Golden-brown cake surfaces, pale tan, lightly curry-tinted (warm beige-orange, from the red curry paste) cooked interior, green-bean ring flecks, pale-green cucumber, purple-pink shallot, red chile, white rice, green cilantro.

## Forbidden elements

- Pink, salmon-colored, or translucent interior; moist, raw, or undercooked-looking centers
- Salmon or any salmon-colored fish
- Peas, edamame, or whole or long-cut green beans
- Breadcrumbs, panko, batter, or any crumb coating; pebbly, crumbly, or crusted surface
- Peanuts, cashews, or any crushed nuts
- Sweet chili sauce or dark dipping sauce
- Shrimp or other shellfish
- Uniform deep-fried spheres
- Lettuce cups
- Noodles
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces
