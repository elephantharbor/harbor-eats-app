# Image / Plating Brief — Mushroom and Hominy Pozole Rojo

Source (read-only): this package's recipe only — `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version` (package_revision `freeze-r1`). Written before generation. Standard: `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md` — truthful enough to trust, attractive enough to choose.

## Hero subject

A deep bowl of brick-red pozole broth with plump white hominy and browned mushroom quarters, crowned with a bright pile of shredded cabbage, radish coins, and diced white onion.

## Vessel

Wide, deep off-white or warm-cream ceramic soup bowl; corn tostadas on a small side plate.

## Camera angle

High three-quarter, near overhead. Soup reads from above, and this angle shows the broth color, the hominy, and the garnish pile together.

## Required visible components

- Brick-red, slightly translucent chile broth
- Plump white hominy kernels
- Deeply browned quartered cremini mushrooms
- Finely shredded pale-green cabbage
- Thin pink-and-white radish coins
- Finely diced raw white onion
- Lime wedge
- Corn tostadas beside the bowl

## Texture cues

Glossy red broth with small oil droplets; puffed, matte hominy; browned, meaty mushroom edges; crisp raw cabbage and radish; brittle golden tostadas.

## Sauce / component placement

Broth fills the bowl to about 3/4. Hominy and mushrooms break the surface. Garnish piled to one side so the red broth stays visible.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cabbage, radish, white onion, crumbled dried oregano, lime wedge (all recipe-listed).

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of extra lime wedges, two or three whole radishes, a few dried guajillo chiles. Subtle and partly out of frame.

## Natural color opportunities

Brick-red broth, white hominy, brown mushrooms, pale-green cabbage, pink radish, lime green, golden tostadas.

## Forbidden elements

- Cheese, crema, sour cream, or avocado
- Cilantro (not in recipe)
- Pork, chicken, or any meat
- Shrimp or seafood
- Tortilla chips or tortilla strips in the soup
- Beans
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered with margin on all sides; tostadas and lime at the edge are allowed to crop. The red broth plus garnish pile must read as the hero at 640x480 and in the card crop. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

This is a red (rojo) pozole with mushrooms, not a green pozole or a pork stew.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
