## Hero subject

A generous mound of fluffy long-grain basmati biryani with distinct saffron-orange and white grains, studded with golden cauliflower florets, orange carrot cubes, and green peas, crowned with dark-golden crispy fried onions, whole toasted cashews, and torn fresh mint.

## Vessel

Wide off-white or warm-cream stoneware serving platter or wide shallow bowl, with a lemon wedge or two on the side of the platter.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 50-55 degrees) to show the grain length, saffron streaks, and the topping crown.

## Required visible components (fidelity)

- Long separate basmati grains in two colors (saffron-orange and white)
- Cauliflower florets, carrot cubes, green peas

- Fresh mint leaves
- Lemon wedge

## Texture cues

Dry, separate, fluffy grains (not mushy or sticky), crisp onion strands, crunchy cashews.

## Sauce / component placement

No sauce or gravy; the vegetables are moist but there is no pooled liquid.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Fried onions, toasted cashews, fresh mint, lemon wedges only.
