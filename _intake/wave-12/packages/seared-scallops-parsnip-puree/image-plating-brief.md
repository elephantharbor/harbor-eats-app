# Image / Plating Brief — Seared Scallops with Parsnip Purée and Crispy Sage

Source (read-only): this package's recipe only — `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version` (package_revision `freeze-r2`). Written before generation. Standard: `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md` — truthful enough to trust, attractive enough to choose.

## Hero subject

Four golden-crusted seared scallops on a swoosh of silky ivory parsnip purée, spooned with brown butter and topped with crispy sage, with a small mound of lemony arugula and a lemon wedge.

## Vessel

Wide off-white or pale-gray rimmed dinner plate with generous negative space.

## Camera angle

Low-to-mid three-quarter (about 30-35 degrees) so the scallops' crust and height show along with the purée swoosh.

## Required visible components

- Four large sea scallops with a deep golden-brown seared crust on top
- Visible translucent-to-opaque scallop sides, tall and plump
- Silky ivory parsnip purée swooshed beneath
- Nutty brown butter spooned over, with small brown flecks
- Dark-green crispy fried sage leaves
- Small mound of baby arugula
- Lemon wedge

## Texture cues

Caramelized crust on the scallops, satiny purée, translucent and brittle fried sage, tender dressed arugula leaves.

## Sauce / component placement

Brown butter in small glossy pools and drips on and around the scallops; no cream sauce.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Crispy sage, arugula, and lemon (all recipe-listed). A few flakes of salt are fine.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a sage sprig and a halved lemon in soft focus; a fork and neutral linen. No wine glass dominating the frame.

## Natural color opportunities

Golden-brown crusts, ivory purée, amber butter, dark-green sage, bright-green arugula, yellow lemon.

## Forbidden elements

- Bacon or pancetta
- Pea purée or green purée
- Microgreens or edible flowers
- Shrimp, shells, or scallop shells
- Pale, steamed-looking or rubbery scallops
- Cream sauce or foam
- Black burnt butter
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Plate centered; scallops in the middle third, large enough that crusts read at 640x480. Keep the plate rim within or just touching the frame on the left and right for the 4:3 crop. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

Seared sea scallops (large, tall), not bay scallops or scallops in the shell.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
