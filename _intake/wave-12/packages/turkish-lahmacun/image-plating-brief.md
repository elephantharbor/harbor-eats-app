## Hero subject

Two thin oval lahmacun with browned, crisp, bubbled rims and a thin layer of red-brown spiced lamb pressed edge to edge; the front one heaped with sumac red-onion, tomato, and parsley salad, partly rolled to show how it's eaten; lemon wedges.

## Vessel

Large light-colored ceramic platter or a pale wood board with parchment under the flatbreads (the recipe starts each bake on parchment).

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 40–50 degrees) to show thinness, crisp rims, and the salad volume.

## Required visible components

Very thin oval flatbreads with crisp browned edges and some blistering; thin, even red-brown lamb paste with tiny flecks of parsley and pepper; herb salad: whole parsley leaves, thin magenta sumac-tinted red-onion slivers, diced red tomato; lemon wedges.

## Texture cues

Crisp, cracker-like rims with soft fold; sizzled, browned spots on the meat layer; fresh, glossy salad.

## Sauce/component placement

No sauce; lemon juice and oil glisten on the salad only.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Herb salad, sumac, Aleppo pepper flakes, lemon wedges.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of the herb salad, a lemon half, a pinch of sumac in a tiny dish. No drinks, no extra sides.

## Color opportunities

Red-brown topping, golden-brown crust, bright green parsley, magenta onion, red tomato, lemon yellow.

## Forbidden elements

Cheese or melted cheese, pizza look with thick crust, pita pockets, meatballs or chunky ground meat, yogurt or tahini sauce, cucumber, olives, eggs, pine nuts, pickles, fries, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Crop strategy

Flatbreads fill much of the frame but the rolled front piece and salad stay inside the middle ~70% of the width; generator output may be 16:9 and is center-cropped to 4:3. Must read at 640×480 and in the card crop.

## Ambiguity

Lahmacun is not pizza: there is no cheese and the meat layer is a thin paste, not crumbles.
