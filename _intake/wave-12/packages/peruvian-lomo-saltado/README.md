## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven and marinate the beef

Heat the oven to 425°F (or the temperature on your fry package, 425 to 450°F); it preheats during the 15 minutes of prep (overlap stated). Toss the beef strips with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 cloves minced garlic, the cumin, and the pepper; let it marinate while you prep.

### 2. Prep the vegetables, sauce, and rice

Cut the onion into 1/2-inch wedges and separate the layers. Cut the tomatoes into seeded wedges. Mince the remaining 3 garlic cloves. Chop the cilantro. Rinse the rice. In a small bowl stir together 2 tbsp soy sauce, the red wine vinegar, and the ají amarillo paste (the sauce).

### 3. Start the fries and rice

Spread the frozen fries in a single layer on a rimmed sheet pan and bake 22 to 25 minutes, flipping once halfway, until deep golden and crisp. At the same time bring the rice, 2 1/4 cups water, and 3/4 tsp salt to a boil in a 2-quart saucepan, cover, reduce to low, and cook 15 minutes; then keep covered off the heat. Both are hands-off; flip the fries at about minute 12.

### 4. Sear the beef

About 13 minutes after the fries go in, heat a 12-inch carbon-steel or cast-iron skillet (or wok) over high until smoking, about 2 minutes. Add 1 tbsp oil and half the beef in a single layer; sear undisturbed 1 minute, then toss 30 to 60 seconds until browned outside and still pink inside. Transfer to a plate. Repeat with 1 tbsp oil and the rest of the beef.

### 5. Char the onion and garlic

Add the last 1 tbsp oil and the onion. Stir-fry over high 90 seconds, until the edges char but the onion is still crisp. Add the garlic and toss 15 seconds.

### 6. Tomatoes, sauce, and beef

Add the tomatoes and toss 45 seconds just to warm them. Pour the sauce around the edge of the pan so it sizzles and reduces, about 20 seconds. Return the beef and its juices and toss 30 seconds. Off heat, add most of the cilantro; taste and add salt only if needed (the soy is salty).

### 7. Plate

Fold about one-third of the hot fries into the skillet so they soak up some sauce; pile the rest alongside. Pack rice into a small cup and unmold a dome onto each plate. Spoon the lomo saltado next to the rice with its juices, add the remaining fries, and scatter the remaining cilantro. Serve at once while the fries are crisp. kitchen_tested is false.
