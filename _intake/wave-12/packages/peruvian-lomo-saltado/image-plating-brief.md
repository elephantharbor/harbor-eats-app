## Hero subject

A generous heap of glossy seared sirloin strips tossed with charred red-onion petals and juicy red tomato wedges in a light amber sauce, flecked with cilantro, with golden crisp French fries tucked into and beside it, next to a neat domed mound of white rice.

## Vessel

Wide off-white stoneware dinner plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 35-40 degrees) to show the height of the stir-fry, the rice dome, and fry texture.

## Required visible components (fidelity)

- Browned sirloin strips, juicy (pink interior visible on a cut edge or two)
- Red onion wedges/petals, charred edges, still crisp
- Red tomato wedges holding shape
- Golden crisp straight-cut fries, some folded in and some alongside
- Domed white rice
- Chopped cilantro
- Light glossy amber pan sauce

## Texture cues

Seared beef, crisp fries, glossy sauce on tomatoes and onions.

## Sauce / component placement

Light glossy pan juices under the beef and catching some fries; not a thick gravy.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped cilantro only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a few cilantro sprigs and a red onion half at the frame edge.

## Natural color opportunities

Deep-brown beef, red tomato, purple-pink onion, golden fries, white rice, green cilantro.

- fried egg on top
- cheese or creamy yellow sauce (huancaína)
- green bell peppers
- broccoli
- sesame seeds
- scallions
- lime
- ketchup or mayonnaise
- potato chips
- chopsticks
- text, logos, watermarks, packaging, hands, faces
- excessive steam
