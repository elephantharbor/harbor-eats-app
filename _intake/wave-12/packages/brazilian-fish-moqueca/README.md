## Steps

### 1. Marinate the fish

Pat the halibut dry and cut into 2-inch pieces. Toss in a bowl with 2 tbsp lime juice, 2 cloves minced garlic, 1 tsp paprika, 3/4 tsp salt, and the black pepper. Let it marinate while you prep, about 15 minutes (no longer than 30, or the lime starts to firm the fish). HARD CONTRACT: white finfish only; no shrimp or any shellfish.

### 2. Prep the vegetables and rice

Slice the onion, red pepper, and yellow pepper into thin rings and the tomatoes into 1/4-inch rounds. Thinly slice the chile. Mince the remaining 3 garlic cloves. Finely chop 2 tbsp cilantro stems and pick the leaves. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. Cut the third lime into wedges. Shake and whisk the coconut milk smooth with 1/2 tsp salt.

### 3. Start the rice

In a 2-quart saucepan bring the rice, 2 1/4 cups water, and 3/4 tsp salt to a boil over high (about 4 minutes). Cover, reduce to low, and cook 15 minutes; then leave it covered off the heat until serving (at least 5 minutes). This is set-and-forget and runs during steps 4 to 7 (overlap stated).

### 4. Build the base

At the same time, heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a 12-inch braiser or wide 5-quart Dutch oven over medium. Add half the onion, half of each pepper, the 3 cloves garlic, the cilantro stems, 1 tsp paprika, and 1/2 tsp salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes. Spread in an even layer.

### 5. Layer

Lay half the tomato slices over the base. Nestle the fish in a single layer and pour over any marinade. Top with the remaining onion, pepper rings, tomatoes, and chile. Pour the coconut milk evenly over everything and drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil.

### 6. Simmer gently

Bring to a gentle simmer over medium, about 4 minutes (small bubbles at the edges). Cover, reduce to medium-low, and simmer without stirring 10 to 12 minutes, tilting the pan and spooning broth over the top once halfway, until the fish is opaque and flakes at the thickest part (145°F) and the peppers are tender but still hold their rings.

### 7. Finish and serve

Off heat, sprinkle with the remaining 2 tbsp lime juice, taste the broth for salt, and scatter the cilantro leaves. Serve in shallow bowls with the rice and lime wedges. No shrimp, no dende, no dairy. kitchen_tested is false.
