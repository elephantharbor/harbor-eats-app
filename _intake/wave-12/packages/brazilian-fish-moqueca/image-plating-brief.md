## Hero subject

Large tender chunks of white halibut sitting in a creamy coral-orange coconut broth, crowned with glossy red and yellow bell-pepper rings, soft tomato rounds, onion rings, thin chile slices, and fresh cilantro leaves, with a neat mound of fluffy white rice to one side of the bowl.

## Vessel

Wide, shallow off-white stoneware bowl. (The braiser is not the hero; one served portion reads better at card scale.)

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 50 degrees) to show the layered pepper rings on top and the depth of broth.

## Required visible components (fidelity)

- 2 to 3 large flaky white fish chunks, opaque and moist
- Red and yellow bell-pepper rings, intact
- Tomato rounds
- Coral-orange creamy coconut broth pooling
- Cilantro leaves
- Mound of white rice in the bowl
- Lime wedge on the rim

## Texture cues

Moist flaking fish, glossy tender pepper rings, creamy broth with a light olive-oil sheen.

## Sauce / component placement

Plenty of broth pooled around the fish and lapping the rice; creamy pale coral-orange from paprika and tomato, not deep red palm-oil orange, not brown.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro leaves, thin chile slices, a lime wedge.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a halved lime, a few cilantro sprigs, one small red chile at the frame edge on a neutral linen.

## Natural color opportunities

Red and yellow peppers, red tomato, white fish and rice, green cilantro, coral broth.

## Forbidden elements

- shrimp, prawns, or any shellfish (mussels, clams, crab)
- whole fish, fish heads, or visible fish skin
- deep red-orange dende oil slick
- farofa or toasted manioc
- black beans
- cheese, crema, or yogurt
- bread
- green bell pepper
- text, logos, watermarks, packaging, hands, faces
- excessive steam
