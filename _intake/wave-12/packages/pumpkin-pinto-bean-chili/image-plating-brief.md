# Image / Plating Brief — Smoky Pumpkin and Pinto Bean Chili

Author: Juniper (Harbor Eats - Catalog Curator), 2026-10-08 (America/Chicago). Written before generation. Source: this package's `v1.json` `recipe_version` (exact recipe), plus the FlavorWeave house style (`image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`; house-quality reference = Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for quality/light/appeal, not composition).

## Hero subject

A wide bowl of thick, brick-red pumpkin-pinto chili with visible whole pinto beans and flecks of red bell pepper, topped (not buried) with a deliberate cluster of diced green avocado, thin white-and-pink radish coins, cilantro, and golden crushed corn tortilla chips.

## Vessel

Wide, shallow off-white or warm-cream stoneware bowl (not a deep mug or tall crock), so the chili surface and toppings read from above. A spoon may rest in the bowl.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 55–65 degrees). Chili is a bowl food; the angle shows the topping cluster and the thick texture at the rim.

## Required visible components

Thick brick-red chili body; whole pinto beans (tan-pink, speckled-brown cooked pintos, not black or red kidney beans); small red bell pepper pieces; diced avocado; thin radish coins; cilantro leaves; crushed golden corn tortilla chips; a lime wedge.

## Texture cues

Chili should look thick and spoon-coating (mounds slightly), glossy but not oily; beans intact with a few crushed; chips visibly crisp; avocado fresh-cut, not browned; radish crisp and translucent-edged.

## Sauce/component placement

The chili is the sauce. Toppings sit in an off-center crescent on top so the red chili still shows on the other side. No drizzles.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Avocado, radish, cilantro, crushed corn tortilla chips, lime wedge.

## Environmental styling

Optional and small: a lime half, two whole radishes, a few loose tortilla chips near the bowl. No extra side dishes.

## Color opportunities

Brick-red chili, bright green avocado and cilantro, white-and-magenta radish, golden chips, lime green.

## Forbidden elements

Sour cream, crema drizzle, shredded or crumbled cheese, ground beef/turkey or any meat, black beans, red kidney beans, corn kernels, jalapeño slices, green onions, cornbread, rice, a whole decorative pumpkin, orange 'pumpkin soup' look, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered with its full rim inside the middle 70% of the width; generator output may be 16:9 and is center-cropped to 4:3, so nothing important near the left/right edges. Topping cluster must stay readable at 640×480 and in the FlavorWeave card crop (center).

## Ambiguity

Pumpkin is melted into the chili; it is not visible as cubes. Do not show pumpkin chunks or a pumpkin-colored soup.

## Certification targets

K fidelity (every Required visible component, nothing Forbidden), L1 technical/style (4:3 WebP 1200×900 + 640×480, crop-safe, no artifacts), L2 hero appeal (bright natural light, believable vivid color, visible texture, abundant intentional plating, strong at card scale). Accurate-but-dull fails; beautiful-but-wrong fails.
