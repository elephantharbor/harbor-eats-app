Served with fluffy basmati rice and cilantro.

## Steps

### 1. Season the chicken and prep

Cut the chicken into 1 1/2-inch pieces and toss with 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt and 1 tsp garam masala. Finely chop the onion, mince the garlic, and grate the ginger. In a small bowl combine 1 tsp garam masala, the cumin, coriander, turmeric, paprika, and cayenne. Rinse the basmati until the water runs mostly clear and drain. Cut 2 tbsp of the butter into cubes and keep cold. About 15 minutes.

_Clock: prep 15 min_

### 2. Sauté the aromatics

Set a 6-quart electric pressure cooker to Sauté (high). Add 3 tbsp butter and the 1 tbsp oil; when the butter foams (about 2 minutes), add the onion and 1/2 tsp kosher salt. Cook, stirring, until soft and golden at the edges, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and stir 1 minute. About 8 minutes.

_Clock: cook 8 min_

### 3. Bloom the spices and deglaze

Add the spice mix and stir 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in 1/2 cup water and scrape the bottom thoroughly until nothing is stuck; this prevents a burn warning. Turn off Sauté. About 2 minutes.

_Clock: cook 2 min_

### 4. Layer and pressure cook

Spread the chicken in an even layer over the onions. Pour the tomato puree over the top and do not stir (the thick tomato stays off the bottom). Lock the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and Pressure Cook on High for 5 minutes. It takes about 10 minutes to come to pressure. When the cook time ends, let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes, then quick-release the rest. About 20 minutes.

_Clock: cook 20 min_

### 5. Cook the rice

As soon as the cooker is sealed, combine the rinsed basmati, 2 1/4 cups water, and 1/2 tsp kosher salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and cook 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and let it stand covered 5 minutes, then fluff. This runs entirely inside the 20-minute pressure step and adds no time.

_Clock: overlap 0 min (runs inside the 20-minute pressure step)_

### 6. Finish the sauce

Open the lid away from you. Set to Sauté. Stir in all but 2 tbsp of the cream, the sugar, and the last 1/2 tsp garam masala. Simmer, stirring gently, 3 to 4 minutes, until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon and the chicken is very tender (at least 165 F; thighs are best at 175 F). Turn off the heat and stir in the 2 tbsp cold cubed butter until the sauce turns glossy. Taste and adjust salt. About 5 minutes.

_Clock: cook 5 min_

### 7. Serve

Spoon rice into one side of 4 wide, shallow bowls and ladle the butter chicken beside it. Drizzle the reserved 2 tbsp cream over the sauce in a loose swirl and scatter cilantro. About 2 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 2 min_

## Scaling notes

- **1 serving(s):** A true single serving is not recommended in a 6-quart cooker (minimum liquid and burn risk). For 1, make the 2-serving half batch below (1-serving shopping ratio 0.5) and refrigerate the second portion up to 3 days.
- **2 serving(s):** Halve everything; keep the 1/2 cup deglazing water unchanged (minimum liquid). Same 5-minute High pressure; come-up is a few minutes faster. Rice: 3/4 cup basmati, 1 1/8 cups water.
- **4 serving(s):** As written.
- Do not scale: 1/2 cup deglaze water minimum; do not stir the tomato into the bottom before pressure cooking; 5 minutes High + 5 minutes natural release (piece size, not batch size, sets the time)
