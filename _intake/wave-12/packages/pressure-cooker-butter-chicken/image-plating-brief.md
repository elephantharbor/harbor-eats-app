## Hero subject

Tender chicken thigh pieces in a glossy, velvety orange-red tomato-butter-cream sauce with a loose swirl of cream on top and fresh cilantro, beside a mound of fluffy white basmati in the same bowl.

## Vessel

Wide, shallow off-white stoneware bowl; rice on one side, curry on the other (not rice in a ring, not a copper karahi).

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 50–60 degrees) to show the sauce surface, the chunky chicken, and the rice grains.

## Required visible components

Bite-size chicken thigh pieces (boneless, tender, partly submerged); thick orange-red sauce with a buttery sheen; a loose white cream swirl; chopped cilantro; fluffy long-grain white basmati with separate grains.

## Texture cues

Silky, spoon-coating sauce; tender, not dry chicken; separate fluffy rice grains.

## Sauce/component placement

Sauce pooled generously around the chicken against the rice; cream drizzled in a single loose swirl, not a heavy blanket.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro, cream swirl.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small sprig of cilantro, a small piece of fresh ginger and a few garlic cloves at the edge, a spoon. No breads.

## Color opportunities

Orange-red sauce, ivory cream swirl, green cilantro, white rice.

## Forbidden elements

Naan or any flatbread, cashews or nuts, paneer cubes, peas, chickpeas, potatoes, bone-in chicken, charred tandoori pieces or skewers, raita/yogurt bowl, lemon/lime, red food-coloring look, a pressure cooker in frame, copper or brass serveware, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Ambiguity

Butter chicken is tomato-cream, not a brown or green curry; color must be orange-red, not neon.
