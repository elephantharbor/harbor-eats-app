## Hero subject

A generous serving of glossy cashew chicken stir-fry (bite-size 3/4-inch golden chicken cubes, red pepper, celery, onion, scallions, whole curved toasted cashews) beside fluffy jasmine rice.

## Vessel

Wide off-white shallow bowl or rimmed plate.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 55-60 degrees) to show the mix of components and the cashews.

## Required visible components

- Bite-size 3/4-inch cubes of stir-fried chicken breast, golden at the edges and lightly glossed (velveted, not breaded); no whole or sliced cutlets, no strips longer than 1 inch, no grill marks
- Red bell pepper squares
- Pale-green celery slices cut on the bias
- Translucent onion pieces
- Plentiful whole, curved (kidney-shaped) golden toasted cashews, the only nuts in the dish
- 1-inch scallion lengths
- Thin glossy brown soy-hoisin sauce coating
- Fluffy white jasmine rice

## Texture cues

Seared chicken edges, crisp-tender charred vegetables, crunchy cashews, glossy (not gloopy) sauce, separate rice grains.

## Sauce / component placement

Light glossy coating on the stir-fry; no pool of thick sauce on the plate.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallion greens (recipe-listed).

## Environmental styling

Optional: chopsticks and a knob of ginger in soft focus.

## Natural color opportunities

Golden chicken cubes, red pepper, pale-green celery, green scallions, golden cashews, white rice.

## Forbidden elements

- Peanuts
- Chickpeas or any other legume
- Round nuggets, round nuts, or any nut other than whole cashews
- Grill marks or grill-striped chicken
- Whole chicken cutlets, sliced cutlets, or strips longer than 1 inch
- Broccoli
- Carrots
- Sesame seeds scattered on top
- Whole dried chiles
- Breaded or deep-fried chicken (orange chicken look)
- Thick gloopy or bright orange sauce
- Noodles
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered with margin left and right; stir-fry and rice both within the center so a 4:3 crop and the 640x480 card keep the cashews legible. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

Chinese-American cashew chicken with a brown soy-hoisin sauce, not kung pao (peanuts, chiles) or orange chicken.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
