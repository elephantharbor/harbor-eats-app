## Steps

### 1. Start the rice

At the start of prep, put the rinsed rice and 2 cups water in a saucepan and bring to a boil over high heat. Cover, reduce to low, and cook 15 minutes; turn off the heat and rest, covered, 10 minutes. It needs no further attention and is ready before the stir-fry is done.

### 2. Velvet the chicken and mix the sauce

Cut the chicken into 3/4-inch pieces and toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sherry, and 1 tbsp cornstarch until evenly coated; let it sit while you prep. In a small bowl, whisk the remaining 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sherry, the hoisin, brown sugar, 1 tsp cornstarch, and 1/3 cup water. Cut the bell pepper, celery, and onion; mince the garlic and ginger; cut the scallions, keeping whites and greens apart.

### 3. Toast the cashews

Toast the cashews in a dry 12-inch skillet over medium heat, tossing often, until golden in spots and fragrant, 3 to 4 minutes. Move them to a plate.

### 4. Sear the chicken

Raise the heat to high. Add 1 tbsp oil and, once shimmering, half the chicken in a single layer. Sear undisturbed until golden underneath, about 2 minutes, then stir-fry until just cooked through with no pink (165 F), about 2 minutes more. Move to a plate. Repeat with 1 tbsp oil and the rest of the chicken.

### 5. Stir-fry the vegetables

Add the last 1 tbsp oil to the hot skillet. Add the onion, celery, bell pepper, and scallion whites and stir-fry until crisp-tender with a few charred spots, about 3 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and stir 30 seconds, until fragrant.

### 6. Sauce and finish

Return the chicken and its juices. Re-whisk the sauce, pour it in, and toss until it bubbles and turns glossy and lightly thick, about 1 minute. Off the heat, toss in the cashews, scallion greens, and sesame oil. Fluff the rice and serve the stir-fry over it. kitchen_tested is false.
