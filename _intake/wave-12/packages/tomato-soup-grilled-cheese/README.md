## Steps

### 1. Heat the oven and prep the tomatoes

Heat the oven to 450 F with a rack in the upper-middle position at the start of prep; it preheats while you work. Core and halve the tomatoes lengthwise, cut the onion into 8 wedges, and peel the garlic. On a rimmed sheet pan, toss the tomatoes, onion, and garlic with the olive oil, 1 1/2 tsp salt, and the pepper, then turn the tomatoes cut side up. Shred the cheddar and leave the butter out to soften.

### 2. Roast the vegetables

Roast until the tomatoes slump and blister with browned, caramelized edges, the onion tips char, and the garlic is soft, 25 to 28 minutes.

### 3. Build the sandwiches

While the tomatoes roast, butter one side of each bread slice. Make 4 sandwiches with the buttered sides out and about 1/2 cup cheddar inside each, pressing gently so they hold together.

### 4. Simmer the soup

Scrape the roasted vegetables and every bit of their juices into a large pot. Add the tomato paste, vegetable broth, and basil leaves (keep a few small leaves for the top). Bring to a simmer over medium-high, about 4 minutes, then simmer gently 6 minutes. Turn the oven off.

### 5. Griddle the grilled cheese

As soon as the soup goes on, heat a 12-inch skillet over medium-low. Cook 2 sandwiches at a time until the bread is deep golden and crisp and the cheese has melted, 3 to 4 minutes per side, pressing lightly with a spatula. Keep the first batch on a rack in the turned-off, still-warm oven while you cook the second.

### 6. Blend and finish

Take the soup off the heat and blend with an immersion blender until silky, 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in all but 1 tbsp of the cream. Taste; add up to 1/2 tsp salt, and the sugar only if the soup tastes sharp. Rewarm briefly without boiling.

### 7. Serve

Ladle the soup into bowls, swirl the reserved cream on top, and add the reserved basil leaves. Cut each grilled cheese in half diagonally and serve alongside for dunking. kitchen_tested is false.
