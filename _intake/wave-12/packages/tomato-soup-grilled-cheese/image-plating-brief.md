# Image / Plating Brief — Roasted Tomato Soup with Grilled Cheese

Source (read-only): this package's recipe only — `README.md` and `v1.json` `recipe_version` (package_revision `freeze-r1`). Written before generation. Standard: `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md` — truthful enough to trust, attractive enough to choose.

## Hero subject

A bowl of velvety, deep orange-red roasted tomato soup with a cream swirl and small basil leaves, next to a sourdough grilled cheese cut on the diagonal with a molten cheddar cross-section.

## Vessel

Wide off-white ceramic soup bowl; grilled cheese on a small matching plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter (about 35-40 degrees) so the soup surface and the sandwich's cut face, with its cheese layer and crisp crust, both read.

## Required visible components

- Smooth, deep orange-red tomato soup with a few darker roasted flecks
- Cream swirl on the soup
- Small fresh basil leaves
- Sourdough grilled cheese cut diagonally into 2 triangles
- Thick layer of melted orange-yellow sharp cheddar visible in the cut face
- Deep golden, crisp, buttery crust

## Texture cues

Velvety soup with a gentle sheen; crisp, golden, open-crumb sourdough crust; soft melted cheese with a slight pull.

## Sauce / component placement

Cream swirl only on the soup surface; no drizzle on the sandwich.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Small basil leaves and the cream swirl (recipe-listed). A crack of black pepper is fine.

## Environmental styling

Optional: two or three whole Roma tomatoes or a small basil sprig in soft focus; a soup spoon. No extra sides.

## Natural color opportunities

Orange-red soup, white cream swirl, green basil, golden toast, orange-yellow cheddar.

## Forbidden elements

- Croutons
- Grated Parmesan
- Bacon or ham
- Tomato slices inside the sandwich
- Pale white sandwich bread
- Burnt or blackened toast
- Exaggerated cheese pull stretching unrealistically
- Text, logo, watermark, packaging, hands, faces

## Crop strategy

Soup bowl and sandwich grouped in the center 70% of the width so a 4:3 center-crop keeps both. The cheese layer must be legible at 640x480. Master 1200×900 and card 640×480 are center-cropped 4:3 from the GenerateImage output; if the output is 16:9, the left and right edges are lost.

## Ambiguity

Blended roasted tomato soup (smooth), not chunky tomato stew or gazpacho.

## House style

Bright, premium editorial food photography: soft diffused daylight, light neutral stone or linen surface, realistic texture, vibrant but believable color, one clear hero, no clutter. The house-quality reference (Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl) sets light, finish, and appeal — not composition.
