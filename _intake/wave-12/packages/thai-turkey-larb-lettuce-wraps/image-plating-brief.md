## Hero subject

Butter lettuce cups generously filled with fine crumbles of lime-dressed turkey larb flecked with red chile, sliced shallot, scallion, torn mint, cilantro, and a dusting of golden toasted rice powder.

## Vessel

Large light off-white ceramic platter holding 4–5 filled lettuce cups, with cucumber slices and lime wedges arranged on the platter.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 50–60 degrees) so the filled cups' bowl shape and the herbs inside both read.

## Required visible components

Pale-green butter lettuce cups; finely crumbled cooked turkey (light golden-brown with a few browned bits, moist with dressing); visible red chile flakes; thin purple-pink shallot slices; green scallion rings; whole/torn mint leaves; cilantro; golden toasted rice powder specks; cucumber slices; lime wedges.

## Texture cues

Juicy, finely crumbled meat (not large chunks, not glazed); crisp cupped lettuce; fresh herbs; sandy toasted rice.

## Sauce/component placement

The lime-fish-sauce dressing is absorbed into the meat with a slight sheen; no thick brown glaze, no drizzled sauces.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Mint, cilantro, scallion, chile flakes, toasted rice powder, lime wedges, cucumber.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small mound of extra toasted rice powder in a tiny dish, a lime half, a few mint sprigs. No bowls of rice.

## Color opportunities

Lettuce green, mint green, red chile flecks, purple shallot, golden rice powder, pale cucumber, lime.

## Forbidden elements

Glossy brown hoisin/teriyaki glaze (no takeout-style chicken lettuce wraps), water chestnuts, diced mushrooms, carrots, red cabbage, peanuts, cashews, sesame seeds, sriracha or sweet chili drizzle, tomatoes, rice bowls, noodles, crispy wonton strips, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Crop strategy

Platter centered with the filled cups in the middle ~70% of the width; generator output may be 16:9 and is center-cropped to 4:3. At 640×480 the filled cups must read as lettuce wraps with herbs.

## Ambiguity

Larb is a warm meat salad dressed with lime and fish sauce, not a stir-fry in sauce.

## Certification targets

K fidelity (every Required visible component, nothing Forbidden), L1 technical/style (4:3 WebP 1200×900 + 640×480, crop-safe, no artifacts), L2 hero appeal (bright natural light, believable vivid color, visible texture, abundant intentional plating, strong at card scale).
