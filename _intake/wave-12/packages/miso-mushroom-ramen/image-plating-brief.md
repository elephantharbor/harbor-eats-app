## Hero subject

A deep bowl of plant miso ramen: opaque golden-tan miso-tahini broth with springy noodles visibly lifting at one side, topped in neat sectors with glossy soy-seared shiitake slices, two bright green baby bok choy halves, a mound of yellow corn, two nori rectangles standing at the rim, scallion greens, and sesame seeds.

## Vessel

Deep, wide off-white or warm-cream stoneware ramen bowl with a plain rim. Plain wooden chopsticks may rest across the bowl rim.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 55-65 degrees) so the topping sectors and noodles both read; not flat overhead, not side-on.

## Required visible components (fidelity)

- Opaque golden-tan miso broth (creamy-looking, not clear)
- Wavy yellow ramen noodles visible
- Glossy browned shiitake slices
- Two baby bok choy halves, bright green
- Corn kernels in a small mound
- Two nori rectangles at the rim
- Scallion greens and sesame seeds

## Texture cues

Seared mushroom edges, glossy noodles, crisp-tender bok choy, a faint sheen of sesame oil on the broth surface.

## Sauce / component placement

The broth is the sauce: plenty of it, surface just below the toppings, with a few sesame-oil droplets.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallion greens, toasted sesame seeds, nori only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: two or three fresh shiitake caps, a few scallions, a small dish of sesame seeds at the frame edge. Nothing that reads as an extra side.

## Natural color opportunities

Brown mushrooms, green bok choy and scallions, yellow corn, dark nori, golden broth.

## Forbidden elements

- soft-boiled or marinated egg (ajitama) or any egg
- chashu pork or any meat
- chicken
- shrimp or seafood
- fish cake (narutomaki)
- butter pat or corn-butter
- bean sprouts
- bamboo shoots (menma)
- chili oil or red chili threads
- tofu
- clear consommé-style broth
- text, logos, watermarks, packaging, hands, faces
- excessive steam
