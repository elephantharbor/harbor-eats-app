## Hero subject

Fanned slices of pepper-crusted seared ahi tuna with a ruby-red rare center and a thin cooked edge, at the center of a composed niçoise: jammy egg halves, warm dressed baby potatoes, green beans, cherry tomatoes, olives, capers, on smooth, cupped butter (Bibb) lettuce leaves.

## Vessel

Large oval off-white platter (family-style composed salad) with a soft rim. Not a deep bowl.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter to near-overhead (60–75 degrees) so the separate groups of components read clearly; enough angle to show the tuna's seared edge and red center on the cut faces.

## Required visible components

Sliced seared tuna (red center, cooked edge, crust of coarsely cracked black peppercorns only, dark, no pale seeds); eight jammy egg halves (four eggs, halved) with deep orange yolks (at least six halves clearly visible); halved baby potatoes glistening with vinaigrette; bright green beans; halved red cherry tomatoes; dark Niçoise/Kalamata olives; a few capers; parsley; smooth, rounded cup-shaped butter (Bibb) lettuce leaves, soft and tender, no crinkling, no thick white ribs; visible vinaigrette with shallot flecks.

## Texture cues

Tuna: seared crust, silky rare interior (not grey through, not raw-looking outside). Eggs: jammy, glossy yolk. Potatoes: tender, dressing-glossed. Beans: crisp, snappy. Lettuce: soft, smooth, tender cupped butter (Bibb) leaves with fine veins, no crinkling, no thick white ribs (must not read as cabbage).

## Sauce/component placement

Vinaigrette spooned over the composed groups so it beads on potatoes and pools lightly; optionally a small glass jar of the vinaigrette at the platter's edge (the recipe's own dressing).

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley, capers, cracked black pepper. Optional anchovies are allowed by the recipe but leave them OFF the hero to show the default build.

## Environmental styling

Optional: small jar of the Dijon vinaigrette, a few loose cherry tomatoes. No lemon, no bread.

## Color opportunities

Ruby tuna, orange yolks, green beans and lettuce, red tomatoes, black-purple olives, golden potatoes.

## Forbidden elements

Napa cabbage, savoy cabbage, romaine, iceberg (or any crinkled, thick-white-ribbed greens), sesame crust or sesame seeds (no 'ahi tataki' styling), pale seed-like specks in the tuna crust, canned flaked tuna, fully grey overcooked tuna, raw unseared tuna, avocado, cheese, croutons, bread, corn, red onion rings, radishes, lemon wedges, wasabi, soy sauce, anchovies on the hero, butter pats, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Crop strategy

Platter centered with the tuna fan in the middle third; generator output may be 16:9 and is center-cropped to 4:3, so keep all components within the central ~75% of the width. Tuna + eggs must remain legible at 640×480 and in the FlavorWeave card crop.

## Ambiguity

Tuna is seared rare by default; show a rare center. Potatoes are warm and dressed, not roasted.

## Certification targets

K fidelity (every Required visible component, nothing Forbidden), L1 technical/style (4:3 WebP 1200×900 + 640×480, crop-safe, no artifacts), L2 hero appeal (bright natural light, believable vivid color, visible texture, abundant intentional plating, strong at card scale). Accurate-but-dull fails; beautiful-but-wrong fails.
