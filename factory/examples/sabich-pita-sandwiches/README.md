## Steps

### 1. Boil water, salt the eggplant, and prep

Bring a medium saucepan of water to a boil for the eggs (it heats during this prep). Slice the eggplant into 1/2-inch rounds, sprinkle both sides with 1 tsp salt, and lay them on a towel-lined sheet pan for 10 minutes. Meanwhile, finely dice the tomatoes and cucumbers, chop the parsley, slice the pickles, grate the garlic, and juice the lemon. Read the pita and hummus labels: no milk, whey, butter, yogurt, or cheese. HARD CONTRACT: dairy-free pita and hummus; no feta, no yogurt sauce.

### 2. Make the tahini sauce and salad

In a bowl, stir the tahini with 2 tbsp lemon juice, the grated garlic, and 1/4 tsp salt. Whisk in the cold water a little at a time; it will seize and thicken, then loosen into a smooth, pourable sauce. In another bowl, toss the tomatoes, cucumbers, half the parsley, 1 tbsp lemon juice, the olive oil, and 1/4 tsp salt. Pat the eggplant slices very dry with the towel.

### 3. Cook the eggs

Lower the eggs into the boiling water with a spoon and boil 10 minutes for firm but still moist yolks. Move them to a bowl of ice water for 5 minutes, then peel and slice into rounds. This runs alongside the eggplant.

### 4. Pan-fry the eggplant

Heat a 12-inch skillet over medium-high with about 2 1/2 tbsp oil until it shimmers. Fry a single layer of eggplant rounds until deep golden-brown and crisp at the edges, 3 to 4 minutes per side, and creamy all the way through. Drain on paper towels. Repeat in two more batches with the remaining oil, adjusting the heat so the slices brown without burning.

### 5. Warm the pitas

Wipe out the skillet and warm each pita over medium heat, about 30 seconds per side, until soft and pliable. Cut a 2-inch opening along one edge (or halve them) to make a pocket.

### 6. Stuff and serve

Spread 2 tbsp hummus inside each pita. Layer in the eggplant, egg slices, chopped salad, and pickles, then drizzle generously with tahini sauce and scatter with the remaining parsley. Add a spoonful of amba if you have it. Serve right away while the eggplant is still crisp. kitchen_tested is false.
