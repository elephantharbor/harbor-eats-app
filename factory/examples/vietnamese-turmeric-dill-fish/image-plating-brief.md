## Hero subject

Golden turmeric-seared catfish pieces draped with vivid wilted dill and scallion lengths, sitting on a nest of white rice vermicelli, finished with mint, chopped cashews, and red chile.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white stoneware bowl. A small off-white dipping bowl of amber nước chấm with chile and garlic flecks beside it.

## Camera angle

Higher three-quarter (about 50–60 degrees) so the noodle nest, the golden fish, and the green dill layer all read.

## Required visible components

Bite-size (about 2-inch) catfish pieces with turmeric-yellow color and golden-brown seared faces; generous bright green dill sprigs and scallion lengths, glossy and just wilted; fine white rice vermicelli; whole mint leaves; coarsely chopped pale-golden cashews (a few recognizable cashew halves); thin red chile rings; lime wedge; nước chấm in a small bowl.

## Texture cues

Seared, slightly crisp turmeric crust on tender flaky fish; dill silky but still bright; noodles springy and separate; cashews crunchy.

## Sauce/component placement

Nước chấm in a small side bowl (and a little glistening on the fish); no thick or dark sauce on the plate.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Mint leaves, chopped roasted cashews, red chile slices, lime wedge, the dill and scallions themselves.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bunch of dill, a lime half, a few whole cashews near the bowl, chopsticks. Must not imply another dish.

## Color opportunities

Turmeric gold, vivid dill green, white noodles, red chile, pale-gold cashews, lime green.

## Forbidden elements

Peanuts or peanut-looking whole round nuts, shrimp, purple-grey shrimp paste, broth or soup (not pho), cilantro, Thai basil, bean sprouts, lettuce cups, rice paper, battered or deep-fried fish, skin-on or whole fish, sesame seeds, soy sauce, text, logos, watermarks, hands, faces.

## Ambiguity

This is a noodle plate, not a soup: noodles are drained and cool, no broth.
