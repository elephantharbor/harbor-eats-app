# Vietnamese Turmeric Fish with Dill and Noodles

Chả cá–style catfish: bite-size pieces marinated in turmeric, ginger, shallot, and fish sauce, seared golden, then tossed with a big handful of dill and scallions wilted in the hot pan. Served over cool rice vermicelli with mint, chopped roasted cashews, red chile, and a bright lime nước chấm. No shrimp paste; cashews instead of peanuts.

## Steps

### 1. Marinate the fish

Pat the catfish dry and cut into 2-inch pieces. In a bowl stir together 1 tbsp turmeric, the grated ginger, the minced shallot, 2 cloves of the garlic, 1 1/2 tbsp of the fish sauce, 1 tsp of the sugar, 1/4 tsp kosher salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper, and 1 tbsp of the oil. Add the fish and turn gently to coat every piece yellow. Let it marinate at room temperature for 15 minutes while you do the next step (or refrigerate up to 2 hours). About 5 minutes.

_Clock: prep 5 min_

### 2. Boil water; prep herbs, cashews, and nước chấm

Bring a large covered pot of water to a boil for the noodles. Cut the dill into 2-inch sprigs and the scallions into 2-inch lengths. Pick the mint leaves. Coarsely chop the cashews. Thinly slice the chile. Juice 2 limes (about 3 tbsp) and cut the third into wedges. For the nước chấm, stir 2 tbsp sugar into 1/2 cup warm water until dissolved, then add 3 tbsp fish sauce, the 3 tbsp lime juice, the last clove of minced garlic, and half the chile. Taste: it should be bright, salty-sweet-sour. The fish finishes its 15-minute marinade during this step. About 15 minutes.

_Clock: prep 15 min (15-minute marinade runs inside this block)_

### 3. Cook the noodles

Boil the rice vermicelli according to the package, usually 3 to 5 minutes, until tender but springy. Drain, rinse under cold water until cool, shake very dry, and divide among 4 shallow bowls. About 6 minutes.

_Clock: cook 6 min_

### 4. Sear the turmeric fish

Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add the fish in a single layer with space between pieces, scraping in any marinade. Cook without moving for 3 minutes, until the undersides are deep golden. Turn each piece gently with a thin spatula and cook 2 to 3 minutes more, until opaque all the way through and flaking at the edges (145 F). Move the fish to a plate. About 6 minutes.

_Clock: cook 6 min_

### 5. Wilt the dill and scallions

Return the skillet to medium-high and add the last 1 tbsp oil. Add the scallions and toss 30 seconds, then add the dill and toss 30 to 45 seconds, just until wilted, glossy, and bright green. Turn off the heat, return the fish, and fold gently so the dill drapes over the fish. About 2 minutes.

_Clock: cook 2 min_

### 6. Assemble

Spoon the fish, dill, and scallions over the noodles. Scatter mint leaves, chopped cashews, and the remaining chile over each bowl; add a lime wedge. Pour nước chấm over at the table, about 2 to 3 tbsp per bowl, and toss everything together. No peanuts, no shrimp paste. About 3 minutes. kitchen_tested is false.

_Clock: cook 3 min_
