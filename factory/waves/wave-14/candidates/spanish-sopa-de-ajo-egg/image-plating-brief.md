# Image / Plating Brief — Castilian Garlic Soup with Poached Eggs and Spinach

Package `spanish-sopa-de-ajo-egg`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A wide bowl of brick-red, smoky garlic broth with softened golden bread chunks, wilted spinach, slivers of golden garlic, and a glossy poached egg in the center with its yolk just showing, scattered with parsley.

## Vessel

Wide terracotta (cazuela-style) bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees.

## Required visible components

Poached egg; bread pieces; spinach; garlic slices; parsley; brick-red broth.

## Texture cues

Silky egg, soft bread, glossy oil-flecked broth.

## Sauce / component placement

Broth fills the bowl, egg and bread above the surface.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a garlic bulb; a hunk of bread.

## Color opportunities

Brick red, white and yellow egg, deep green spinach, golden bread.

## Forbidden elements

Ham or jamón, chorizo, cheese, cream, a clear yellow broth, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Egg at the center of the bowl; legible at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> spanish-sopa-de-ajo-egg` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Spanish garlic soup with egg. Not shakshuka, not tomato soup.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
