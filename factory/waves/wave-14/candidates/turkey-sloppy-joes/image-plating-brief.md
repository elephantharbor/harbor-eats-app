Package `turkey-sloppy-joes`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A soft toasted bun overflowing with glossy, saucy brick-red turkey sloppy joe filling dripping down the side, next to a pile of golden-edged garlicky zucchini half-moons.

## Vessel

Matte white plate or a parchment-lined tray.

## Camera angle

Low three-quarter, about 20 degrees, to show the overflowing filling.

## Required visible components

Bun; saucy filling with diced pepper and onion; zucchini.

## Texture cues

Thick saucy filling, soft toasted bun, browned zucchini.

## Sauce / component placement

Filling spilling slightly onto the plate.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

None.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a napkin.

## Color opportunities

Brick red, golden bun, green and gold zucchini.

## Forbidden elements

Cheese slices, fries, coleslaw, a burger patty, sesame seeds, text, logos, hands.

Sandwich centered; legible at 640x480.

## Ambiguity

Saucy sloppy joe sandwich. Not a burger, not chili.

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
