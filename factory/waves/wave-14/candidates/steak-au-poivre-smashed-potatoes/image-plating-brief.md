## Hero subject

A seared strip steak with a dark, craggy cracked-peppercorn crust, sliced to show a rosy medium-rare interior, napped with a glossy ivory-tan peppercorn cream sauce, beside golden crispy smashed potatoes with chives.

## Vessel

Wide white plate with a gentle rim.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 30 degrees, catching the sliced interior and crust.

## Required visible components

Peppercorn crust; pink center; cream sauce pooling; four or five smashed potatoes with craggy edges; chives.

## Texture cues

Crusty seared steak, silky sauce, shattering potato edges with fluffy centers.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce spooned over part of the steak and pooling beside it, not drowning it.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Snipped chives on the potatoes.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a steak knife at the plate edge.

## Color opportunities

Dark crust, rosy pink, ivory sauce flecked with pepper, golden potatoes, green chives.

## Forbidden elements

Raw-looking or gray meat, fries, green salad, flames, a brandy bottle, text, logos, hands.

Steak slices centered; potatoes in frame at the card crop.

Steak au poivre with cream sauce. Not a gravy-covered chop, not a cheesesteak.
