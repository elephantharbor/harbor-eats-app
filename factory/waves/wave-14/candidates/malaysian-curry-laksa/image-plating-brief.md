A deep bowl of glossy orange-red coconut curry broth with a slick of red chile oil on top, pink curled shrimp, rice vermicelli peeking through, and a fresh heap of bean sprouts, cucumber matchsticks, mint, and a lime wedge.

## Vessel

Deep wide ceramic noodle bowl in white or speckled cream.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Orange broth with oil droplets; shrimp; noodles; sprouts; cucumber; mint; lime.

## Texture cues

Creamy rich broth; plump shrimp; crisp sprouts and cucumber.

## Sauce / component placement

Broth fills the bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Mint, cucumber, sprouts, lime.

## Environmental styling

Optional: chopsticks and a ceramic soup spoon.

Orange-red broth, pink shrimp, white noodles, green mint and cucumber.

## Forbidden elements

Tofu puffs, chicken pieces, boiled eggs, peanuts, a clear broth, text, logos, hands.
