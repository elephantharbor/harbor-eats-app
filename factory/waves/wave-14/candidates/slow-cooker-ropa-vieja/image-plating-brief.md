## Hero subject

A generous mound of glossy, tomato-red shredded beef tangled with soft strips of red and green pepper, onion, and halved green olives, over a bed of fluffy white rice.

## Vessel

Wide shallow bowl in a warm terracotta or cream glaze.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Distinct beef shreds; red and green pepper strips; green olive halves with pimento; white rice; chopped cilantro.

## Texture cues

Tender, saucy shreds; soft peppers; separate rice grains.

## Sauce / component placement

Thick tomato sauce clinging to the beef, a little seeping into the rice.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped cilantro only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: the slow-cooker insert out of focus at the frame edge is not allowed; use a small bowl of extra olives instead.

## Color opportunities

Brick red, pepper red and green, olive green, white rice.

## Forbidden elements

Black beans, fried plantains, tortillas, cheese, a slow-cooker appliance, raw beef, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered; the shredded texture must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> slow-cooker-ropa-vieja` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Shredded beef with peppers and olives over rice. Not chili, not pulled-pork barbecue.

## House style
