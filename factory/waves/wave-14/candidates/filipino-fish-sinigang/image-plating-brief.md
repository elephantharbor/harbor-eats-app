## Hero subject

A deep bowl of clear, faintly cloudy amber tamarind broth holding pink-edged red snapper chunks, wilted bok choy halves, slumped tomato wedges, translucent daikon half-moons, and green beans.

## Vessel

Deep wide ceramic soup bowl in a warm neutral glaze; a small bowl of white rice beside it.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees, so the broth surface and the components above it both read.

## Required visible components

Snapper with reddish skin edges; bok choy; tomato wedges; daikon; green beans; one whole green serrano; clear broth; white rice on the side.

## Texture cues

Flaky white fish, glossy wilted greens, soft tomatoes, a broth with a light sheen.

## Sauce / component placement

Broth fills about two thirds of the bowl with components breaking the surface.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

None beyond the whole serrano.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a spoon resting on the rice bowl.

## Color opportunities

Amber broth, white and red fish, deep green bok choy, red tomato, bright green beans.

## Forbidden elements

Coconut milk, noodles, shrimp, pork, a creamy or opaque broth, herb garnish piles, text, logos, hands.

Soup bowl centered and dominant; the rice bowl partly in frame at the upper edge, so the card keeps the soup whole.
