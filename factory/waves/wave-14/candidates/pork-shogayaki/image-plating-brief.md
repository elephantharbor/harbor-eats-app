# Image / Plating Brief — Pork Shōgayaki: Japanese Ginger Pork with Shredded Cabbage

Package `pork-shogayaki`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

Glossy, ginger-glazed thin pork slices with soft browned onion, layered on a plate beside a tall fluffy pile of pale-green shredded cabbage and a neat mound of short-grain rice.

## Vessel

Rectangular or oval Japanese ceramic plate in a speckled off-white glaze; a small rice bowl optional.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Pork slices with glaze; onion; shredded cabbage pile; rice; scallions.

## Texture cues

Tender glazed slices, fine crisp cabbage threads, sticky rice.

## Sauce / component placement

A little glaze pooling under the pork, not touching the cabbage.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Sliced scallions.

## Environmental styling

Optional: chopsticks on a rest.

## Color opportunities

Amber-brown pork, pale-green cabbage, white rice, green scallions.

## Forbidden elements

Breaded cutlets, mayonnaise squiggles, sesame seeds, tomato wedges, noodles, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Plate centered; cabbage and pork both inside the card crop.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> pork-shogayaki` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Japanese ginger pork with cabbage. Not tonkatsu, not teriyaki chicken.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
