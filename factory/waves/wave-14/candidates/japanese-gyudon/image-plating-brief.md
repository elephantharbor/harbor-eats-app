## Hero subject

A deep donburi bowl of glossy white short-grain rice topped with wide, paper-thin shaved beef ribbons and sheets, soy-glazed, softly folded and draped over the rice with visible fat streaks, tangled with translucent sweet onion, a little broth soaking into the rice, garnished with green scallions and a small heap of vivid red pickled ginger.

## Vessel

Deep ceramic donburi bowl in a dark or indigo glaze.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees.

## Required visible components

Wide, paper-thin shaved beef ribbons with visible folds and fat streaks; translucent onion; rice edge; scallions; beni shoga.

## Texture cues

Silky, tender, thin beef sheets that drape and fold; soft onion; sticky rice.

## Sauce / component placement

Broth glossing the beef and seeping into rice.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallions and pickled red ginger.

## Environmental styling

Optional: chopsticks on a rest.

## Color opportunities

Brown beef, amber onion, white rice, green scallion, red ginger.

## Forbidden elements

Crumbled or ground beef, small beef clumps or chunks, raw egg yolk, sesame seeds, broccoli, steak slices with grill marks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered; legible at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> japanese-gyudon` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Japanese beef rice bowl. Not a stir-fry, not teriyaki chicken.

## House style
