# Image / Plating Brief — Senegalese Fish Yassa with Lemon-Mustard Onions and Rice

Package `senegalese-fish-yassa`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A broiled tilapia fillet with lightly bronzed edges resting on fluffy white rice, heaped with glossy, golden, jammy onion ribbons in a thick lemon-mustard sauce.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white stoneware plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, so the onion pile has height and the fish edge reads.

## Required visible components

Golden jammy onions on top of the fish; a visible white fish edge; white rice; chopped parsley; one lemon wedge. No whole habanero on the plate (it is removed).

## Texture cues

Silky, translucent-golden onions; flaky white fish with browned edges; separate rice grains.

## Sauce / component placement

Glossy onion sauce spooned over the fish, a little running into the rice. Not soupy.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped parsley and a lemon wedge only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: half a lemon and a small bowl of extra onions in soft focus at the edge.

## Color opportunities

Golden onions, white fish and rice, bright green parsley, yellow lemon.

## Forbidden elements

Tomato sauce, coconut, peppers or carrots in the onions, fried or breaded fish, chicken, olives, cilantro, a whole chile on the plate, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Plate centered and filling about 75 percent of the frame width; the onion pile must still read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> senegalese-fish-yassa` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Fish yassa: broiled fish under lemon-mustard onions. Not a tomato stew, not fried fish, not chicken.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
