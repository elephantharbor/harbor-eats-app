## Hero subject

A puffed, deep-golden battered poblano chile relleno lying on a pool of chunky red ranchero sauce, split open at one end so molten white cheese oozes out, drizzled with ivory crema and scattered with cilantro, beside cilantro-lime rice.

## Vessel

Wide shallow plate in a rustic glazed ceramic (cream or Talavera-inspired without text).

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, showing the puffed batter and the cheese pull.

## Required visible components

Battered chile with the green stem; ranchero sauce; oozing cheese; crema drizzle; cilantro; rice with green flecks.

## Texture cues

Airy, puffed whipped-egg batter (capeado): irregular pale-to-deep-golden billows with soft blisters and a few thin spots where the dark-green poblano shows through; glossy chunky sauce; stretchy cheese.

## Sauce / component placement

Ranchero under and around the chile, not covering the crust.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Crema drizzle and cilantro.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a lime half; a small bowl of extra sauce.

## Color opportunities

Golden batter, deep red sauce, white cheese and crema, green cilantro and stem.

## Forbidden elements

Tortillas, ground meat, shredded orange cheese on top, refried beans, a breaded (crumb) coating, a dense fine-crumb or sandy coating, a smooth uniform corn-dog-style batter, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Chile on the diagonal at center; rice at the edge may be cropped.

## Ambiguity

Battered stuffed poblano in ranchero sauce. Not an enchilada, not a corn dog, not an egg roll.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
