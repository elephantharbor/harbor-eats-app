# Image / Plating Brief — Chinese Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry over Rice

Package `chinese-tomato-egg-stir-fry`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

Large, soft, golden scrambled-egg curds tangled with glossy red collapsed tomato wedges in a shiny orange-red sauce, spooned over a mound of white jasmine rice and scattered with green scallion rings.

## Vessel

Wide shallow bowl, pale celadon or white glaze.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Yellow egg curds; red tomato wedges with some skins visible; glossy sauce; white rice; scallion greens.

## Texture cues

Fluffy, moist curds; soft juicy tomatoes; a glossy, clinging sauce.

## Sauce / component placement

The sauce coats the eggs and seeps into the edge of the rice.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Sliced scallion greens only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: chopsticks resting on the rim; a small dish of extra scallions.

## Color opportunities

Golden yellow, tomato red, white rice, green scallions.

## Forbidden elements

Fried rice, peas or carrots, meat, shrimp, a dry omelet sheet, sesame seeds, chili oil, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered and filling the frame; legible at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> chinese-tomato-egg-stir-fry` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Tomato-egg stir-fry over rice. Not shakshuka (no skillet of poached eggs) and not egg fried rice.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
