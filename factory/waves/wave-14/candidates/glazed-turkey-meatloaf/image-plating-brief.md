# Image / Plating Brief — Glazed Turkey Meatloaf with Lemon Green Beans

Package `glazed-turkey-meatloaf`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

Two thick slices of juicy turkey meatloaf with a sticky, glossy, caramelized red glaze on top, leaning against each other, beside a pile of blistered bright-green roasted green beans with lemon zest and parsley.

## Vessel

Wide matte white plate; the rest of the glazed loaf on parchment in soft focus behind.

## Camera angle

Low three-quarter, about 25 degrees, so the glaze edge and the moist interior both read.

## Required visible components

Sliced meatloaf interior with herb flecks; glossy glaze; green beans; lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Moist, tender slices; tacky lacquered glaze; crisp-tender beans with browned spots.

## Sauce / component placement

Glaze only on top of the loaf, a little pooling at the slice base.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and lemon zest on the beans.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of extra glaze; a linen napkin.

## Color opportunities

Ruby-red glaze, golden-brown slices, bright green beans, yellow lemon.

## Forbidden elements

Mashed potatoes, gravy, bacon wrap, a loaf pan, ground-beef red color, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Slices centered; the loaf behind may be cropped by the card.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> glazed-turkey-meatloaf` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Glazed turkey meatloaf with green beans. Not a burger patty, not ham.
