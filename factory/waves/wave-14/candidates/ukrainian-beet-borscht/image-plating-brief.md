# Image / Plating Brief — Ukrainian Beet Borscht with White Beans and Dill

Package `ukrainian-beet-borscht`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A wide bowl of deep ruby borscht with visible grated beet, shredded cabbage, potato cubes, and white beans, scattered with bright fresh dill.

## Vessel

Wide white or cream ceramic soup bowl so the ruby color reads; two slices of dark rye on a small board beside it.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees.

## Required visible components

Ruby broth; beet shreds; cabbage ribbons; pale potato cubes; white beans; dill; rye bread.

## Texture cues

Brothy soup with tender vegetables; a light sheen of oil on the surface.

## Sauce / component placement

Broth fills the bowl, vegetables just breaking the surface.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Fresh dill only. NO sour cream dollop (the recipe is dairy-free).

## Environmental styling

Optional: a garlic bulb and a sprig of dill in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Deep ruby, bright green dill, cream potatoes and beans, dark rye.

## Forbidden elements

Sour cream, cream swirl, meat, a purée texture, an orange tomato-soup color, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered, bread at the frame edge inside the central 80 percent.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> ukrainian-beet-borscht` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

A clear, chunky ruby soup. Not a puréed beet soup and not a tomato soup.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
