## Hero subject

A whole roasted branzino with crisp, blistered silver-gold skin and lemon slices peeking from its cavity, resting on a bed of golden sliced potatoes and red onion, glistening with a green-flecked lemon-oregano salmoriglio.

## Vessel

Large oval platter or the rimmed sheet pan lined with parchment.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees, showing the whole fish shape.

## Required visible components

Whole fish (head on is fine but tidy); lemon slices; oregano sprigs; potatoes; onions; salmoriglio.

## Texture cues

Crisp skin, moist flaky flesh at a scored cut, golden potatoes.

## Sauce / component placement

Salmoriglio drizzled over fish and potatoes, extra in a small bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and oregano.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a fish spatula; a halved lemon.

## Color opportunities

Silver-gold skin, yellow lemon, golden potatoes, purple onion, green herbs.

## Forbidden elements

Tomatoes, olives, capers, fennel, butter, raw-looking fish, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Fish on the diagonal across the center; the card crop must keep head and tail or crop symmetrically.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> roasted-whole-branzino-salmoriglio` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Whole roasted Mediterranean fish with potatoes. Not fish fillets in a pan sauce.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
