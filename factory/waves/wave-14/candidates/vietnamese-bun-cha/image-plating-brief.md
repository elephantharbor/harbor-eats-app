## Hero subject

A bowl of amber nước chấm holding four small, round, charred and glistening grilled pork patties (about 2 inches across) and orange pickled carrot ribbons, beside a nest of fine white rice vermicelli and a lush pile of lettuce, mint, cilantro, and Thai basil.

## Vessel

Small deep ceramic bowl for the sauce and patties; a flat plate for noodles; a platter for herbs.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees, so all three parts read.

## Required visible components

4 to 6 small round charred pork patties per bowl, each about 2 inches across and 1/2 inch thick, half submerged in the sauce; sliced chiles and garlic in the sauce; pickled carrot; white vermicelli nest; green herbs and lettuce.

## Texture cues

Smoky charred patties, glossy thin sauce, springy noodles, crisp fresh herbs.

## Sauce / component placement

Patties half submerged in the bowl of nước chấm.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

The herb plate itself.

## Environmental styling

Optional: chopsticks; lime wedges.

## Color opportunities

Charred brown, amber sauce, orange carrot, white noodles, bright green herbs, red chile.

## Forbidden elements

One or two large flat patties, burger- or steak-like shapes, pork-belly slices (not in this recipe), hot broth, beef pho slices, peanuts, fried spring rolls, skewers, tofu, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Sauce bowl at center-left with noodles and herbs right; all inside the central 80 percent.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> vietnamese-bun-cha` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Bún chả dipping-noodle meal. Not pho, not a noodle salad bowl.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
