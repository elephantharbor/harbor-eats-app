## Hero subject

A thick wedge of deep-golden, crisp-edged potato rösti showing its shredded texture, topped with pinkish flaked hot-smoked trout and a tangle of arugula, cucumber, radish, and dill.

## Vessel

Wide matte off-white plate; the rest of the rösti on a wooden board in soft focus behind.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, catching the crisp cut edge.

## Required visible components

Golden rösti crust and cut side; flaked trout; arugula; cucumber and radish slices; capers; dill and chives; a lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Lacy, crisp potato shreds; moist flaked fish; glossy dressed greens.

## Sauce / component placement

Only the vinaigrette sheen on the salad.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Snipped chives and dill.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of capers; a linen napkin.

## Color opportunities

Golden-brown, coral-pink trout, green arugula and dill, pink-white radish.

## Forbidden elements

Sour cream or crème fraîche dollops, butter, a fried egg, cheese, smoked salmon lox slices (use flaked hot-smoked trout), hash browns in a pile, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Wedge at the center of the frame; the board in the upper third may be cropped by the card.

A large Swiss potato cake with smoked trout. Not latkes, not a hash, not a pizza.
