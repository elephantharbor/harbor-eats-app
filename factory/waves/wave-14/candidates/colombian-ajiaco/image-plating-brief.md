## Hero subject

A wide bowl of thick, pale golden chicken and potato soup with shredded chicken, chunks of yellow potato, and a piece of corn on the cob standing in it, topped with a dollop of sour cream, a scatter of capers, and fanned avocado slices.

## Vessel

Rustic glazed clay or terracotta bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Thick golden broth; shredded chicken; potatoes; corn piece; cream; capers; avocado.

## Texture cues

Thick, creamy broth from dissolved potatoes; tender chicken; glossy avocado.

## Sauce / component placement

Soup fills the bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Sour cream, capers, cilantro, avocado.

## Environmental styling

Optional: small bowls of capers and cream alongside.

## Color opportunities

Pale gold, yellow corn, white cream, green avocado and cilantro.

## Forbidden elements

Noodles, tortilla strips, clear broth, rice, text, logos, hands.

Colombian ajiaco. Not chicken noodle soup, not tortilla soup.
