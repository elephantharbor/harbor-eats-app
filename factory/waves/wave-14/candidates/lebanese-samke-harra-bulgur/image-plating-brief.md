## Hero subject

A thick cod fillet under a creamy ivory tahini blanket, lightly golden and bubbled at the edges, speckled with a red-and-green chile, garlic, and cilantro topping, resting on a toasty orange-tinted bulgur pilaf.

## Vessel

Wide shallow ceramic plate in a warm sand or terracotta glaze.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Ivory tahini with golden spots; red chile flecks and green cilantro; a white fish edge or flake; coarse #3/#4 bulgur grains; a lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Silky sauce, flaky fish, and coarse #3/#4 cracked-wheat bulgur: irregular, angular, larger, slightly darker tan grains with visible texture, separate and chewy.

## Sauce / component placement

Tahini sauce pooling slightly around the fish into the bulgur.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro leaves and a lemon wedge.

## Environmental styling

Optional: the baking dish with the remaining fillets at the frame edge.

## Color opportunities

Ivory, red, green, golden-orange grain, yellow lemon.

## Forbidden elements

Walnuts, pine nuts, tomato sauce, yogurt, pita stacks, fried fish, couscous-looking fine grain, fine round pearl-like grains, text, logos, hands.

## Ambiguity

Tahini-baked spicy fish over bulgur. Not a cream sauce, not a curry.
