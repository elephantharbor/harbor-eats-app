# Image / Plating Brief — Philly Cheesesteaks with Griddled Onions and Provolone

Package `philly-cheesesteaks`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A toasted hoagie roll overflowing with chopped, browned shaved steak and sweet golden onions under a glossy blanket of melted provolone, cut in half to show the filling.

## Vessel

Rustic parchment-lined metal tray or a wooden board.

## Camera angle

Low three-quarter, about 20 degrees, looking into the cut end.

## Required visible components

The cut cheesesteak cross-section; browned onions; melted cheese; toasted roll edge; two pickle spears; a few pepper rings.

## Texture cues

Juicy chopped beef, molten cheese, soft roll with a crisp toasted face.

## Sauce / component placement

No sauce; the cheese binds the filling.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

None beyond the pickles and pepper rings.

## Environmental styling

Optional: the second half in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Golden roll, brown beef, pale melted cheese, green pickle, yellow pepper rings.

## Forbidden elements

Bright orange cheese sauce, lettuce, tomato, sesame-seeded bun, fries, green bell pepper strips, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

The cut end centered and filling the frame; legible at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> philly-cheesesteaks` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

A Philly cheesesteak. Not a burger, not a sub with cold cuts.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
