## Hero subject

A wide bowl of thick, creamy ivory clam chowder with visible tender clams and diced potatoes, topped with crisp bacon bits, bright green chives, and a few cracked black pepper flecks, with a scatter of oyster crackers beside it.

## Vessel

White or navy-rimmed ceramic soup bowl on a plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Creamy chowder; clearly visible chopped clam pieces (small tan-grey, ruffled, chewy-looking bits) scattered on and in the chowder among the potato cubes; bacon; chives; oyster crackers.

## Texture cues

Thick creamy body, tender potatoes and clams, crisp bacon and crackers.

## Sauce / component placement

Chowder fills the bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Bacon, chives, black pepper.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a soup spoon and a small bowl of extra crackers.

Ivory, pink-brown bacon, green chives, golden crackers.

## Forbidden elements
