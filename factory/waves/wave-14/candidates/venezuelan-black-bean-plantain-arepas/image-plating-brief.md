# Image / Plating Brief — Venezuelan Arepas with Black Beans, Sweet Plantains, and Avocado

Package `venezuelan-black-bean-plantain-arepas`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

Two split golden arepas with crusty, spotted-brown surfaces, generously stuffed with dark glossy black beans, caramelized plantain slices, and pale-green avocado, a third arepa behind.

## Vessel

Rustic wooden board or a matte dark plate.

## Camera angle

Low three-quarter, about 25 degrees, to show the open pocket and filling layers.

## Required visible components

The arepa crust and the white corn interior at the split; black beans; caramelized plantains; avocado; cilantro; a lime wedge.

## Texture cues

Crisp-crusted corn cake, steamy soft interior, glossy beans, sticky plantains, creamy avocado.

## Sauce / component placement

None poured; the beans are thick, not soupy.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro leaves and lime wedges only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of extra beans and plantains in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Golden arepa, black beans, amber-caramel plantain, green avocado and cilantro.

## Forbidden elements

Shredded cheese, crema or sour cream, meat, chicken, flour tortillas, a taco shape, pupusas (sealed discs), text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

The front stuffed arepa at the center of the 4:3 frame; still legible as a stuffed corn cake at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> venezuelan-black-bean-plantain-arepas` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Split, stuffed arepas. Not tacos, not pupusas, not English muffins or burger buns.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
