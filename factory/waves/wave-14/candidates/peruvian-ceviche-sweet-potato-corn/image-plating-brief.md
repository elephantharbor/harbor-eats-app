## Hero subject

A generous mound of glistening, translucent-to-opaque white cubed flounder ceviche with only tiny, sparse flecks of minced red chile, cilantro, and translucent magenta red-onion slivers, in a little milky lime juice, on a pale-green Bibb leaf.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white ceramic bowl or rimmed plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, close enough to read the cube texture.

## Required visible components

Cubed white fish; tiny sparse minced red-chile flecks (no diced red cubes); red onion slivers; cilantro sprigs; a small pool of lime juice; Bibb lettuce; two orange sweet potato rounds; one or two yellow corn wheels.

## Texture cues

Moist, opaque fish cubes with a slightly translucent center; crisp onion; tender sweet potato.

## Sauce / component placement

A shallow pool of lime cure (leche de tigre) around the fish, not a soup.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro sprigs only.

## Environmental styling

None on the plate; plain linen or tabletop only. No lime halves or wedges.

## Color opportunities

White fish, magenta onion, green cilantro and lettuce, orange sweet potato, yellow corn.

## Forbidden elements

Lime halves or wedges, bright red diced cubes that read as tomato or red bell pepper, shrimp, avocado, tomato, tortilla chips, toasted corn nuts, cooked or seared fish, fried items, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Ceviche mound at center, sweet potato and corn inside the central 75 percent so the 4:3 crop and 640x480 card keep all three.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> peruvian-ceviche-sweet-potato-corn` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Peruvian lime-cured fish with sweet potato and corn. Not a poke bowl and not a shrimp cocktail.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
