## Hero subject

A stack of two golden-brown, blister-spotted whole-wheat parathas, the top one cut into a wedge to show a pale-gold spiced potato filling flecked with green cilantro and chile.

## Vessel

Steel or ceramic thali-style plate, or a wide matte plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, with the cut wedge facing the camera.

## Required visible components

Brown-spotted paratha surface; the cut layer and filling; a small bowl of vivid green chutney; a small bowl of kachumber (cucumber, tomato, red onion).

## Texture cues

Flaky, blistered, crisp-flecked bread; soft potato filling; glossy smooth chutney; crunchy diced salad.

## Sauce / component placement

Chutney in its own small bowl with a spoon; not drizzled over the bread.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

A few cilantro leaves on the kachumber.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a pinch bowl of extra chiles; a cloth-lined basket at the edge.

## Color opportunities

Golden-brown bread, pale gold filling with green flecks, bright green chutney, red, green, and white salad.

## Forbidden elements

Butter pats, ghee pools, yogurt or raita, pickle (achar) jars, curry bowls, naan bubbles, deep-fried puris, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

The cut paratha at center-left, chutney and salad bowls inside the central 80 percent for the card.

## Ambiguity

Stuffed potato flatbread. Not a quesadilla, not naan, not a pancake stack.
