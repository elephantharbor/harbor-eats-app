# Image / Plating Brief — Chicken and Andouille Gumbo with a Dark Roux

Package `chicken-andouille-gumbo`, revision `freeze-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at freeze-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A deep bowl of glossy, dark mahogany-brown gumbo with shredded chicken and coins of browned andouille, a neat mound of white rice in the center, scattered with green scallions and parsley.

## Vessel

Deep wide bowl in white or pale cream so the dark gumbo reads.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees.

## Required visible components

Dark brown broth; andouille slices; shredded chicken; diced celery and pepper; rice mound; scallions; parsley; a bottle-free hot-sauce dash in a small dish.

## Texture cues

Silky, slightly thick broth with a sheen; tender shreds; browned sausage edges.

## Sauce / component placement

Gumbo fills the bowl around the rice; rice peeks above the surface.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallions and parsley.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of hot sauce.

## Color opportunities

Deep brown, white rice, bright green scallions, red-brown sausage.

## Forbidden elements

Shrimp, crab, okra pods, a tomato-red broth, a hot sauce bottle with a label, crackers, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered and filling the frame; legible at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> chicken-andouille-gumbo` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Dark-roux chicken-andouille gumbo. Not chili, not jambalaya.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
