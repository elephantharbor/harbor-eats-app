# Wave-17 rejected ideas

Ideas considered for wave-17 and dropped, with the reason.

## Near-duplicates of live meals

| Idea | Reason |
|---|---|
| Mujadara | Too close to egyptian-koshari (lentils, rice, crisp onions). |
| Ribollita | Too close to white-bean-kale-soup. |
| Shrimp etouffee | Too close to chicken-andouille-gumbo in method and flavor. |
| Misir wat / shiro wat | Too close to ethiopian-atakilt-wat as a berbere stew with injera-style service. |
| Shakshuka-style egg dishes | skillet-shakshuka is live. |
| Tom kha, coconut curries | coconut-chickpea-curry, thai-red-curry-kabocha, and malaysian-curry-laksa already cover coconut curries. |
| Poke-like bowls | hawaiian-ahi-poke-bowl is live. |
| Falafel or chana plates | Chickpea plates are covered by moroccan-chickpea-skillet and the shawarma plate. |
| Pasta e fagioli | Overlaps white-bean-kale-soup and the bean soups. |
| Cheddar mac and cheese, stuffed shells | Overlap baked-ziti-italian-sausage and add to the large Italian-American cheese cluster. |
| Fish pie | Overlaps cottage-pie in format. |
| Salmon cakes | Overlap maryland-crab-cakes-slaw and thai-fish-cakes-cucumber-relish. |
| Loco moco | Overlaps japanese-hambagu (patty with gravy over rice). |
| Mongolian beef, kung pao | Overlap the existing stir-fries; kung pao adds peanuts. |
| Com tam | Overlaps vietnamese-bun-cha and thai-moo-ping-sticky-rice (grilled marinated pork with rice). |
| Tacos al pastor | Overlaps new-mexican-carne-adovada and the pork chile dishes; a vertical spit cannot be done at home. |
| Gambas al ajillo | Too close to lemon-garlic-shrimp-pasta, and to shrimp saganaki in this wave. |

## Peanut dishes (the brief asks for no more)

| Idea | Reason |
|---|---|
| Khao soi | Usually finished with peanuts, and coconut curry noodles are covered by laksa. |
| Nasi lemak | Peanuts and anchovies; sambal rice overlaps ikan bakar in this wave. |
| American peanut dan dan | Replaced by a sesame-paste version. |

## Too long, too much equipment, or a weak fit

| Idea | Reason |
|---|---|
| Lobster rolls | Whole live lobsters are hard to source and costly; the catalog's shellfish gap is better served by crab Louie and cioppino. |
| Red beans and rice | Long soak, overlaps punjabi-rajma-chawal and the bean stews. |
| Pelau | Caramelized-sugar chicken rice is too close to jollof (this wave) and the rice dishes. |
| Jamaican brown stew chicken | Overlaps puerto-rican-pollo-guisado and kedjenou in this wave. |
| Pollo al ajillo | Overlaps chkmeruli in this wave (garlic chicken). |
| Chicken marsala, chicken parm, saltimbocca | Italian-American cluster already large; chicken parm needs a second pan of oil plus an oven. |
| Oyakodon | Overlaps japanese-gyudon (simmered protein and egg over rice). |
| Dakgalbi | Overlaps gochujang-grilled-flank-steak and ojingeo bokkeum in this wave (gochujang stir-fry). |
| Pad kra pao | Overlaps thai-basil-eggplant-stir-fry. |
| Yaki udon | Overlaps the noodle stir-fries; pad see ew covers wide noodles in this wave. |
| Anda curry | Overlaps chinese-tomato-egg-stir-fry and the tomato-based vegetarian curries; jollof with eggs in this wave already covers an egg-and-tomato dinner. |
| Camarones a la diabla | Overlaps shrimp saganaki in this wave (shrimp in a red sauce). |
| Chakalaka | A relish rather than a dinner. |
