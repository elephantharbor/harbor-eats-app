## Hero subject

A wide bowl brimming with open clams and black mussels, pink shrimp, and white cod chunks in a glossy red tomato broth with soft fennel, scattered with parsley, a slice of toasted sourdough leaning on the rim.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white ceramic bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so the open shells and broth read.

## Required visible components

Open littleneck clams; open blue-black mussels with orange meat; pink curled shrimp; white flaking cod chunks; red tomato broth with fennel slices; chopped parsley; one golden toasted sourdough slice.

## Texture cues

Plump shellfish, flaky fish, thin brothy sauce, crunchy toast.

## Sauce / component placement

Thin glossy red tomato-wine broth filling the bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped parsley only.

## Environmental styling

Only the bowl, a spoon, and a linen napkin.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.
