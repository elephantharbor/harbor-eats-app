# Image / Plating Brief — Japanese Chicken Nanban with Tartar Sauce, Shredded Cabbage, and Rice

Package `japanese-chicken-nanban`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

Sliced golden chicken thigh glossy with sweet-sour sauce and blanketed with a thick stripe of chunky white egg tartar sauce flecked with parsley, beside a tall fluffy pile of finely shredded green cabbage and a mound of white rice.

## Vessel

Rectangular off-white ceramic plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, so the tartar and coating read.

## Required visible components

One sliced chicken thigh with a thin golden egg coating, glossy with amber sauce; chunky white tartar with egg pieces and green parsley; fine pale green shredded cabbage; white short-grain rice.

## Texture cues

Tender-crisp coating, juicy chicken, creamy chunky tartar, crisp cabbage.

## Sauce / component placement

Amber nanban sauce soaked into the coating; tartar spooned on top.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley in the tartar only.

## Environmental styling

Only the plate, chopsticks, and a linen napkin.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Golden chicken, white tartar, green cabbage, white rice.

## Forbidden elements

Panko crumbs, katsu sauce, lemon slices, miso soup, pickles side dishes, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Plate centered, food filling about 75 percent of the frame; the tartar must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> japanese-chicken-nanban` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Japanese chicken nanban with tartar. Not chicken katsu, not karaage, not fried chicken with gravy.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
