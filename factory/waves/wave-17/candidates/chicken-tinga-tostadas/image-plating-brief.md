## Hero subject

Three crisp golden corn tostadas heaped with brick-red shredded chipotle chicken and soft onions, topped with shredded lettuce, white drizzles of crema, crumbled queso fresco, and avocado slices, a lime wedge beside them.

## Vessel

Large off-white ceramic platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so the height of the toppings reads.

## Required visible components

Flat crisp golden tostadas; saucy brick-red shredded chicken with onion strands; pale green shredded lettuce; white crema drizzle; white queso fresco crumbles; green avocado slices; lime wedges.

## Texture cues

Shatter-crisp shells, moist shredded chicken, creamy toppings.

## Sauce / component placement

Smoky red tinga sauce clinging to the chicken; crema drizzled on top.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Lime wedges only.

## Environmental styling

Only the platter and a linen napkin.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Golden shells, brick-red chicken, white crema and cheese, green lettuce and avocado.

## Forbidden elements

Soft tacos, beans, rice, chips, salsa bowls, sour-cream dollops, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Platter centered, tostadas filling about 75 percent of the frame; the shredded chicken must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> chicken-tinga-tostadas` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Chicken tinga tostadas. Not tacos, not nachos, not enchiladas.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
