# Image / Plating Brief — Penne alla Vodka with Basil

Package `penne-alla-vodka`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A shallow bowl of penne coated in a glossy blush-pink vodka sauce, with sauce clinging inside the ridged tubes, topped with fine grated cheese and torn basil.

## Vessel

Shallow off-white ceramic pasta bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, so the ridged tubes and sauce read.

## Required visible components

Ridged penne in an even blush-orange-pink cream sauce; fine pale grated cheese; a few torn green basil leaves.

## Texture cues

Glossy creamy sauce clinging to al dente ridged pasta.

## Sauce / component placement

Blush-pink tomato-cream sauce coating every piece; no pooling.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Torn basil and grated cheese only.

## Environmental styling

Only the bowl, a fork, and a linen napkin.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Blush-pink sauce, pale cheese, bright green basil.

## Forbidden elements

Meat, sausage, shrimp, chicken, bread, salad, wine, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered, food filling about 75 percent of the frame; the ridges and sauce must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> penne-alla-vodka` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Penne alla vodka. Not marinara, not Alfredo, not baked ziti.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
