## Hero subject

A generous composed plate of golden pan-fried tempeh slabs, halved hard-cooked eggs, boiled potato cubes, bright green beans, soft cabbage, bean sprouts, and cucumber slices, with warm tan peanut sauce spooned over the middle.

## Vessel

Large off-white ceramic plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so each component and the sauce texture read.

## Required visible components

Golden-brown tempeh slabs with visible whole soybeans in the cut face; egg halves with firm yellow yolks; pale gold potato cubes; bright green beans; wilted pale green cabbage; white bean sprouts; green-edged cucumber rounds; thick tan peanut sauce.

## Texture cues

Crisp-edged tempeh, tender potatoes, crisp-tender beans, glossy thick sauce.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce spooned across the center so some of every vegetable stays uncovered and visible; no separate sauce bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

None; the peanut sauce is the finish.

## Environmental styling

Only the plate, a fork and spoon, and a linen napkin.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Golden tempeh, yellow yolks, gold potatoes, bright green beans, pale green cabbage, white sprouts, tan peanut sauce.

## Forbidden elements

Shrimp crackers, fried shallots, rice or rice cakes, tofu, satay skewers, meat, lettuce leaves, lime wedges, chopped peanuts, side bowls, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Plate centered, filling about 75 percent of the frame; tempeh and egg halves must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> indonesian-gado-gado-tempeh` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Gado-gado: boiled vegetables, eggs, and tempeh under warm peanut sauce. Not satay, not pad thai, not a niçoise salad.

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
