# Image / Plating Brief — Sicilian Pasta alla Norma with Golden Eggplant and Ricotta Salata

Package `sicilian-pasta-alla-norma`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A generous bowl of rigatoni in glossy red tomato sauce, studded and topped with golden-brown eggplant cubes, showered with snowy grated ricotta salata and torn green basil.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white ceramic pasta bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees, so the rigatoni tubes, eggplant cubes, and cheese read.

## Required visible components

Ridged rigatoni tubes; golden-brown eggplant cubes with soft creamy interiors; red tomato sauce with garlic slices; white fluffy grated ricotta salata; whole and torn basil leaves.

## Texture cues

Caramelized eggplant edges, glossy sauce clinging to the pasta, dry snowy cheese.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce coating the pasta, not pooled; extra eggplant piled on top.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Grated ricotta salata and fresh basil only.

## Environmental styling

Only the bowl and a fork on linen.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Tomato red, golden-brown eggplant, white cheese, bright green basil.

## Forbidden elements

Breaded or layered eggplant, melted mozzarella, meat, meatballs, olives, capers, bread, side salad, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered, filling about 70 percent of the frame; eggplant cubes must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> sicilian-pasta-alla-norma` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Pasta alla Norma: rigatoni with tomato, golden eggplant cubes, and ricotta salata. Not eggplant parmesan, not baked ziti, not puttanesca.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
