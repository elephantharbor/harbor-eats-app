## Hero subject

A shallow bowl of wide golden egg noodles topped with a generous spoonful of creamy pale-tan mushroom stroganoff, deeply browned mushroom slices visible, scattered with fresh dill.

## Vessel

Shallow off-white stoneware bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so the browned mushroom faces and the noodle ribbons read.

## Required visible components

Wide flat ribbon egg noodles; thick browned cremini slices; creamy pale-tan sauce with a faint paprika blush; soft onion; chopped green dill.

## Texture cues

Glossy creamy sauce, caramelized mushroom edges, tender buttered noodles.

## Sauce / component placement

Stroganoff spooned over the center of the noodles, sauce clinging rather than pooling.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped fresh dill only.

## Environmental styling

Only the bowl, a fork, and a linen napkin.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Golden noodles, deep brown mushrooms, pale cream-tan sauce, bright green dill.

## Forbidden elements

Beef or any meat, rice, mashed potatoes, bread, parsley, pickles, a dollop of sour cream on top, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered, filling about 70 percent of the frame; the mushroom slices must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> russian-mushroom-stroganoff` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Mushroom stroganoff: browned mushrooms in a sour cream sauce over egg noodles. Not beef stroganoff, not fettuccine Alfredo, not goulash.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
