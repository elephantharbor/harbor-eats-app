## Hero subject

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white ceramic bowl.

## Camera angle

High three-quarter, about 50 degrees.

## Required visible components

Bright white soft tofu cubes with clean edges; glossy red sauce with red oil beading at the edges; small dark diced shiitake (clearly mushroom pieces, not crumbled meat); a few black fermented beans; Sichuan peppercorn specks; sliced green scallion; white jasmine rice.

Silky soft tofu, thick glossy sauce, fluffy rice.

Mapo on one side, rice on the other, touching in the middle; thick, not soupy.

Sliced scallion greens and ground Sichuan peppercorn only.
