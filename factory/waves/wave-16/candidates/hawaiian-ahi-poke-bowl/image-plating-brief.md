# Image / Plating Brief — Hawaiian Shoyu Ahi Poke Bowls with Seasoned Rice, Cucumber, Avocado, and Edamame

## Hero subject

A generous bowl of glossy deep-red ahi poke cubes over white short-grain rice, with neat sections of diced cucumber, fanned avocado, and bright green edamame, topped with nori strips and sesame seeds.

## Vessel

Wide off-white ceramic bowl.

## Camera angle

Near-overhead, about 70 degrees, so every section of the bowl reads.

## Required visible components

Deep ruby-red raw tuna cubes glossy with shoyu and flecked with sliced sweet onion, scallion, and chile flakes; white slightly sticky rice; diced pale green cucumber; fanned green avocado slices; bright green edamame; thin black nori strips; white and tan sesame seeds.

## Texture cues

Silky glistening raw tuna, creamy avocado, crisp cucumber, tender rice.

## Sauce / component placement

Dressing glossing the tuna only; no sauce drizzled over the bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Nori strips and toasted sesame seeds only.

## Environmental styling

Only the bowl and chopsticks on linen.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, sauce bowls, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Ruby-red tuna, white rice, greens from cucumber, avocado, and edamame, black nori.

## Forbidden elements

Cooked or seared tuna, salmon, spicy mayo drizzle, masago or roe, pickled ginger, wasabi, mango, seaweed salad, crab, side bowls, drinks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered, filling about 75 percent of the frame; the tuna cubes must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> hawaiian-ahi-poke-bowl` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Hawaiian shoyu ahi poke over rice. Not a sushi roll, not seared tuna, not ceviche.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, watermark, or hands.
