## FAILs

### Roasted Cabbage Steaks with Cashew Romesco — E

The oven is turned to 425 F and the cabbage goes in as part of the same setup. The 30-minute roast is then counted as if the oven were already hot. The 15-minute prep overlaps the roast (the sauce is blended while the cabbage cooks). It is not a preheat before the food goes in. A cold oven at the bake step, with no preheat minutes and no earlier overlap, fails E. Cod in this wave states the oven heat inside the prep before the roast starts. That is the pattern this package needs.

K, L1, and L2 pass. Dietary eligibility and allergens pass (`cashews_only_ok`, plant-based, no almonds). Classification passes (moderate / simple).

### Sichuan Green Bean and Mushroom Stir-Fry — B

Water is one 2.75-cup ingredient. The note splits it: 2 1/4 cups for the rice, and of the remaining 1/2 cup, 2 tbsp for the cornstarch and the rest for the sauce. Step 1 tells the cook to stir the sauce ingredients "and water" with no amount. Step 2 then takes 2 1/4 cups of that same water for the rice. Following the steps in order puts the rice water into the sauce. The split has to be in the steps, not only in the note.

K, L1, and L2 pass. Dietary eligibility and allergens pass (soy, wheat, sesame; shrimp excluded by the chili-bean-paste contract; HH001 true). Stir-fry is classified as stir-fry, not as frying. Classification passes (involved / standard).
