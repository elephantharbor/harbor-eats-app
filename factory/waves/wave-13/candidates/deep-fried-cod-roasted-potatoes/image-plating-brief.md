# Image / Plating Brief — Deep-Fried Cod with Oven-Roasted Potatoes

## Hero subject

Two pieces of deep-fried cod with a visibly rough, golden batter crust, next to a pile of oven-roasted potato wedges with browned edges.

## Vessel

Warm off-white dinner plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 30 degrees, so the crust edge and the potato wedges both read.

## Required visible components

Golden battered cod, crisp-edged potato wedges, a lemon wedge, chopped parsley, and a small pool of vinegar or a lemon-bright sheen. No second protein.

## Texture cues

Irregular batter crust, flaky white fish at one broken edge, rough roasted potato skin.

## Sauce / component placement

No gravy. Lemon and parsley on the fish. Vinegar is a small spoonful or a tiny ramekin, not a puddle under the food.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and lemon only.

## Environmental styling

Optional lemon half in soft focus. No newspaper cone and no malt bottle with a label.

## Color opportunities

Golden crust, brown potato edges, white fish, yellow lemon, green parsley.

## Forbidden elements

Butter, tartar sauce, ketchup, peas, french fries from a fryer, coleslaw, shrimp, logos, text.

## Crop strategy

Fish and potatoes inside the center 70 percent so a 4:3 crop keeps both. The crust must still read at 640x480.

Master target is WebP 1200x900. Card target is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops. Export later with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py <source> factory/waves/wave-13/candidates/deep-fried-cod-roasted-potatoes deep-fried-cod-roasted-potatoes`.

## Ambiguity

Battered cod and roasted wedges. Not fish tacos and not a blackened fillet.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
