## Hero subject

Four spring rolls, two cut in half so the cabbage, carrot, and mushroom filling shows, with a small bowl of dark soy-lime dip.

## Vessel

Off-white plate and a small dipping bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, low enough that the cut faces read.

## Required visible components

Blistered golden wrappers, shredded cabbage, orange carrot, brown shiitake, pale vermicelli, dark dip.

## Texture cues

Dry blistered wrapper, not an oily bubble crust. Moist filling.

## Sauce / component placement

Dip beside the rolls, not poured over them.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

A few chile rings on the dip, from the recipe. No mint sprig unless it is clearly not a second dish.

## Environmental styling

Optional lime half. No fish-sauce bottle.

## Color opportunities

Gold wrappers, orange carrot, green cabbage, brown mushrooms, amber dip.

## Forbidden elements

Shrimp, crab, pork, a pot of bubbling oil, rice paper summer rolls that look wet and translucent, peanut sauce, text, logos.

## Crop strategy

Cut faces in the center third. The dip stays in frame at 640x480.

Master target is WebP 1200x900. Card target is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops. Export later with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py <source> factory/waves/wave-13/candidates/air-fried-vegetable-spring-rolls air-fried-vegetable-spring-rolls`.

## Ambiguity

Crisp baked-looking rolls from an air fryer. Not a fryer basket and not a fresh summer roll.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
