# Image / Plating Brief — Shallow-Fried Green Tomatoes with White Beans and Slaw

Package `shallow-fried-green-tomatoes`, revision `author-r1`. Written before any image exists. Image pass complete 2026-10-11. Standard: `factory/IMAGE-SPEC.md` and `factory/tools/image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A stack of two or three golden cornmeal-crusted green tomato slices, beans to one side, bright slaw to the other.

## Vessel

Off-white dinner plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter so the crust edge and the green interior of a cut slice both show.

## Required visible components

Golden crust, pale green tomato interior, white beans with a red-brown paprika tint, shredded cabbage slaw.

## Texture cues

Coarse cornmeal crust, juicy tomato, glossy beans, raw crunchy cabbage.

## Sauce / component placement

No white sauce. The slaw is undressed-looking but lightly vinegared, not swimming.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallion greens already in the slaw. Nothing else.

## Environmental styling

Optional extra tomato slice in soft focus. No bacon, no buttermilk pitcher.

## Color opportunities

Gold crust, green tomato, white beans, pale green cabbage, red paprika.

## Forbidden elements

Mayonnaise, ranch, bacon, shrimp, fried chicken, wheat-flour sandwich bread, text, logos.

## Crop strategy

The tomato stack is the center. Beans and slaw stay in frame at card size.

Master target is WebP 1200x900. Card target is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops. Export later with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py <source> factory/waves/wave-13/candidates/shallow-fried-green-tomatoes shallow-fried-green-tomatoes`.

## Ambiguity

A tomato plate with beans and slaw. Not a shrimp boil and not a sandwich.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
