# Image / Plating Brief — Grilled Mackerel with Fennel and Orange

Package `grilled-mackerel-fennel-orange`, revision `author-r1`. Written before any image exists. Slot status: pending_image_pass. Standard: `factory/IMAGE-SPEC.md` and `factory/tools/image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

One whole grilled mackerel, skin marked, with fennel wedges and a bright orange-parsley relish.

## Vessel

Oval off-white platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter so the whole fish reads, not a top-down fillet shot.

## Required visible components

Mackerel with intact skin and grill marks, fennel wedges, orange pieces, green parsley.

## Texture cues

Charred skin, oily sheen that looks like the fish, not a glaze, tender fennel.

## Sauce / component placement

Relish spooned along the fish, not a cream pool.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and orange from the relish. Lemon wedge from the recipe.

## Environmental styling

Optional fennel frond. No olive bowl.

## Color opportunities

Silver and charred skin, pale fennel, orange, green.

## Forbidden elements

Butter, olives, capers, a fillet that could be swordfish, shrimp, text, logos.

## Crop strategy

The whole fish stays inside the center so the card does not cut off the head and tail in a confusing way. If the frame is tight, keep the marked skin and the relish.

Master target is WebP 1200x900. Card target is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops. Export later with `python3 factory/tools/export_meal_webps.py <source> factory/waves/wave-13/candidates/grilled-mackerel-fennel-orange grilled-mackerel-fennel-orange`.

## Ambiguity

Whole grilled mackerel. Not swordfish and not salmon.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
