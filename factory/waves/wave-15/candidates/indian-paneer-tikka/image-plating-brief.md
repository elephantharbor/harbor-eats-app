# Image / Plating Brief — Paneer Tikka with Peppers and Onions, Mint Chutney, and Naan

## Hero subject

Two skewers of orange-red marinated paneer cubes with charred edges, alternating with blistered red and green bell pepper squares and red onion, beside folded naan, a small bowl of bright green mint chutney, and kachumber salad.

## Vessel

Rectangular dark slate or white platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

Paneer cubes coated in an orange-red marinade with black char spots; red and green pepper squares; red onion petals; two naan halves; a bowl of smooth green chutney; diced cucumber-tomato salad; lemon wedges.

## Texture cues

Charred spots, soft paneer, blistered peppers, creamy chutney.

## Sauce / component placement

Chutney in its own bowl; no gravy.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro leaves and lemon wedges.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of chaat masala in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Orange-red paneer, black char, red and green peppers, bright green chutney, golden naan.

## Forbidden elements

Chicken pieces, tofu (the cubes must read as paneer with a firm, slightly crumbly cut face), creamy orange gravy, rice, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Platter fills about 80 percent of the frame; the paneer cubes and green chutney must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> indian-paneer-tikka` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Paneer tikka: charred marinated paneer skewers with mint chutney. Not chicken tikka, not tofu, not tikka masala curry.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
