# Image / Plating Brief — Greek Gigantes Plaki: Baked Giant Beans in Tomato and Dill

Package `greek-gigantes-plaki`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A rustic skillet of oversized white giant beans in thick, jammy, edge-caramelized tomato sauce, flecked with dill and parsley.

## Vessel

Black cast-iron or terracotta oven dish, with a small plate of cucumber salad and sliced bread beside it.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so the bean size and caramelized edges read.

## Required visible components

Very large flat white beans, clearly bigger than ordinary beans; thick red sauce; darker caramelized edges; chopped dill and parsley; a glossy olive-oil sheen; cucumber slices; crusty bread.

## Texture cues

Creamy plump beans, jammy sauce, crusty caramelized edges.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce thick, coating the beans; no broth.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped dill and parsley only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bottle of olive oil in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Tomato red, creamy white beans, green herbs, dark edges.

## Forbidden elements

Feta crumbles, chickpeas, small navy beans, sausage, ground meat, eggs, cheese crust, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Dish centered, filling about 70 percent of the frame; the beans' giant size must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> greek-gigantes-plaki` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Gigantes plaki: giant white beans baked in tomato sauce. Not baked beans in sweet sauce, not shakshuka, not chili.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
