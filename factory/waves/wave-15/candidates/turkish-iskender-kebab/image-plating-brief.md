# Image / Plating Brief — Turkish İskender Kebab: Sliced Lamb over Pide with Tomato Sauce, Brown Butter, and Yogurt

## Hero subject

Thin slices of browned lamb piled over cubes of toasted bread soaked with glossy red tomato sauce, glistening with poured brown butter, with a big dollop of white garlic yogurt, a blistered long green pepper, and roasted cherry tomatoes.

## Vessel

Oval copper-toned or white plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

A heap of thin seared lamb slices; bread cubes visible underneath with red sauce soaking in; shining brown butter with toasted flecks; a thick white yogurt mound; one or two blistered long green peppers; burst cherry tomatoes; parsley.

## Texture cues

Crisp-edged bread softening under sauce, tender lamb, nutty butter sheen, cool yogurt.

## Sauce / component placement

Tomato sauce over the bread and lamb, brown butter pooling at the edges.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and the blistered peppers.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small copper pan of brown butter mid-pour in soft focus (no hands).

## Color opportunities

Red tomato sauce, browned lamb, golden butter, white yogurt, green pepper.

## Forbidden elements

Pita wrap, rice pilaf, skewered kebabs, tzatziki with dill, lettuce, fries, text, logos, hands.

Plate centered; the bread cubes, yogurt, and butter sheen must read at 640x480.

## Ambiguity

İskender kebab: sliced lamb over pide with tomato sauce, brown butter, and yogurt. Not a gyro wrap, not a shish kebab, not shawarma.
