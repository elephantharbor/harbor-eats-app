## Hero subject

A heap of browned okra coins tossed with soft onion and tomato, beside two folded whole-wheat chapatis with brown blister spots.

## Vessel

Off-white stoneware plate, slightly rimmed.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, so okra coins show their cut star-shaped faces.

## Required visible components

Okra coins with visible seeds and star-shaped cross-sections and browned edges; softened onion; tomato pieces; chopped cilantro; two chapatis folded in quarters.

## Texture cues

Crisp-edged, matte okra (not slimy or glossy); soft speckled chapatis.

## Sauce / component placement

Dry masala clinging to the okra; no gravy pool.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped cilantro only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of lemon wedges in soft focus at the edge.

## Color opportunities

Deep green okra, browned edges, red tomato, golden onion, tan-speckled chapati.

## Forbidden elements

Gravy or curry sauce, cream, paneer, naan with char bubbles, ghee pool, rice, green beans or zucchini that could be mistaken for okra, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Okra pile left of center and chapatis right, both inside the 4:3 crop; okra coins must read as okra at 640x480.
