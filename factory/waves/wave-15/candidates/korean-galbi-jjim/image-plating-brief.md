## Hero subject

Glossy, dark mahogany braised short ribs on the bone piled with rounded chunks of amber radish, orange carrots, and whole shiitake caps, glazed and scattered with scallions and sesame seeds.

## Vessel

Shallow wide earthenware or brass Korean serving bowl, with a small bowl of rice beside it.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Three or four short ribs with exposed bone; amber-glazed radish chunks with rounded edges; carrot chunks; whole shiitake caps; scallion slices; a few sesame seeds; a bowl of white rice.

## Texture cues

Lacquered glaze, meltingly tender meat, soft vegetables.

## Sauce / component placement

Glaze coating everything with a shallow pool in the bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallions and sesame seeds.

## Environmental styling

Optional: small banchan dishes in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Deep mahogany, amber radish, orange carrot, brown shiitake, green scallion, white rice.

## Forbidden elements

Grill marks, lettuce wraps, kimchi on the platter, chestnuts, red chile sauce, noodles, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered; bone-in ribs and radish must read at 640x480.

Galbi-jjim: braised soy-glazed short ribs with radish and carrots. Not grilled galbi, not beef stew with potatoes.
