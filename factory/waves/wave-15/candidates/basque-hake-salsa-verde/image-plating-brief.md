## Hero subject

A white hake fillet in a pale green, parsley-flecked silky sauce with bright green peas, over chunks of yellow boiled potato.

## Vessel

Shallow wide earthenware cazuela or off-white bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

One flaky white fillet; light green sauce with lots of chopped parsley; scattered peas; three or four yellow potato chunks; thin garlic slices; a lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Silky lightly thickened sauce, flaky fish, tender potatoes.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce pooled around the fish and potatoes; not a thick puree.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped parsley.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small glass of white wine in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Pale green sauce, bright green peas and parsley, white fish, yellow potatoes.

## Forbidden elements

Clams or shellfish, cream sauce, pesto, breaded fish, rice, salmon, text, logos, hands.
