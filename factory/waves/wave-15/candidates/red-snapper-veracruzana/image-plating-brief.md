## Hero subject

A red snapper fillet with its pink-red skin edge visible, lying in a chunky red tomato sauce studded with green olives, capers, and pickled jalapeño rings, with white rice alongside.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

One snapper fillet with flaky white flesh and a strip of red skin; chunky tomato sauce; halved green olives; small capers; a few jalapeño rings; a scoop of white rice; parsley; two lime wedges.

## Texture cues

Flaky moist fish; glossy chunky sauce; separate rice grains.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce pooled under and over part of the fish, leaving the skin edge visible; rice beside, not drowned.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped parsley and lime wedges only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of extra olives in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Tomato red, olive green, white fish, pink-red skin, white rice, bright lime.

## Forbidden elements

Breaded or fried fish, a whole fish with head, tortillas, cheese, avocado, cilantro bunch, black olives, shrimp, text, logos, hands.
