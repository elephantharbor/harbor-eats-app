## Hero subject

A shallow bowl of coarse, creamy mashed fava beans with many whole plump fava beans clearly visible, pooled with golden-green olive oil, dusted with cumin, topped with diced red tomato, chopped parsley, and chopped jalapeño, with warm pita alongside.

## Vessel

Shallow off-white or speckled ceramic bowl on a board with a small plate of cucumber spears, halved radishes, and olives.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 45 degrees, so the olive-oil pool, the whole beans, and toppings read.

## Required visible components

Creamy tan-brown fava mash with several whole oval fava beans with smooth skins on top; a glossy olive-oil pool; a dusting of cumin; diced tomato; parsley; chopped green jalapeño; two pita rounds torn in halves; cucumber spears; pink-red radish halves; dark olives; lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Thick, creamy, rustic bean mash with whole beans; crisp raw vegetables; soft pita.

## Sauce / component placement

Olive oil pooled on top only.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley, tomato, jalapeño, cumin, olive oil.

## Environmental styling

No other props.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Tan-brown beans, red tomato, green herbs, golden oil, pink radish.

## Forbidden elements

Anything that looks like crumbled or browned ground meat, crumbles, hummus swirl, chickpeas, tahini drizzle, falafel balls, eggs, feta, meat, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl left of center with the vegetable plate and pita right, all within the 4:3 crop; the beans must read as rustic mashed fava, not smooth hummus, at 640x480.
