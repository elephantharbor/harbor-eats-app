## Hero subject

Thick white cod fillets coated in vivid green chermoula, resting on a layered bed of golden potato slices, red pepper strips, and tomato wedges, with green olives and preserved lemon strips.

## Vessel

Shallow terracotta-toned tagine base or wide off-white braiser, served from the pan.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, looking into the pan.

## Required visible components

Two or three cod fillets with green herb coating; visible potato slices; red pepper strips; tomato wedges; green olives; thin preserved-lemon rind strips; a lemon wedge; sliced crusty bread at the edge.

## Texture cues

Flaky cod, tender potato slices, glossy juices.

## Sauce / component placement

Juices pooled around the vegetables, not covering the fish.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped cilantro and parsley, lemon wedges.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a tagine lid leaning in soft focus; the bread at the frame edge.

## Color opportunities

Herb green, red pepper, tomato red, golden potato, olive green, white fish.

## Forbidden elements

Couscous, chickpeas, chicken, a whole fish, fried or crumbed fish, tahini, black olives, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Pan centered and filling about 80 percent of the frame; the green-coated fish must read at 640x480.

Chermoula fish tagine: herb-coated cod braised over potatoes. Not chicken tagine, not couscous.
