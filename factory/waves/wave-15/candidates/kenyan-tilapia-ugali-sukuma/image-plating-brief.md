# Image / Plating Brief — Kenyan Tilapia in Tomato-Onion Sauce with Ugali and Sukuma Wiki

Package `kenyan-tilapia-ugali-sukuma`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A golden tilapia fillet in chunky tomato-onion sauce beside a smooth white dome of ugali and a pile of dark green shredded collard greens.

## Vessel

Wide off-white enamel or stoneware plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

One golden-edged fillet; red tomato sauce with onion slices and green pepper strips; a firm smooth white ugali dome or wedge; thin collard ribbons with bits of tomato; cilantro; one lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Golden fish edge, matte firm ugali, tender glossy greens.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce around the fish, not touching the ugali.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped cilantro and a lemon wedge.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of chopped chiles in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Tomato red, white ugali, dark green collards, golden fish.

## Forbidden elements

Rice, mashed potatoes that could be mistaken for ugali (the ugali must look firm, smooth, and shaped), batter or breadcrumbs, whole fish with head, grits in a bowl, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Three components arranged in a triangle; the ugali shape must read as a firm molded starch at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> kenyan-tilapia-ugali-sukuma` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Kenyan fish plate: tilapia in tomato sauce, molded white ugali, shredded collards. Not rice, not mashed potatoes, not grits.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
