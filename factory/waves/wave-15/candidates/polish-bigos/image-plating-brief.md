# Image / Plating Brief — Polish Bigos: Hunter's Stew of Sauerkraut, Cabbage, Kielbasa, and Pork

Package `polish-bigos`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A deep bowl of thick, brown, glossy hunter's stew of soft sauerkraut and cabbage strands studded with browned pork cubes, kielbasa half-moons, and dark prune pieces, with dark rye bread slices alongside.

## Vessel

Deep earthenware or enamel bowl on a wooden table.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Golden-brown tangled cabbage and sauerkraut; browned pork cubes; kielbasa half-moons with a smoky pink cut face; dark prune halves; bay leaf; two slices of dark rye bread with caraway seeds.

## Texture cues

Silky soft cabbage, tender pork, smoky sausage.

## Sauce / component placement

Thick stew with almost no loose broth.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

A bay leaf and chopped dill optional.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small glass of beer in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Golden-brown cabbage, pink-brown sausage, dark prunes, dark rye bread.

## Forbidden elements

Hot dogs, pierogi, mashed potatoes, sour cream, red borscht, beans, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered; kielbasa and cabbage strands must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> polish-bigos` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Bigos: Polish sauerkraut-and-meat hunter's stew with rye bread. Not choucroute with hot dogs, not soup.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
