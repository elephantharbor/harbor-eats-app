## Hero subject

A pressed Cuban sandwich cut on a sharp diagonal and stacked to show the cross-section: flattened golden crust, layers of thin flat slices of roast pork tenderloin, pink ham, melted Swiss, and green pickle slices.

## Vessel

Wooden board or parchment-lined tray.

## Camera angle

Eye-level to slightly elevated, about 20 degrees, so the cut layers face the camera.

## Required visible components

Two diagonal halves stacked; a shiny pressed crust with flattened grid-free surface; visible distinct layers of thin flat pale roast pork slices (sliced, not shredded or pulled), pink ham, oozing melted Swiss, green pickle slices, and a line of yellow mustard.

## Texture cues

Shatteringly crisp pressed bread, melted cheese pull.

## Sauce / component placement

Mustard thin and visible at the edge only.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

None; a few pickle spears on the board.

## Environmental styling

Only pickles and a small dish of yellow mustard as props; nothing else.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Golden crust, pink ham, pale pork, creamy cheese, green pickles, yellow mustard.

## Forbidden elements

Shredded or pulled pork, plantain chips, chips of any kind, lettuce, tomato, mayonnaise, a sesame-seed bun, panini grill stripes, fries, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Board centered; the cross-section layers must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> cuban-sandwiches` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Cuban sandwich: pressed sliced pork, ham, Swiss, pickle, and mustard. Not a club sandwich, not a panini with grill stripes, not a pulled-pork sandwich.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
