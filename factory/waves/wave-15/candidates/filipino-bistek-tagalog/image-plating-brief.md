## Hero subject

Glossy, dark soy-glazed thin beef slices topped with a heap of soft translucent white onion rings, beside steamed white rice.

## Vessel

Round off-white plate or a shallow enamel plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

Thin seared beef slices with browned edges in a dark glossy sauce; whole soft white onion rings piled on top; white jasmine rice; sliced scallions; a lemon half.

## Texture cues

Tender glossy beef, soft onion rings, fluffy rice.

## Sauce / component placement

Thin dark sauce pooling a little under the beef.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Scallions and a lemon half.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of calamansi halves in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Dark mahogany beef, white onion rings, white rice, green scallion, yellow lemon.

## Forbidden elements

Battered onion rings, broccoli, sesame seeds, peppers, noodles, gravy, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Plate centered; the onion rings must read as soft rings (not fried) at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> filipino-bistek-tagalog` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Bistek tagalog: soy-citrus beef with soft onion rings over rice. Not beef and broccoli, not fried onion rings.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
