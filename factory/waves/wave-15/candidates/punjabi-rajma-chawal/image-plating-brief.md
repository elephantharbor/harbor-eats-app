# Image / Plating Brief — Punjabi Rajma Chawal: Kidney Bean Curry with Basmati Rice

Package `punjabi-rajma-chawal`, revision `author-r2`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r2. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A generous ladle of glossy brick-red kidney bean curry beside a mound of long-grain basmati rice.

## Vessel

Wide shallow off-white stoneware bowl.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so the gravy gloss and individual beans read.

## Required visible components

Whole red kidney beans, clearly bean-shaped, in a thick reddish-brown gravy; separate long basmati grains; chopped cilantro; one lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Plump beans with a few crushed ones thickening the gravy; oil sheen at the gravy edge; fluffy rice.

## Sauce / component placement

Curry on one side of the bowl, rice on the other, touching in the middle. Gravy thick, not watery.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chopped cilantro and a lemon wedge only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small dish of whole cumin seeds in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Deep brick red, white rice, bright green cilantro, yellow lemon.

## Forbidden elements

Chickpeas, lentils, black beans, cream swirl, paneer, naan, coconut, chicken, a whole chile on top, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered, filling about 70 percent of the frame width; beans must be identifiable at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> punjabi-rajma-chawal` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Rajma: whole red kidney beans in onion-tomato gravy. Not chili con carne, not chana masala, not dal.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
