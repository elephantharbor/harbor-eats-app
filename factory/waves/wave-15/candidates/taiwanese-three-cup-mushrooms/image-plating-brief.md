## Hero subject

Thick, deeply browned king oyster mushroom rounds with crosshatched faces, glazed and glossy, tangled with ginger coins, whole golden garlic cloves, and wilted Thai basil.

## Vessel

Matte charcoal or off-white stoneware plate with a small mound of jasmine rice and halved bok choy.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, so the crosshatch and glaze catch light.

## Required visible components

Round mushroom medallions with visible crosshatch scoring; whole garlic cloves; ginger coins; dark green Thai basil leaves; a few dried red chiles; white jasmine rice; two or three bright green bok choy halves.

## Texture cues

Sticky lacquered glaze; meaty seared mushrooms; tender-crisp bok choy.

## Sauce / component placement

Glaze clinging to the mushrooms with only a little in the pan; rice clean.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Thai basil and scallions only.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small clay pot or cast-iron pan in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Mahogany glaze, golden garlic, deep green basil and bok choy, white rice, a few red chiles.
