Two rolls, one sliced to show the spiral with pink bacon and a green pickle strip at the center; dark brown gravy; three halved boiled potatoes with parsley; a small heap of thin cucumber slices with dill.

Tender braised beef, silky gravy, soft potatoes, crisp cucumber.

Parsley and dill.
