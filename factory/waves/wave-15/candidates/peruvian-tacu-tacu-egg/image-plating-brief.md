## Hero subject

A thick, clearly elongated oval rice-and-bean cake with a deep golden crisp crust, topped with a sunny-side-up egg and a tangle of thin pink-purple red onion salsa with cilantro.

## Vessel

Oval off-white plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, so the crust edge and the height of the cake read.

## Required visible components

One golden crusted oval cake about twice as long as it is wide, with rice grains and pale tan canary or pinto beans visible at the edge; a fried egg with runny yolk; a pile of glossy thin red onion slices with lime juice and cilantro; a lime wedge.

## Texture cues

Crisp crust, soft interior, runny yolk, crisp onions.

## Sauce / component placement

Yolk just starting to run; salsa juices glistening.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Cilantro and a lime wedge.

## Environmental styling

Only the plate; no side bowls, dishes, sauces, cups or props of any kind.

Styling props are limited to items in this recipe or neutral tableware (plates, boards, cutlery, linen). No drinks, breads, sides, condiments, or spices that the recipe does not list.

## Color opportunities

Golden-brown crust, pale tan beans, white and yellow egg, purple-pink onion, green cilantro.

Any side bowl or sauce dish, Black beans, dark beans, yellow pepper strips, bell pepper, steak, plantains, burger buns, cheese, tortillas, a round burger patty look, text, logos, hands.
