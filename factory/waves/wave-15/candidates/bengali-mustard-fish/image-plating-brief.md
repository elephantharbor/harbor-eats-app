# Image / Plating Brief — Bengali Shorshe Maach: Haddock in Mustard-Seed Sauce with Rice

## Hero subject

Golden-edged pieces of white fish in a thick, mustard-yellow, slightly grainy sauce speckled with black nigella seeds and slit green chiles, with plain white basmati rice beside them.

## Vessel

Shallow off-white or steel bowl, rice in a mound to one side.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Three or four fish pieces with golden turmeric edges; bright mustard-yellow sauce with fine seed texture; black nigella seeds; two slit green chiles; long basmati grains; a little cilantro.

## Texture cues

Flaky fish, creamy-looking grainy sauce, separate rice.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce coating the fish and pooling a little; not watery.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Slit green chiles and a pinch of cilantro.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of yellow mustard seeds in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Vivid mustard yellow, white fish, black seeds, green chile, white rice.

## Forbidden elements

Coconut milk, orange tomato curry, cream swirl, shrimp, salmon, naan, whole fish with head, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered; nigella specks and the mustard sauce must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> bengali-mustard-fish` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Shorshe maach: white fish in mustard-seed sauce. Not a coconut fish curry, not Dijon cream sauce.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
