# Image / Plating Brief — Classic Cobb Salad with Seared Chicken, Bacon, Egg, and Blue Cheese

Package `cobb-salad`, revision `author-r3`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r3. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

A wide platter of chopped romaine topped with neat parallel rows of sliced golden chicken, crumbled bacon, quartered eggs with yellow yolks, diced avocado, halved cherry tomatoes, and crumbled blue cheese.

## Vessel

Large rectangular or oval white platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated, about 55 degrees, so the rows read as distinct stripes.

## Required visible components

Six distinct rows: sliced chicken, bacon crumbles, egg quarters, avocado, red tomato halves, white-blue cheese crumbles; chive sprinkle; a small jar of vinaigrette beside.

## Texture cues

Crisp lettuce, crisp bacon, creamy avocado and yolk.

## Sauce / component placement

Vinaigrette drizzled lightly; mostly served on the side.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Snipped chives.

## Environmental styling

Optional: salad servers resting on the platter edge.

## Color opportunities

Green romaine, red tomato, yellow yolk, pale green avocado, golden chicken, white-blue cheese.

## Forbidden elements

Croutons, ranch dressing pools, shredded cheddar, tortilla strips, corn, olives, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Platter fills about 85 percent of the frame; the rows must read as separate stripes at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> cobb-salad` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Cobb salad: rows of chicken, bacon, egg, avocado, tomato, and blue cheese. Not a Caesar, not a chopped taco salad.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
