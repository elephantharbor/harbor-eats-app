# Image / Plating Brief — Italian Chicken Cacciatore with Peppers, Mushrooms, and Crusty Bread

## Hero subject

Two browned bone-in chicken thighs nestled in a rustic red tomato sauce full of red and yellow pepper strips, mushrooms, and black olives, with a rosemary sprig.

## Vessel

Shallow wide off-white bowl, with a slice of crusty bread on the rim.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

Two thighs with browned skin above the sauce; chunky tomato sauce; red and yellow pepper strips; quartered mushrooms; black olives; a rosemary sprig; chopped parsley; a slice of crusty bread.

## Texture cues

Tender braised chicken, glossy chunky sauce, soft peppers.

## Sauce / component placement

Sauce pooled around the chicken; the skin stays visible.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and a rosemary sprig.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a glass of red wine in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Tomato red, golden-brown skin, red and yellow peppers, black olives, green herbs.

## Forbidden elements

Pasta, melted cheese, breaded chicken, cream, boneless chunks, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Bowl centered at about 70 percent of frame width; peppers and mushrooms must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> italian-chicken-cacciatore` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Chicken cacciatore: bone-in chicken braised with peppers, mushrooms, and tomato. Not chicken parm, not a pasta dish.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
