## Hero subject

Thick slices of juicy roast turkey breast with golden herb-flecked skin, fanned beside browned roasted potatoes, carrots, and red onion, with a small bowl of glossy ruby cranberry-orange sauce.

## Vessel

Large white oval platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees.

## Required visible components

Four or five turkey slices with moist white meat and golden crisp skin edged with herbs; halved browned potatoes; orange carrot pieces; soft red onion wedges; a bowl of chunky deep-red cranberry sauce with orange zest; a sage sprig.

## Texture cues

Juicy sliced turkey, crisp skin, caramelized vegetables, jammy cranberry.

## Sauce / component placement

Pan juices glossing the slices; the cranberry sauce in its own bowl.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Fresh sage and thyme sprigs.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a carving fork in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Golden skin, white meat, ruby cranberry, orange carrot, browned potato.

## Forbidden elements

Whole turkey with drumsticks, stuffing, gravy boat, jellied canned cranberry log, chicken drumsticks, ham, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Platter fills about 80 percent of the frame; the sliced breast and cranberry must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> herb-roast-turkey-breast-cranberry` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Roast turkey breast with cranberry sauce and vegetables. Not a whole holiday turkey, not ham, not chicken.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
