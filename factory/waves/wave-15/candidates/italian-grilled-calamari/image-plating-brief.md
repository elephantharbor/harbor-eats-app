## Hero subject

Grill-charred squid rings and curled tentacles tossed with halved cherry tomatoes, capers, red onion, parsley, and arugula, with two slices of grill-marked garlic bread.

## Vessel

Large shallow white or speckled ceramic bowl or platter.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees.

## Required visible components

Opaque white squid rings with grill char and crosshatch scoring; curled tentacles; red and yellow cherry tomato halves; capers; thin red onion; parsley leaves; arugula; two slices of charred bread; a lemon wedge.

## Texture cues

Tender lightly charred squid, juicy tomatoes, crisp bread.

Light olive-oil dressing glossing the salad; no sauce.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Parsley and a lemon wedge.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a glass of white wine in soft focus.

White squid with char, red tomato, green arugula and parsley, golden bread.
