## Hero subject

Bone-in chicken pieces in a thick, clinging, deep red-orange tomato masala with oil glistening at the edges, topped with fine ginger matchsticks, cilantro, and slit green chiles, served in the pan.

## Vessel

Black steel karahi (wok-shaped two-handled pan) or dark wok, with a basket of folded naan beside it.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 40 degrees, looking into the pan.

## Required visible components

Browned bone-in thighs and drumsticks; thick tomato masala clinging to them; an oil sheen at the rim; ginger julienne; cilantro; two slit green chiles; lemon wedge; naan with a few charred spots.

## Texture cues

Sticky, clinging sauce; browned chicken skin; soft naan.

## Sauce / component placement

No thin gravy and no cream swirl; the sauce coats the chicken.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Ginger julienne, cilantro, green chiles.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a small bowl of sliced onions in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Deep red-orange masala, golden-brown chicken, green chiles and cilantro, cream-colored naan.

## Forbidden elements

Cream swirl, butter pat, orange creamy gravy (butter chicken), rice, paneer, boneless cubes, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Pan centered and filling most of the frame; must read as bone-in pieces in a dry clinging masala at 640x480.

Chicken karahi: bone-in chicken in a dry tomato-ginger masala in a karahi. Not butter chicken, not tikka masala.
