# Image / Plating Brief — Argentine Grilled Strip Steak with Chimichurri, Grilled Potatoes, and Ensalada Criolla

Package `argentine-chimichurri-steak-potatoes`, revision `author-r3`. Written before the first image was generated. Image: master and card WebPs present, frozen at author-r3. Standard: the meal-image-spec skill and `image-standards/hero-appeal-amendment.md`.

## Hero subject

Sliced grilled strip steak with a dark crust and rosy medium-rare center, generously spooned with vivid green chimichurri, beside grill-marked potato halves and a tomato-onion salad.

## Vessel

Wooden board or large white plate.

## Camera angle

Elevated three-quarter, about 35 degrees, so the pink center and crust read.

## Required visible components

Steak slices fanned to show a rosy pink center and charred crust; loose bright green chimichurri with visible garlic and chile flakes; potato halves with grill marks; tomato wedges with thin white onion and green pepper; a small bowl of extra chimichurri.

## Texture cues

Charred crust, juicy center, oily herb sauce, crisp-edged potatoes.

## Sauce / component placement

Chimichurri spooned across the slices, oil pooling slightly.

## Legitimate garnish (recipe-listed only)

Chimichurri is the garnish.

## Environmental styling

Optional: a glass of red wine in soft focus.

## Color opportunities

Rosy pink, char brown, vivid green, red tomato, golden potato.

## Forbidden elements

French fries, cream sauce, butter pat, peppercorn crust, gochujang, cilantro bunch, raw or blue meat, text, logos, hands.

## Crop strategy

Board fills about 80 percent of the frame; the green sauce and pink center must read at 640x480.

Master is WebP 1200x900. Card is WebP 640x480. Both are 4:3 center crops of the GenerateImage source. Export with `python3 /workspace/flavorweave-catalog-factory/export_meal_webps.py <source> <this dir> argentine-chimichurri-steak-potatoes` (finalize_images.py does this).

## Ambiguity

Argentine steak with chimichurri. Not steak au poivre, not a pesto steak, not fajitas.

## House style

Bright, premium, realistic editorial food photography. Soft daylight, light neutral surface, food filling the frame. The house-quality reference is Chipotle Lime Black Bean Bowl for light and finish only, not for composition. No text, logo, or watermark.
